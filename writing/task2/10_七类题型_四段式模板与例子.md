# 10｜IELTS Writing Task 2 七类题型：四段式模板 + 套用示例

[返回 Task 2 目录](README.md) · [三大母模板](03_三大母模板_Effects_Facts_TwoPart.md) · [七类题型选型](02_七类题型与模板选型.md) · [完整范文目录](README.md#完整范文) · [返回仓库首页](../../README.md)

> **训练目标**：Band 6.5–7.0，建议 250–290 词，40 分钟。本文的英文模板是帮助组织论证的**训练支架**，不是雅思官方规定的固定句式；占位符必须替换。  
> **例子来源**：第 2 节的“接受现状/改善现状”题是 **Cambridge IELTS 14 Academic Test 1 Writing Task 2 真题**；其余例子基于本仓库已有教学练习题，**不声明为剑雅原题**。每类都配一组四段式短例子和一篇完整范文链接。

## 0｜先选择题型，再选择底层逻辑

| 题型 | 题目识别词 | 主体段任务 | 需表达个人立场？ | 典型陷阱 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Opinion / Agree–Disagree | To what extent do you agree or disagree? | 两个理由支持你的立场；可部分让步 | **必须** | 只说 X 有好处，没回应 best / most important |
| 2. Discuss Both Views + Opinion | Discuss both views and give your own opinion | **一段真正解释 A，一段解释 B** | **必须** | 两段都在支持 B，却没解释 A 为什么合理 |
| 3. Advantages & Disadvantages | What are the advantages and disadvantages? | 一段优点、一段缺点 | **不一定**，看具体问法 | 无要求却强行比较哪边更多 |
| 4. Outweigh | Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? | 利与弊都解释，且比较轻重 | **必须** | 只列两边，不给比较标准 |
| 5. Positive / Negative Development | Is this a positive or negative development? | 对总体变化给出评估与两条逻辑链 | **必须** | 只描述现象，没判断总体好坏 |
| 6. Causes / Problems / Solutions | What are the causes/problems? What can be done? | **按题目问法**写原因/问题与对应措施 | 通常需要明确主要判断/方案 | 题目问问题却写原因，或措施与原因脱节 |
| 7. Two-Part Questions | Why...? What effects...? / 两个具体问句 | **一问一段**，缺一不可 | 根据问题，未必是 agree/disagree | 第二问没回答或写偏范围 |

**底层逻辑与原有笔记兼容：** Effects → 行为/趋势及其结果；Facts → 命题成立与否（尤其 only / best / most important）；Two-Part → 按两个问题组织答案。详见 [三大母模板](03_三大母模板_Effects_Facts_TwoPart.md) 与 [四个可选模块](04_四个可选模块.md)。

### 每个 Body 的统一展开方法

**Point（中心论点）→ Explanation（为什么/怎样）→ Example（具体例子）→ Result（结果）→ Link（回到题目）**

如果例子已经足够具体，Explanation / Result 可合并；不要机械把五项全部写成五个空洞的句子。

## 1｜Opinion / Agree–Disagree 同意反对题

**题型信号：** To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**四段模板**

- **Introduction**：It is often argued that [statement]. I largely agree/disagree with this view because [main reason 1] and [main reason 2].
- **Body 1**：One important reason why I agree/disagree is that [point 1]. This is because [why/how]. For example, [example]. Therefore, [link to the original claim].
- **Body 2**：Another reason is that [point 2]. When/If [condition], [mechanism]. As a result, [effect]. This suggests that [verdict].
- **Conclusion**：In conclusion, I largely agree/disagree that [statement] because [two main reasons in brief].

**套用示例：科学最重要的目标**

题目：The most important aim of science should be to improve people's lives. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

- **Intro**：I largely agree that improving people's lives should be a central aim of science, although basic research also deserves support.
- **Body 1**：Medical research develops treatments and vaccines, directly protecting public health. This makes improving lives an especially important priority.
- **Body 2**：However, basic science can produce discoveries whose applications are not immediately clear. Supporting it helps societies solve future problems as well.
- **Conclusion**：Human well-being should be a leading goal of science, but research without immediate practical value should not be neglected.

**提醒：** 题目含 **the most important**，必须解释“为何应优先”，不是只说医学研究有用。部分同意时 Body 2 可作为有限让步，但总体立场要一致。

## 2｜Discuss Both Views + Opinion 双边讨论题

**题型信号：** Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

**四段模板（本次练习重点）**

- **Introduction**：Some people believe that [View A], while others argue that [View B]. Although [limited merit of A], I believe that [preferred view] is generally a better approach because [main reason].
- **Body 1**：On the one hand, one major reason why some people support [View A] is that [reason A]. This is because [mechanism]. For example, [specific example]. As a result, [outcome A]. **必须让 A 自己站得住脚。**
- **Body 2**：On the other hand, I believe that [View B] is more beneficial because [reason B]. For instance, [specific example]. This can lead to [positive result B]. Therefore, [why B is preferable].
- **Conclusion**：In conclusion, while [View A] may be useful in [condition], I believe that [View B] is a better [short-/long-term] approach because [decisive reason].

**套用示例：接受不理想的工作，还是改善现状？（剑雅14 Academic Test 1 真题）**

- **Intro**：While accepting a difficult situation can provide temporary stability, I believe taking practical steps to improve it is generally better.
- **Body 1 — View A（接受）**：A person may dislike their job but still need the salary to pay rent. Quitting without another offer can increase financial pressure. Keeping the job temporarily therefore gives them time to plan.
- **Body 2 — View B（改善）**：They could improve their skills and apply for better positions while remaining employed. These actions increase their chances of higher earnings and more satisfying work.
- **Conclusion**：Acceptance can be sensible in the short term, but it should be a starting point rather than a permanent solution.

**错误示范（来自本次真实练习）：** Body 1 写“改善的好处”，Body 2 写“接受的坏处”，实际上两段都支持改善。**讨论对方**不等于**批评对方**。建议先在草稿上写明 A 为什么合理、B 为什么合理，再决定支持哪边。

## 3｜Advantages & Disadvantages 利弊列举题

**题型信号：** What are the advantages and disadvantages of ...?

**四段模板**

- **Introduction**：[Trend] has become increasingly common. It offers [main advantage], but it can also cause [main disadvantage].
- **Body 1**：One major advantage of [trend] is that [benefit]. This is particularly useful when [context]. For example, [example]. As a result, [positive effect].
- **Body 2**：A significant disadvantage, however, is that [drawback]. When [condition], [negative mechanism]. Consequently, [negative result].
- **Conclusion**：In conclusion, [trend] offers [advantage], but it also creates [disadvantage]. [Optional: practical boundary/response].

**套用示例：远程办公**

题目：More people are working from home rather than travelling to an office. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend?

- **Intro**：Remote work can give employees more flexibility, although it may reduce face-to-face communication.
- **Body 1**：Avoiding a daily commute saves time and money. Workers may use this time to rest or care for their families, improving work-life balance.
- **Body 2**：Online messages are less effective for some urgent discussions. New employees may also find it harder to learn from experienced colleagues.
- **Conclusion**：Remote work brings clear personal benefits, but firms need to manage communication difficulties.

**提醒：** 题目没有要求 outweigh，就不必在结尾硬判“利大于弊”。

## 4｜Do the Advantages Outweigh the Disadvantages? 利弊比较题

**题型信号：** Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

**四段模板（推荐把更支持的一边放 Body 2 也可以，保持整篇一致）**

- **Introduction**：Although [trend] may cause [main drawback], I believe its advantages outweigh the disadvantages because [decisive benefit].
- **Body 1**：Admittedly, [trend] can create [disadvantage]. This is because [mechanism]. For example, [example]. However, [explain why this cost is limited/possible to reduce].
- **Body 2**：More importantly, [trend] can [major advantage]. When [condition], [how it works]. As a result, [broader or longer-term benefit]. This benefit is more significant because [**explicit comparison criterion**].
- **Conclusion**：In conclusion, despite [drawback], I believe the advantages outweigh the disadvantages because [comparison].

**套用示例：网购**

题目：More people are choosing to shop online rather than in physical stores. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?

- **Intro**：Although online shopping can put pressure on local stores, I believe its benefits are greater overall.
- **Body 1 — drawback**：Some small shops may lose customers and struggle to compete. Yet many can adapt by selling through online channels.
- **Body 2 — decisive benefit**：Customers can compare prices and buy products without travelling. These repeated savings of time and money benefit a wide range of consumers.
- **Conclusion**：The wider and more frequent convenience for consumers outweighs the challenges faced by some retailers.

**比较标准：** 影响人数、影响程度、持续时间、是否可缓解。**两边都谈 ≠ 已经完成 outweigh 比较**。

## 5｜Positive or Negative Development 正负发展题

**题型信号：** Is this a positive or negative development?

**四段模板（以总体负面为例）**

- **Introduction**：[Trend] has become more common in recent years. Although it may bring [limited benefit], I consider it a largely negative development because [reason 1] and [reason 2].
- **Body 1**：One important problem with [trend] is [negative effect 1]. When [cause/condition], [mechanism]. As a result, [outcome 1].
- **Body 2**：Another negative consequence is [effect 2]. For example, [example]. This can [result 2]. Although [possible benefit], [why this does not overturn overall judgment].
- **Conclusion**：In conclusion, despite [limited benefit], [trend] is largely negative because [two main effects].

**套用示例：农村人口减少**

题目：In many countries, people are moving from rural areas to cities, so the population in the countryside is decreasing. Is this a positive or negative development?

- **Intro**：Although urban migration may improve individual incomes, rural depopulation is largely a negative trend.
- **Body 1**：As young workers leave villages, local shops and transport services may become harder to maintain. This reduces convenience for residents who stay.
- **Body 2**：Cities may also experience higher demand for housing and transport. If services cannot expand quickly, rents and congestion can rise.
- **Conclusion**：The combined pressure on villages and cities makes the overall trend negative.

**提醒：** 可以写“两条负面影响”，也可以“一正一负并明确比较”；重点是始终在**评价这个 development**。

## 6｜Causes / Problems / Solutions 原因、问题、解决方案题

**题型信号：** What are the causes of ...? What can be done to solve it? 也可能问 What problems does this cause and how can they be solved?

**四段模板（以下是 Causes + Solutions 版本）**

- **Introduction**：[Problem] is a growing concern in [context]. It is mainly caused by [major cause], and [main solution] could help address it.
- **Body 1 — Cause**：One major cause of [problem] is [cause]. This is because [background]. When [mechanism], [problem becomes worse]. For example, [example].
- **Body 2 — Solution**：One effective way to address this issue is to [solution]. By [specific action], [target group] can [behaviour change]. As a result, [how problem is reduced]. Therefore, [link].
- **Conclusion**：In conclusion, [problem] is largely caused by [cause], and [solution] is a practical response.

**套用示例：城市交通拥堵**

题目：Traffic congestion is a serious problem in many cities. What are the main causes, and what measures can be taken to address it?

- **Intro**：Heavy dependence on private cars is a major cause of congestion, while better public transport can help reduce it.
- **Body 1 — Cause**：When buses and trains are slow or unreliable, commuters prefer driving. Too many cars then use the same roads during rush hour.
- **Body 2 — Solution**：More frequent and affordable buses and metro services can encourage people to leave their cars at home, reducing traffic volume.
- **Conclusion**：Making public transport a realistic alternative addresses an important cause of urban congestion.

**重要变体：** 如果题目问 **problems + solutions**，Body 1 就应该说明造成的**问题/后果**，不能机械写 causes。如果问 **causes + effects**，Body 2 就写 effects。**先看确切问句，再分配段落。**

## 7｜Two-Part Questions 双问题题

**题型信号：** 同一题干后给出两个独立问题，例如 Why do they do this? What effects can it have on their studies?

**四段模板**

- **Introduction**：[Paraphrase of issue]. This is mainly due to [short answer Q1], and it can [short answer Q2].
- **Body 1 — Q1**：One major reason why [issue] occurs is [reason]. This is because [mechanism]. For example, [example]. Therefore, [direct answer to Q1].
- **Body 2 — Q2**：Regarding [Q2], [main answer]. When/By [condition/action], [outcome]. However, [relevant qualification if needed]. Therefore, [direct answer to Q2].
- **Conclusion**：In conclusion, [brief answer to Q1], while [brief answer to Q2].

**套用示例：Gap Year**

题目：Some students take a year off between finishing school and starting university to work or travel. Why do they do this? What effects can this have on their studies?

- **Intro**：School leavers often take a gap year to earn money or gain experience, and the break can affect their future studies.
- **Body 1 — Why**：Some need to save for university expenses. Others want time to explore careers before choosing a degree.
- **Body 2 — Effects on studies**：Work experience may improve motivation and time management, but a long break can also make returning to academic routines harder.
- **Conclusion**：A gap year has practical motivations, and its academic value depends on how the time is used.

**提醒：** 题目问的范围是 **their studies**，不要把旅行带来的所有生活好处都当成对学习的影响。

## 8｜考场可背的极简功能框架

| 段落 | 核心功能 | 英文提示 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | 改写题目 + 立场/任务概览 | Some people believe ... while others ... / Although ..., I believe ... |
| Body 1 | 第一方观点或第一个问题，独立逻辑链 | One major reason/benefit/problem ... / This is because ... |
| Body 2 | 第二方观点或第二个问题，独立逻辑链 | On the other hand ... / Another important point ... / By doing ..., ... |
| Conclusion | 不添新观点，直接回答题目 | In conclusion, while ..., I believe ... / Overall, ... |

**注意：** On the one hand / On the other hand 适合真正形成对照的观点，不应为了模板在两个互补原因之间强加对立。

### 40 分钟训练流程

1. **0–5 分钟：** 圈出题型问法、关键词、限制范围（Scope）以及要求作出的比较。
2. **5–8 分钟：** 写两个 Body 的中心论点，各列 Point → Why/How → Example → Result。
3. **8–33 分钟：** 按四段写 250 词以上；Body 各约 80–110 词。
4. **33–40 分钟：** 检查是否漏答、立场一致、主谓一致、V-ing、冠词、单复数及不完整句。

### 下次自检：先结构，再语言

- [ ] 四段是否分别完成应有的**任务**？
- [ ] 双边讨论是否公平解释了双方理由，而不是只支持一边？
- [ ] Outweigh 是否明确比较了影响范围/强度/时间？
- [ ] Two-Part / Causes-Solutions 是否逐一回答两个问句？
- [ ] 每个 Body 的例子是否能直接证明中心论点？
- [ ] 是否消除了模糊、重复、无关的套话？
- [ ] 基础语法是否准确（make an effort、with accepting、no one makes 等）？
