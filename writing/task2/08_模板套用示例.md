4. 套用模板示例：从题目到 80–100 词主体段  
练习时不要先看成段。先只写“判断 → 观点 → P/C/A/E/F/Link”，再用对应模板扩成句子。  
### 示例 1｜Science｜Facts：most important
题目：The most important aim of science should be to improve people's lives. To what extent do you agree or   
disagree?  
判断：Facts → SFVS。Statement 的核心限定是 the most important aim；不能只证明“science improves life”，还要证  
明为什么它应当优先。  
立场：Mostly agree.  
选用观点：Health：科学研究 → 疾病治疗 → 更长、更健康的生活。  
套槽位：P science improves health → C serious diseases remain difficult to treat → A research develops   
medicines/vaccines/technology → E better treatment + longer lives → Link public health deserves priority  
成段（约 80–100 词）：  
One major reason is that scientific research can directly improve public health. Many people still suffer from   
serious diseases that cannot be fully treated with current medical methods. Through continued research,  
scientists can develop new medicines, vaccines and medical technologies that prevent or treat these conditions   
more effectively. As a result, patients can receive better care and enjoy longer, healthier lives. Therefore, when   
scientific resources are limited, research that directly protects human health should remain one of the highest   
priorities.  
为什么成立：PCAE 展开完整，最后一句主动回到“priority”，因此不是只写 science 的好处，而是在证明 most important   
的优先级。  
### 示例 2｜University Education｜Facts：the best way
题目：Some people believe that university education is the best way to get a good job. To what extent do you agree or   
disagree?  
判断：Facts → SFVS。限定词 the best way；最有效的反驳不是“大学不好”，而是证明其他路径在某些工作上同样或更有  
效。  
立场：Mostly disagree.  
选用观点：Practical experience：很多岗位需要真实工作能力，实习/工作可比纯理论学习更直接。  
套槽位：Concession university provides systematic knowledge → C employers need real workplace skills → A   
internships/work experience → F job-specific competence + employer readiness → Link university is useful but not   
always the best route  
成段（约 80–100 词）：  

Although university education can provide systematic knowledge, this does not mean that it is always the best   
route to a good job. In many industries, practical experience is equally or even more important because   
employers need people who can deal with real workplace tasks. By completing internships or gaining relevant   
work experience, applicants can develop job-specific skills and become familiar with professional expectations.   
Therefore, securing a good job depends on several factors, and practical experience may sometimes be more   
valuable than a university degree alone.  
为什么成立：直接攻击 the best，而不是证明 university education 有缺点；用了替代事实 practical experience 证伪强命  
题。  
### 示例 3｜Technology｜Effects：Discuss both views
题目：Some people think technology mostly benefits our lives, while others believe it brings more disadvantages.   
Discuss both views and give your own opinion.  
判断：Effects → ATES，而且 Discuss both views 必须双边。可分别选效率（+）和健康/就业（-），最后比较影响强度。  
立场：Benefits are greater overall when technology is used appropriately.  
选用观点：正面 Body：Efficiency。  
套槽位：P technology improves efficiency → C repetitive/time-consuming tasks → A software/automation → E save   
time + focus on complex tasks → Link broad productivity benefit  
成段（约 80–100 词）：  
A major advantage of technology is that it can significantly improve efficiency. Many employees and consumers   
spend time on repetitive or routine tasks that do not require complex judgement. By using digital tools and   
automated systems, these tasks can be completed more quickly and with less manual effort. As a result, people   
can save time and concentrate on work, study or personal activities that are more valuable to them. Since this   
benefit affects both workplaces and everyday life, it is one of the strongest reasons why technology has   
improved modern living.  
为什么成立：这是标准 Positive Effect PCAE；末句把影响范围扩大到 workplace + everyday life，为后面的“benefits   
greater”比较做准备。  
### 示例 4｜Environment｜Two-Part：Causes + Solutions
题目：In many cities, traffic congestion is becoming worse. Why is this happening, and what can be done to solve the   
problem?  
判断：Two-Part → ICESS。正文一段 Causes，一段 Solutions；不要把 effects 写成主线。  
立场：原因：private-car dependence + poor urban planning；解决：reliable public transport + better planning.  
选用观点：Solution Body：public transport。  

套槽位：Barrier many commuters rely on cars → actor government → A improve frequency/coverage/affordability → E   
commuters switch modes → final fewer cars + less congestion  
成段（约 80–100 词）：  
A practical way to reduce traffic congestion is for city governments to improve public transport. Many   
commuters rely on private cars because buses or trains may be slow, crowded or inconvenient. By increasing   
service frequency, extending routes and keeping fares affordable, authorities can make public transport a more   
realistic alternative. This would encourage more people to leave their cars at home, particularly for daily   
commuting. As a result, the number of vehicles on major roads would fall, which could significantly reduce   
congestion during peak hours.  
为什么成立：先明确现有 barrier，再由 actor 执行 Action，最后给出行为改变和最终效果，符合 Solution 段的“措施为什么  
有效”。  
### 示例 5｜Gap Year｜Effects：Advantages outweigh disadvantages
题目：School leavers go travelling or work for a period before they go directly to university. Are there more advantages   
or disadvantages on their study?  
判断：Effects → ATES。Scope 是 on their study；所有结果必须回到 future study。比较题需要承认至少一个 downside。  
立场：Advantages outweigh disadvantages if the period is purposeful.  
选用观点：Academic understanding：缺实践经验 → 工作/旅行看到真实应用 → 更理解专业 → 更有学习动力。  
套槽位：P better understanding of future field → C limited real-world experience → A work/travel exposes real   
applications → E clearer purpose + motivation → Link better future study  
成段（约 80–100 词）：  
One important academic benefit of working or travelling before university is that students can develop a clearer   
understanding of their future field. Many school leavers have limited practical experience and may not know   
how a chosen subject is used outside the classroom. Through an internship, temporary job or purposeful travel,  
they can observe real workplaces and social needs. As a result, the value of their future studies becomes more   
concrete, which can increase their motivation when they enter university. This directly supports their later   
academic development.  
为什么成立：这是原笔记 CAE 的典型逻辑：Condition 是 Action 与 Desired Effect 的桥；最后严格回到 their study。
