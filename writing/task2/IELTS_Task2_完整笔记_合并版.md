## 1｜00_考场SOP_审题结构与40分钟流程

> Task 2 最低 250 词；时间安排仅为训练建议，不是官方强制步骤。

## 40 分钟建议流程

| 分钟 | 操作 | 结果 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | 辨认题型、Effects / Facts / Two-Part、Scope | 立场和两个论点 |
| 5–8 | 列出两条 P → C → A → E/F → Link | Body 段逻辑链 |
| 8–33 | Introduction → Body 1 → Body 2 → Conclusion | 完整四段作文 |
| 33–40 | 检查答题覆盖、连贯、词汇和语法 | 修正错误 |

## 段落分工

| Introduction | Body 1 | Body 2 | Conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 改写题目 + 立场 | 原因/事实/影响 + 解释 + 回扣 | 第二条独立逻辑链 | 重述判断与决定性依据 |

## 原资料速查卡

5. 考场与训练速查卡  
审题 Question form → Effects/Facts/Two-Part → Scope → 立场  
Effects ATES 构思；Body 优先 P → C → A → E → Link  
Facts SFVS 构思；Body 优先 P/Fact → C → A/behaviour → Fact   
confirmed → Link；看到 only/best/most/necessary 主动找反  
例或替代因素  
Two-Part ICESS；题目问什么就答什么，未被问到的 effects 不要喧宾夺  
主  
观点选择 2 个最容易解释的观点 > 4 个浅观点；能写清 why/how 至少两  
步才值得用  
主体段 建议 5–6 句：Point 1 句 + Explanation 2–3 句 + Example 0–1  
句 + Link 0–1 句  
语言 准确搭配 > 生僻词；because / although / if / which / when /   
by doing 足够构成可控复杂句  
最后检查 漏问？立场一致？每段一个中心？有没有回 Scope？主谓/单复  
数/拼写是否有明显错误？  
建议的练习方式  
Step 1｜每天选 2 个观点，只写中文/英文关键词链：P → C → A → E/F → Link，控制在 2 分钟内。  
Step 2｜把 5 个点各写成一句，不查范文；写完再对照 Topic 观点库和模板。  
Step 3｜扩成 80–100 词主体段。优先修正“逻辑断点 + 重复性语法错误”，不要先追求高级词。  
Step 4｜随机真题：5 分钟完成题型判断、立场、两个观点、两条逻辑链，再进入限时整篇。

## 2｜01_核心方法_Effects_Facts_TwoPart_PCAE_CAF

> 从 Word 原资料恢复，保留公式及 Scope 判断。

## 记忆卡

- Effects：Action → Target → Effects → Scope；P → C → A → E → Link。
- Facts：Statement → Facts → Verdict → Scope；P → C → A → F → Link。
- Two-Part：Issue → Causes → Effects → Solutions → Scope。
- Scope：每个论点必须回应题目限定的对象、方面和判断强度。

1. 如何判断题目类型  
先看“题目表面问法”，再判断“真正要证明的核心逻辑”。同样是 Agree / Disagree，既可能是 Effects，也可能是 Facts；  
题型名称本身不能替代逻辑判断。  
步骤 你问自己什么 识别结果 工具  
1 题目是在要求判断、比较，还是  
回答   
Why/How/Problems/Solutions  
？  
判断 / 比较 / Two-Part Question form  
2 我要证明“一个行为会带来什么  
结果”，还是“一个命题是否成  
立”？  
Effects / Facts ATES / SFVS  
3 题目限定影响谁、哪一方面、比  
较到什么程度？有没有 only /   
best / most / necessary /   
some？  
Scope + 命题强度 Scope  
### 1.1 Effects：行为 / 趋势 / 政策 → 好坏结果
典型信号：advantages / disadvantages、positive or negative、benefits / harms、某行为对某群体有什么影响。使用   
ATES：Action → Target → Effects → Scope。主体段优先 CAE：Condition → Action → Effect。  
快速判断句：“如果 X 发生，会带来什么结果？”能自然回答题目，通常就是 Effects。  
### 1.2 Facts：命题是否成立 → 用普遍事实证实 / 证伪
典型信号：题目提出一个 Statement，尤其包含 only / always / the best / the most important / necessary / essential /   
some / mostly。使用 SFVS：Statement → Facts → Verdict → Scope。主体段优先 CAF，并主动找反例。  
快速判断句：“我是在证明这句话对不对，而不是单纯预测 X 的结果吗？”如果是，优先按 Facts。  
强限定词：only / always / all = 一个有效反例就能削弱；necessary / essential = 问有没有“不具备它也能成功”的情况；the  
best / most important = 必须比较优先级，而不是只证明它有价值。  
### 1.3 Two-Part：社会现象 → 原因 / 影响 / 解决方案
题目直接问 Why...? What problems/effects...? What can be done...? 时，用 ICESS：Issue → Causes → Effects → Solutions  
→ Scope。怎么问就怎么答，不要让未被提问的部分占据主体。  
### 1.4 最后一定检查 Scope
Scope 是最容易导致“有内容但偏题”的地方。每个 Body 写完都问：这个结果是否直接回应题目限定对象、限定方面和比较  
强度？例如题目问 gap year 对 their study 的影响，就必须把观点拉回 academic motivation / major understanding / study  
routine，而不是只写赚钱或旅游体验。

## 3｜02_七类题型与模板选型

> 题目表面问法不等于底层逻辑；最终仍应检查 Effects / Facts / Two-Part。

| 类型 | 解题要求 |
| --- | --- |
| Opinion / Agree–Disagree | 提出立场并证明；出现 only、best、most important 时优先验证命题并考虑反例。 |
| Discuss Both Views + Opinion | 两方分别解释理由，再给明确自己的判断。 |
| Advantages & Disadvantages | 说明至少一项重要优势与劣势；除非题目要求，否则不必作 outweigh 判断。 |
| Advantages Outweigh Disadvantages | 完整比较双方影响，明确决定性理由。 |
| Positive / Negative Development | 评估变化带来的总体结果，不只给单一例子。 |
| Causes & Solutions / Problems | 回答问题明确要求的原因、问题及解决办法。 |
| Two-Part Questions | 逐一回应两个子问题，主体段的安排服从题目要求。 |

## 4｜03_三大母模板_Effects_Facts_TwoPart

> [X] / [Action] / [Effect] 等方括号占位符必须替换；记忆的是句子功能。

### 6.2 母模板 A｜Effects：判断行为 / 趋势 / 政策带来的结果
适用：positive/negative development、advantages/disadvantages、outweigh，以及本质上比较两种做法效果的 discuss  
both views。核心：ATES；Body 用 P → C → A → E → LB。  
Effects｜Introduction  
[X] has become increasingly common in many societies. Although it can [opposite-side effect], I believe it is mainly   
[positive/negative] because it can [main effect 1] and [main effect 2].  
如果完全单边，可删除 Although 句，直接写立场 + 两个主要 Effects。  
Effects｜Body  
One major [benefit/problem] of [X] is that it can [P].  
[C: current problem / limitation / requirement].  
By / When [A: how X changes the situation], [target] can [E1].As a result, [E2].  
This may further lead to [E3, optional].  
Therefore, [LB: return to the question].  

Effects｜Conclusion  
In conclusion, although [opposite-side effect], I believe [X] is [positive/negative] overall because [main effect 1] and [main   
effect 2].  
### 例子｜Rural people are moving to cities, so the countryside population is decreasing. Positive
or negative development?  
位置 示例内容  
立场 Mostly negative  
P Rural depopulation can weaken the local economy.  
C Rural communities depend on enough working-age people to   
support agriculture, local businesses and services.  
A Many young adults move to cities, so rural areas lose workers   
and consumers.  
E Farms and small businesses struggle; job opportunities fall;  
more young people leave.  
LB Rural depopulation can create a vicious cycle of population loss  
and economic decline.  
示例 Body：  
One major negative effect of rural depopulation is that it can weaken the local economy. Rural communities   
depend on a sufficient number of working-age people to support agriculture, local businesses and basic services.   
When many young adults move to cities, rural areas may lose both workers and consumers. As a result, farms and   
small businesses may struggle to operate, while local services may become less sustainable. This can lead to fewer   
job opportunities, which may encourage even more young people to leave the countryside. Therefore, rural   
depopulation can create a vicious cycle of population loss and economic decline.  
### 6.3 母模板 B｜Facts：判断一个命题是否成立
适用：agree/disagree、to what extent，以及带 only / best / most important / always / necessary 等强限定词的命题。核  
心：SFVS；Body 用 P → C → A → F → LB。  

Facts｜Introduction  
It is often argued that [statement]. I largely agree/disagree with this view because [reason 1] and [reason 2], although   
[optional concession].  
Facts｜Body  
One important reason why [statement] is valid / questionable is that [P / fact].  
[C: relevant situation / requirement].  
By / When [A], [F1].  
Consequently, [F2].  
Therefore, [LB: explicitly support or refute the statement].  
Facts｜Conclusion  
In conclusion, although [concession], I largely agree/disagree that [statement] because [main reason summary].  
### 例子｜The most important aim of science should be to improve people's lives. To what extent
do you agree?  
位置 示例内容  
命题 Improving people's lives should be the most important aim of   
science.  
Verdict Largely agree  
P / Fact Scientific research can directly protect human health.  
C Serious diseases still affect people and some cannot be fully   
treated.  
A Research develops medicines, vaccines and medical   
technologies.  
F Patients receive better treatment and can live longer, healthier   
lives.  
LB Because protecting human life creates a direct and widespread   
benefit, this aim deserves very high priority.  

示例 Body：  
One important reason why improving people's lives should be a central aim of science is that scientific research can  
directly protect human health. Many people still suffer from serious illnesses that cannot be fully treated with   
current medical methods. Through scientific research, new medicines, vaccines and medical technologies can be   
developed. As a result, more patients can receive effective treatment and enjoy longer and healthier lives. Since   
protecting human life provides such a direct and widespread benefit to society, improving people's lives deserves   
to remain one of the highest priorities of scientific research.  
### 6.4 母模板 C｜Two-Part：原因 / 问题 / 影响 / 解决方案
适用：Why...? What problems/effects...? What can be done...? 以及两个直接问题。核心：ICESS。原则：题目问什么就写什  
么，一问一个 Body；不要额外展开未被提问的部分。  
Two-Part｜Introduction  
[Issue] has become increasingly common in many societies. This essay will examine [short answer to Q1] and discuss /   
argue [short answer to Q2].  
Cause Body  
One major reason for [issue] is [P / cause].  
[C / background].  
Because / When [mechanism], [E1].  
As a result, [E2 / issue becomes more likely].  
Therefore, [cause] is an important factor behind [issue].  
Solution Body  
One effective way to address [problem] is to [P / solution].  
Currently, [C / problem].  
By [A / concrete measure], [target] can [E1].  
As a result, [E2].  
Therefore, [solution] could help reduce [problem].  

### 例子｜Traffic congestion is a serious problem in many cities. What are the causes and what 
can be done?  
位置 示例内容  
Body 1 Cause People rely heavily on private cars because public transport   
may be inconvenient or unreliable.  
机制 More commuters drive during peak hours → too many vehicles   
on limited road space → congestion.  
Body 2 Solution Governments should improve public transport.  
机制 More frequent and reliable buses/metros → more people leave   
cars at home → fewer vehicles → smoother traffic.  
LB Better public transport can reduce dependence on private cars   
and ease urban congestion.  
示例 Solution Body：  
One effective way to reduce traffic congestion is to improve public transport. In many cities, commuters rely on   
private cars because buses or metro services are not convenient enough. By increasing service frequency,  
expanding routes and keeping fares affordable, governments can make public transport a more practical choice. As   
a result, more people may leave their cars at home, reducing the number of vehicles on the road during peak hours.  
Therefore, investment in reliable public transport can help ease urban traffic congestion.

## 5｜04_四个可选模块

> 让步 + 反驳、比较 / Outweigh、Discuss Both Views、Strong Words。

### 6.5 四个可选模块：按题目插入，不单独背一套作文
### 模块 1｜Concession + Rebuttal：让步后拉回主立场
适用：mostly agree/disagree、mostly positive/negative、outweigh。功能：承认对方合理之处，但说明其范围有限、可控  
制或重要性较低。  
模板  
Admittedly, [opposite view] has some merit because [reason].  
However, this does not necessarily mean that [opposite conclusion].  
When [key condition], [your side] becomes more important because [reason].  
Therefore, although [opposite side] should not be ignored, I still believe that [main position].  

### 例子｜农村人口迁往城市
Admittedly, moving to cities can provide rural workers with better jobs and higher incomes. However, these   
individual benefits do not remove the long-term problems caused by a continuing decline in the rural population. If   
villages lose large numbers of working-age residents, local businesses and services may become increasingly   
difficult to maintain. Therefore, although urban migration can benefit individuals, I still consider the overall   
development largely negative.  
### 模块 2｜Comparison / Outweigh：明确比较哪一边更重要
适用：Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? 关键不是分别列好坏，而是解释为什么一边更重要。  
模板  
Although [disadvantage] can cause [negative result], I believe the advantages are more significant because [major   
benefit].  
This benefit is particularly important because [comparison criterion / broader or longer-term impact].  
Overall, [advantage] has a greater impact than [disadvantage].  
### 例子｜Working from home
Although working from home may reduce face-to-face interaction with colleagues, I believe its advantages are   
more significant because it can save commuting time and improve employees' work-life balance. This benefit   
affects workers every day and can give them more time for rest and family responsibilities. Overall, the   
improvement in daily quality of life can outweigh the loss of some in-person communication.  
### 模块 3｜Discuss Both Views：两边都写完整逻辑链
适用：Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. 先判断两个 View 本质上是在比较 Effects 还是验证 Facts，再  
选择母模板 A 或 B；结构上必须两边都展开。  
Introduction + 双 Body 结构  
Some people believe that [View A], while others argue that [View B]. Although [brief merit of weaker view], I believe   
[preferred view] because [main reason].  
Body 1: View A → P → C → A → E/F → LB  
Body 2: View B → P → C → A → E/F → LB  

Conclusion: while [weaker-view merit], I believe [preferred view] because [decisive reason].  
### 例子｜大学生学习额外科目 vs 专注主专业
View A: studying additional subjects → broader, more up-to-date skills → better employability.  
View B: focusing on the main subject → deeper specialist knowledge + qualifications → stronger professional   
foundation.  
Opinion: prefer View A, while acknowledging the value of specialist depth.  
### 模块 4｜Strong Words：only / best / most important / always
适用：强限定词 Facts 题。必须判断“是否配得上唯一 / 最佳 / 最重要”，不能只证明 X 有好处。  
反驳强限定词模板  
Although [X] can certainly [benefit], this does not mean that it is [the only / the best / the most important] way to [goal].  
In many cases, [alternative factor] is equally or even more important because [C].  
By [A], people can [F/E].  
Therefore, [goal] depends on several factors rather than [X] alone.  
### 例子｜University is the best way to get a good job
Although university education can provide specialist knowledge and recognised qualifications, this does not mean   
that it is always the best route to employment. In fields such as skilled trades or some technical occupations,  
vocational training can provide direct practical experience that employers value. Therefore, successful employment  
can depend on different forms of education rather than university study alone.

## 6｜05_功能句型库_Introduction_Body_Conclusion

> 原资料：Introduction、Body、Concession / Rebuttal、Cause / Solution、Conclusion。

3. 分类模板库：按功能套，不按题目机械背  
模板中的 [X] / [Target] / [Condition] / [Action] / [Effect] 是必须替换的槽位。不要保留与题目无关的套话。你的目标是固  
定“句子功能顺序”，而不是复制固定内容。  
### 3.1 Introduction 模板
A. Facts｜Mostly agree / disagree  
适用 Agree / Disagree，尤其有 most important / best / necessary 等判  
断命题  
结构 Background → Position → 预告主要依据  
模板 [Background sentence]. Although [concession], I largely   
agree/disagree that [statement], because [reason 1] and   
[reason 2].  
使用提醒 必须直接回应命题强度；“X 有价值”不等于“X 是最重要/最好/必  
要”。  
B. Effects｜Positive / Negative development  
适用 Is this a positive or negative development?  
结构 现象 → 总体判断 → 2 个结果方向  
模板 [X] has become increasingly common in recent years. Although   
it may [minor opposite effect], I consider this a largely   
positive/negative development because it can [effect 1] and   
[effect 2].  
C. Advantages outweigh disadvantages  
适用 Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?  
结构 承认较弱一边 → 明确较强一边  
模板 Although [X] may cause [main disadvantage], I believe its   
advantages, particularly [advantage 1] and [advantage 2], are   
more significant overall.  
使用提醒 比较题不能只写一边；正文必须真正比较强弱。  

D. Discuss both views + opinion  
适用 Discuss both views and give your own opinion.  
结构 View A → View B → My view  
模板 Some people believe that [View A], while others argue that   
[View B]. In my view, [your position] because [main reason].  
使用提醒 两边必须得到实质讨论。  
E. Two-Part｜Causes + Solutions  
适用 Why...? What can be done...?  
结构 Issue → causes → solutions  
模板 [Issue] has become a noticeable problem in many places. This is  
mainly caused by [cause 1] and [cause 2], and it can be   
addressed through [solution 1] and [solution 2].  
### 3.2 Body：Effects 模板
F. Positive Effect｜PCAE + Link  
适用 优势、正面发展、Agree 的正面理由  
结构 P → C → A → immediate E → further E → Link  
模板 One major benefit of [X] is that it can [Point]. In many cases,  
[Target] face [Condition], which makes it difficult for them to   
[desired result]. By [Action], they are able to [immediate effect].   
As a result, [main effect]. This can further lead to [long-term   
effect]. Therefore, [X] can play an important role in [Scope].  
使用提醒 Condition 很明显时可以缩短，把字数留给 Action + Effect。  
G. Negative Effect｜PCAE + Link  
适用 劣势、负面发展、风险类观点  
结构 P → C → A/situation → E → further E → Link  
模板 One significant problem with [X] is that it may [Point]. This is   
particularly concerning because [Condition]. When   

[situation/action], [Target] may [immediate negative effect]. As   
a result, [main negative effect]. In the long term, this may also   
lead to [further effect]. Therefore, [X] can have a negative   
impact on [Scope].  
H. Comparison｜比较强弱  
适用 advantages outweigh disadvantages / mostly benefits vs   
disadvantages  
结构 承认 A → 指出 B 更直接/更广/更长期 → verdict  
模板 Although [weaker effect] is a genuine concern, its impact is   
relatively limited because [reason]. By contrast, [stronger effect]
