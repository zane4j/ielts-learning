## Question

> More people are choosing to shop online rather than in physical stores. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?

## Sample Essay

Online shopping has become a routine part of daily life for many consumers. Although this trend can place pressure on traditional shops, I believe its advantages outweigh the disadvantages because it makes products more accessible and allows people to compare their options more effectively.

The main advantage is convenience. Customers can search for products, read descriptions and place orders without travelling to several shops. This is particularly useful for busy parents, older people and those who live in areas with limited retail services. Online platforms also allow buyers to compare prices and customer feedback before making a decision. As a result, households can often find products that better match their needs and budgets. These benefits affect large numbers of consumers on a regular basis, which makes them more significant than the occasional inconvenience of shopping in person.

Admittedly, the growth of online retail may reduce sales at physical stores. Smaller businesses can struggle to compete with large websites, potentially leading to shop closures and lost local employment. However, this problem does not mean that digital shopping is harmful overall. Traditional retailers can also sell online, arrange local delivery and use their existing reputation to attract customers. Governments and business associations may support this transition by offering digital training and affordable delivery services. Although some businesses will still face difficulties, helping them adapt offers a more practical solution than resisting changes in consumer preferences.

In conclusion, internet shopping creates real challenges for physical retailers, but the wider benefits of convenience, access and price comparison are more substantial. For this reason, I believe the advantages clearly outweigh the disadvantages.
