## Question

> More people are working from home rather than travelling to an office. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend?

## Sample Essay

Working from home has become more common as digital communication tools have improved. This change can save employees time and give them greater flexibility, but it may also weaken teamwork and make it difficult to separate professional and personal responsibilities.

A major benefit of remote work is that employees can avoid long daily journeys. In large cities, travelling to and from an office may take several hours each week, leaving workers tired and reducing the time they can spend with their families. Working from home allows many people to use that time for exercise, rest or childcare. It can also reduce transport costs and the number of vehicles on crowded roads. In addition, people who are able to arrange their working hours more flexibly may find it easier to balance their jobs with important family responsibilities. These improvements can increase job satisfaction and support long-term well-being.

Nevertheless, remote work can create serious communication problems. Workers may find it harder to exchange ideas or resolve urgent issues when they rely mainly on messages and video calls. New employees can be especially disadvantaged because they have fewer opportunities to observe experienced colleagues and ask informal questions. Another difficulty is that work can continue into the evening when no clear boundary exists between home and office. Employees may feel pressure to answer messages at any time, leading to stress rather than greater freedom. Organisations can reduce these risks by setting clear expectations, arranging regular meetings and providing support for new staff.

In conclusion, working from home offers substantial advantages through reduced commuting and increased flexibility. However, companies must actively manage communication and work-life boundaries to prevent these benefits from being undermined.
