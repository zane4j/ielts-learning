> **主题**：接受不理想的处境，还是努力改善？**练习日期**：2026-11-18。  
> **评分性质**：ChatGPT 模拟评分（不是雅思考官官方评分）；Band 7.5 是训练目标范文，不是经官方核定的分数。

## 1｜题目与审题

> Some people believe that it is best to accept a bad situation, such as an unsatisfactory job or shortage of money. Others argue that it is better to try and improve such situations.
>
> **Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.**

**必答任务：**（1）支持“接受现状”的理由；（2）支持“改善现状”的理由；（3）自己的立场。不是“改善的优点 + 接受的缺点”。

推荐结构：Introduction（改写问题、观点）→ Body 1（为什么有人接受现状）→ Body 2（为什么有人努力改善，且说明为何更赞成）→ Conclusion（承认适用条件、重申立场）。

### 关键问题：两段都在说同一边

- 原 Body 1：努力找工作 → 支付开支、减轻孤独感 → 改善生活。**支持改善**。
- 原 Body 2：不采取行动 → 经济问题可能恶化。**仍在反对接受，没有解释接受的优势**。
- 更合适的 Body 1：短期接受不满意的工作 → 保留稳定收入 → 避免冒险辞职 → 为求职和学习技能留下时间。
- 更合适的 Body 2：主动学习/申请新职位 → 提高获得更好工作的机会 → 长期改善生活。

## 4｜逐段语法纠错：~~删除线~~ = 原错误，**加粗** = 建议表达

> **标记原则**：删除线保留本次真实写错的片段，后面紧跟修改建议。这是训练批注稿，不是新的原文；有些句子为修复残句需要整句替换。涉及语义/逻辑而不仅是语法的修改，另行说明。

### Introduction：观点句的搭配和逻辑

Some people believe that it is best to accept a bad situation, such as an unsatisfactory job or shortage of money, while others argue that it is better to try and improve such situations. In my view, people should ~~spend efforts~~ **make an effort** to improve such situations because ~~it would really come true~~ **taking action can create better opportunities**.

**修正版：**

Some people believe that it is best to accept a difficult situation, such as having an unsatisfactory job or not having enough money, while others argue that people should try to change it. In my view, people should make an effort to improve their circumstances because taking action can create better opportunities.

**说明：** make an effort / make efforts 是正确搭配；come true 多搭配 dreams / wishes，不能自然表达“改变现状就会成功”。立场明确，但题目要求正文两边都讨论。

### Body 1：工作、生活开支与心理健康

One major benefit of ~~the point people should try and improve the situations~~ **trying to improve a difficult situation** is that it ~~provides the opportunities~~ **provides opportunities** for them to live a better life. In many cases, some adults face the problem of ~~losing job~~ **losing their jobs**, which makes it difficult for them to ~~hold their spend even basic daily expenditure~~ **cover even their basic daily expenses**. ~~By looking for a job with spending a lot of times and efforts, even a part-time job, so that they are able to afford household expenditure.~~ **By spending time and effort looking for work, even part-time work, they may earn enough to pay their household bills.** As a result, they have something to do instead of just staying at home and ~~getting isolation~~ **becoming isolated**. This can further lead to ~~a better mental health~~ **better mental health**, ~~moreover, they could also get promotion since they do have a job~~ **and it may eventually create opportunities for career advancement**. Therefore, ~~try and improve the situations can play an important role in job losing~~ **taking practical steps can be especially valuable for people who have lost their jobs**.

**修正版：**

One major benefit of trying to improve a difficult situation is that it can give people a chance to live a better life. For example, some adults lose their jobs and struggle to cover even their basic daily expenses. By spending time and effort looking for work, even part-time work, they may earn enough to pay their household bills. Having a job also gives them a daily routine instead of leaving them isolated at home. This can improve their mental health and may eventually create opportunities for career advancement. Therefore, taking practical steps can be especially valuable for people who have lost their jobs.

**说明：** 介词 by 后接动名词，但必须有完整主句（By doing ..., people can ...）；time / effort 在此不可数；cover expenses、pay bills、become isolated 是更自然的搭配。不要一下子谈收入、孤独、晋升三个方向却没有展开。

### Body 2：动名词、主谓一致与因果跳跃

One significant problem with ~~accept~~ **accepting** a bad situation without doing anything is that it may make the situation worse. This is particularly concerning because the situation would not become better naturally if ~~no one make~~ **no one makes** a decision to change it. When ~~nobody make efforts~~ **nobody makes an effort**, ~~society economic~~ **the economy** may ~~be worse gradually~~ **gradually weaken** because people are reluctant to ~~consume~~ **spend money** and there is ~~no more demands~~ **less demand**, forming ~~a bad cycle in economic~~ **a negative cycle in the economy**. As a result, the situations ~~become worse and worse~~ **may continue to deteriorate**. In the long term, this may also lead to social instability. Therefore, accepting can have a negative impact.

**修正版（仅语言修订，保留原论证方向）：**

One significant problem with accepting a bad situation without taking action is that the problem may become worse. This is because difficult circumstances rarely improve on their own unless someone tries to change them. When many people make no effort to improve their financial situation, economic activity may also weaken. For instance, if households continue to reduce their spending, demand for goods and services may fall, creating a negative cycle in the economy. In the long term, this could increase financial pressure and even contribute to social instability. Therefore, simply accepting difficult circumstances can have negative consequences.

**说明：** with accepting（介词后动名词）；no one makes（第三人称单数）；economy（名词）不同于 economic（形容词）。尤其注意“个人选择接受处境 → 社会经济恶化 → 社会不稳定”缺乏充分论证；此段**根本上答错段落任务**，应解释接受的合理性，而不是只改几处英语。

### Conclusion：拼写、动名词与观点复述

~~n conclusion~~ **In conclusion**, while ~~accept the situations has~~ **accepting difficult circumstances has** such serious disadvantages, I believe that ~~try and improve such situations is~~ **trying to improve these situations is** more convincing because it is practical.

**修正版：**

In conclusion, I believe that trying to improve difficult situations is more practical than passively accepting them, as it gives people a better chance of improving their lives.

**更适合双边讨论题的结尾：**

In conclusion, while accepting a difficult situation may provide temporary stability, I believe taking practical steps to improve it is generally a better long-term approach.

**说明：** 作主语时使用 accepting / trying，而不是 accept / try；简单说 practical 不够，应说明什么地方更实际。

## 5｜完整语言修正版（保留原来的论证方向）

> **注意**：这是语言修正版，并未完全修复双边讨论任务回应（TR）。勿把它当成完整合格的双边讨论范文。

Some people believe that it is best to accept a difficult situation, such as having an unsatisfactory job or not having enough money, while others argue that people should try to change it. In my view, people should make an effort to improve their circumstances because taking action can create better opportunities.

One major benefit of trying to improve a difficult situation is that it can give people a chance to live a better life. For example, some adults lose their jobs and struggle to cover even their basic daily expenses. By spending time and effort looking for work, even part-time work, they may earn enough to pay their household bills. Having a job also gives them a daily routine instead of leaving them isolated at home. This can improve their mental health and may eventually create opportunities for career advancement. Therefore, taking practical steps can be especially valuable for people who have lost their jobs.

One significant problem with accepting a bad situation without taking action is that the problem may become worse. This is because difficult circumstances rarely improve on their own unless someone tries to change them. When many people make no effort to improve their financial situation, economic activity may also weaken. For instance, if households continue to reduce their spending, demand for goods and services may fall, creating a negative cycle in the economy. In the long term, this could increase financial pressure and even contribute to social instability. Therefore, simply accepting difficult circumstances can have negative consequences.

In conclusion, I believe that trying to improve difficult situations is more practical than passively accepting them, as it gives people a better chance of improving their lives.

## 6｜Band 7.5 目标范文（原创训练示例，非官方评分范文）

It is often argued that people should accept unfortunate circumstances, such as an unfulfilling job or financial hardship, rather than struggle against them. Others believe they should actively seek ways to improve their situation. While acceptance can be useful when options are limited, I believe that taking sensible action is generally the better approach.

On the one hand, accepting a difficult situation can provide a sense of stability and prevent impulsive decisions. For instance, someone who dislikes their job may still need a regular income to pay rent and support their family. Quitting immediately without another offer could make their finances worse. By recognizing their current limitations, they can manage their expectations and focus on what they can control. In this sense, acceptance does not necessarily mean giving up; it can be a temporary strategy that allows people to plan carefully.

On the other hand, attempts to improve one's circumstances can open up opportunities that would otherwise remain unavailable. An employee who feels stuck in a low-paid position, for example, could acquire new skills, apply for a better role or negotiate their salary. Although these steps require time and may not guarantee success, they increase the likelihood of positive change. Moreover, making progress towards a realistic goal can restore confidence and reduce the sense of helplessness that often accompanies financial or professional difficulties.

In conclusion, accepting a bad situation can sometimes be the most sensible short-term response, especially when immediate change would involve serious risks. Nevertheless, I believe people should view acceptance as a starting point rather than a permanent solution, and take practical steps towards improvement whenever possible.

### 五个可复用句型

1. **One major benefit of [doing something] is that ...** / One major benefit of learning new skills is that it can create better job opportunities.
2. **This is particularly important because ...** / This is particularly important because many families depend on a stable income.
3. **By [doing something], people can ...** / By improving their professional skills, employees can increase their chances of getting promoted.
4. **Although ... may ..., it can also ...** / Although changing jobs may involve risks, it can also lead to better career opportunities.
5. **This can lead to ..., which in turn ...** / This can lead to higher incomes, which in turn can improve people's quality of life.

### 按模板套用的完整文章

Some people believe that [accepting a difficult situation is the safest choice], while others argue that [people should try to improve their circumstances]. Although [acceptance can provide stability in uncertain times], I believe that [taking practical action] is generally a better approach because [it gives people more control over their future].

On the one hand, one major reason why some people support [accepting an unpleasant situation] is that [it can protect their financial security]. This is because [changing jobs or making a major decision may involve considerable risks]. For example, [an employee who dislikes their position may still depend on a regular salary to pay rent and support their family]. Leaving without another offer could create more serious problems than staying for a few months. As a result, [accepting the situation temporarily can provide time to save money and make a careful plan].

On the other hand, I believe that [trying to improve a difficult situation] is more beneficial because [it can create new opportunities and improve people's quality of life]. For instance, [a worker who is unhappy with their salary could learn new skills and apply for better positions while keeping their current job]. This approach does not require an immediate and risky change. It also gives people a clear goal and helps them feel less helpless. This can lead to [higher earnings and greater job satisfaction]. Therefore, [gradual improvement is usually more effective than simply accepting an unsatisfactory situation forever].

In conclusion, while [accepting a difficult situation] may be useful in [the short term], I believe that [taking sensible steps to improve it] is a better [long-term] approach because [it allows people to make progress without exposing themselves to unnecessary risks].
