## 04 / Band 7.5 目标范文

重新编写的学习目标范文，非官方评分结果。

The bar chart presents the proportions of male and female adults taking part in five different leisure pursuits **in 2020**.

**Overall**, reading and cooking were more popular with women, **while** sports and gaming attracted larger shares of men. The figures for gardening were almost identical, making it the activity with the narrowest gender gap.

Among women, reading had the highest participation rate at **65%**, followed **closely** by cooking at **60%**. These proportions were substantially above the corresponding figures for men, **38% and 35%, respectively**. The differences amounted to **27 and 25 percentage points**. **By comparison**, men were most likely to play sports, **with 62% doing so compared with 42% of women**.

Gaming showed another notable male advantage: **55% of men** participated, as against **31% of women**, **representing a difference of 24 percentage points**. **In contrast**, gardening attracted very similar proportions of both groups, **33% of men and 36% of women**. The female figure was therefore only **slightly** higher in this category.

## 05 / 高频错误与复用句型

- **静态图写法：**不要使用 increase / decline 描述 2020 年的性别差异

- **数值精准：**女性 Sports 为 42%，不是 38%；比较百分比差异用 percentage points

- **对照句型：**The figure for A was higher than that for B.
