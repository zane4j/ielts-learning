04 / Band 7.5 目标范文

重新编写的学习目标范文，非官方评分结果。

The line graph shows changes in the number of people taking part in five activities at a Melbourne social centre between 2000 and 2020.

Overall, the film club remained the most popular activity throughout, whereas amateur dramatics declined to become the least popular by the end. Table tennis grew the most, especially after 2010, while participation in martial arts was relatively stable.

At the start, the film club attracted around 64 people, far more than martial arts at approximately 36. Film club attendance dipped to roughly 60 in 2010 before recovering to 66 in 2020. Meanwhile, martial arts numbers fluctuated slightly between about 32 and 37, ending close to their initial level.

Participation in table tennis increased from approximately 16 in 2000 to 20 in 2010, then climbed sharply to roughly 54 by 2020, making it the second-largest activity. By contrast, amateur dramatics fell from a 2005 peak of just under 30 to around six in 2020. Musical performances had no recorded participants in the first two years, but rose to nearly 20 by the end.

05 / 高频错误与复用句型

  • 趋势搭配：fluctuated slightly / remained relatively stable / rose sharply
  • 结果分词：..., becoming the least popular activity；..., overtaking martial arts
  • 对照组织：Film club + martial arts 为稳定组，table tennis + amateur dramatics 为明显变化组
