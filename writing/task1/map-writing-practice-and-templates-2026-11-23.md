## 一、先记地图题的写作核心

**Maps = Location（方位）+ Change（变化）+ Comparison（对比）+ Overview（概述）。**

本次练习最值得保留的结论：

1. **设施名称和方位准确，优先于高级词汇。** 把「阅览室」写成「入口」，或把 south-east 写成 north-east，属于信息错误。
2. **描述“旧 → 现在”，优先掌握被动语态。** has been converted into / has been replaced by / has been removed / has been expanded。
3. **补充变化，用 with + 名词 + 过去分词。** with a café added / with the old fountain removed。
4. **保持不变，用 has remained unchanged。** remain 是状态动词，这里不是被动语态。
5. **一句话只承载清楚的变化关系。** 不必刻意把所有变化塞进一个超长句；不要用逗号连接两个完整独立分句。

## 二、地图题四段式模板（稳 6.5 优先）

### 2.1 模板 A：过去布局 vs 现在布局

适用：同一个公园、图书馆、校园，只有「20 年前」与「现在」两幅图，建筑区域较多；尤其适合本次 Cambridge 18 Test 3 图书馆题。

**Introduction｜改写题干**

The two maps compare the layout of [PLACE] [TIME IN THE PAST] with its present-day layout.

例如：The two maps compare the layout of a public library 20 years ago with its present-day layout.

**Overview｜两条核心特征**

Overall, [PLACE] has undergone significant changes, with [MAIN CHANGE] and [NEW FACILITIES]. However, [UNCHANGED FEATURE] has remained unchanged.

例如：Overall, the library has undergone significant changes, with most areas repurposed and new facilities introduced. However, the main entrance has remained in the same location.

**Body 1｜描述过去（一般过去时）**

Twenty years ago, [CENTRAL FEATURE] stood in the centre. On the western side, [WEST FEATURE] was situated between [NORTH-WEST FEATURE] and [SOUTH-WEST FEATURE]. On the eastern side, [EAST FEATURES] were located [POSITION].

例如：Twenty years ago, tables and chairs stood in the centre of the library. The adult fiction area was situated between a media room to the north and a reading room to the south.

**Body 2｜描述现在（现在完成时 / 现在时）**

Today, [OLD A] has been converted into [NEW A], while [OLD B] has been replaced by [NEW B]. In addition, [OLD C] has been removed, with [NEW C] added nearby.

例如：Today, the former reading room has been converted into a computer room, while the enquiry desk has been replaced by a café.

**为什么这样分段：**过去设施集中在 Body 1，现在的变化集中在 Body 2。适合只有两张图、设施名称较多的题目；如果 Body 1 已描述旧位置，Body 2 尽量避免每次重复完整方位。

### 2.2 模板 B：按区域分组（西侧 vs 东侧）

适用：地图左右分区清楚、每个区域都有明显替换；可减少过去和现在的来回切换。

**Introduction**

The maps illustrate how the layout of [PLACE] changed between [YEAR A] and [YEAR B].

**Overview**

Overall, the area underwent substantial redevelopment, with [MAIN CHANGE]. The [UNCHANGED FEATURE], however, remained in place.

**Body 1｜Western / Northern section**

On the western side, [OLD A] was replaced by [NEW A], while [OLD B] was converted into [NEW B]. The [FEATURE] remained unchanged.

**Body 2｜Eastern / Southern section**

In the eastern section, [OLD C] gave way to [NEW C]. A new [FACILITY] was also added [POSITION], whereas [OTHER FEATURE] was removed.

**选择哪种模板：**按图中信息的自然分组决定，不要把 A、B 两种模板强行拼在一起；四段式是训练方法，不是官方限定段数。

## 三、五类地图题必背句型（从本次练习提炼）

| 功能 | 推荐母句 | 中文理解 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 重大变化 | The area **has undergone significant changes**. | 区域经历了重大变化 |
| 被改造成 | A **has been converted into** B. | A 被改作 B |
| 被取代 | A **has been replaced by** B. | A 被 B 替代 |
| 同义替换 | A **has given way to** B. | A 让位于 B |
| 被拆除 | A **has been removed / demolished**. | A 已被移除 / 拆除 |
| 被扩建 | A **has been expanded / enlarged**. | A 已被扩建 |
| 新增设施 | A new B **has been added / built**. | 新增 B |
| 保留不变 | A **has remained unchanged**. | A 一直没有变化 |
| 位置不变 | A **has remained in the same location**. | A 的位置没变 |
| 同一句补充拆除 | ... , **with A removed**. | A 被移除 |
| 同一句补充新建 | ... , **with B added / built**. | B 被增建 |
| 同一句补充转换 | ... , **with A converted into B**. | A 被改造成 B |
| 两个设施同时变化 | ... , **with A replaced by B and C expanded**. | 替换 A，扩建 C |
| 两区域对比 | A has been rebuilt, **while** B has remained unchanged. | 一处变化、另一处不变 |
| 原址新建 | A has been removed, **with B built in its place**. | 原址建成 B |

### 3.1 两个容易混淆的语法结构

**完整句子（现在完成时被动）：**

- The former reading room **has been converted into** a computer room.
- The old car park **has been replaced by** a garden.
- The tables and chairs **have been removed**.

**with 伴随结构（名词 + 过去分词；这里不能再带 has been）：**

- The library has been redesigned, **with the former reading room converted into** a computer room.
- The park has undergone significant changes, **with a new rose garden added**.
- The school has been redeveloped, **with the old library removed and a sports hall constructed**.

**错误警报：**

- ❌ with the former room **has been converted** into a computer room
- ✅ with the former room **converted** into a computer room
- ❌ The garden **has converted** into a car park
- ✅ The garden **has been converted** into a car park

> **公式：**完整句子 = has/have + been + 过去分词；with 结构 = with + 名词 + 过去分词。  
> **例外要记：**The main entrance **has remained unchanged** 是 has + 过去分词（主动），不是 has been remained。

### 3.2 in / on / to：位置三分法

| 中文 | 正确表达 | 重点 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 在东北角 | **in the north-eastern corner** | 区域内部的角落 |
| 在西南角 | **in the south-western corner** | corner 必须写 |
| 在图书馆西侧 | **on the western side of the library** | side 必须写 |
| 在入口东侧 | **to the east of the entrance** | 参照物之间的相对方位 |
| 在建筑物北侧 | **to the north of the building** | 不等于 in the northern corner |
| 在 A 与 B 之间 | **between A and B** | 两个参照物 |
| 位于…… | **be located / be situated** | 地图题基础谓语 |
| 过去在…… | **was situated between ... and ...** | Body 1 旧布局 |

不能写 **the western of the library** 或 **on the north-western of the library**，因为 western / north-western 是形容词，后面需要 side、corner 或 section 等名词。

## 四、本次 10 轮翻译原句与修正版（真实答题记录）

以下练习原句按实际提交保留，**不自动改动你的原文**；修正版是复盘参考。方位以每轮中文练习题为准，不把这些模拟场景当成剑雅18地图的官方设施数据。

### 第 1 轮｜阅览室改造 + 入口不变

**我的原句**

> The reading room has been converted into a computer room, whereas the main entrance of library remained unchanged.

**修正版**

> The former reading room has been converted into a computer room, while the library's main entrance has remained unchanged.

**记忆点：**of **the** library；突出过去至今可以用 **has remained unchanged**；former 指“原来的”。

### 第 2 轮｜东北角转换 + 西北角扩建

**我的原句**

> The former children's room has been replaced by a lecture room, while the north-west room has been expanded.

**修正版**

> The former children's books room has been converted into a lecture room, while the room in the north-western corner has been enlarged.

**记忆点：**north-western corner 比 north-west room 更明确；**has been expanded** 本身正确。

### 第 3 轮｜东侧改造 + 新增咖啡馆 + 移除桌椅

**我的原句**

> The eastern side of the library has been changed, added a cafe, whereas the tables and chairs in the centre of library have been removed.

**修正版**

> The eastern side of the library has undergone significant changes, with a new café added, while the tables and chairs in the centre have been removed.

**记忆点：**不能直接接 “changed, added”；需要 **with a café added**；centre of **the** library。

### 第 4 轮｜西侧重新设计 + with 结构

**我的原句**

> The western of the library has been redesigned, with a former reading room has been converted into a computer room, with several self-service machines added

**修正版**

> The western section of the library has undergone significant changes, with the former reading room converted into a computer room and several self-service machines installed.

**记忆点：**western 后补 **section / side**；**with ... converted** 不再使用 has been；两个变化用 and 并列。

### 第 5 轮｜喷泉替换 + 南侧入口不变

**我的原句**

> The park has undergone significant changes in last 20 years, with the former fountain replaced by a children area, while the main entrance in the northern of park has remained unchanged.

**修正版**

> Over the past 20 years, the park has undergone significant changes, with the old fountain replaced by a children's play area, while the main entrance on the southern side has remained unchanged.

**记忆点：****over the past 20 years**；**children's play area**；中文为南侧，不能误写成 northern。

### 第 6 轮｜东北角儿童书室 + 入口西侧阅览室

**我的原句**

> Over the past 20 years, the children' books room in the north-eastern corner has been converted into a lecture room, while the main entrance on the western side of library has been replaced by a computer room.

**修正版**

> Over the past 20 years, the children's books room in the north-eastern corner has been converted into a lecture room, while the reading room to the west of the main entrance has been replaced by a computer room.

**记忆点：**children's 所有格；第二个被替换的是**阅览室**，不是**主入口**。这是信息准确性问题，不能只改语法。

### 第 7 轮｜西南角阅览室 + 入口以东咖啡馆

**我的原句**

> Over the past 20 years, the former reading room in the southwest corner has been replaced by a computer room, with a coffee shop added on the eastern side of the main entrance.

**修正版**

> Over the past 20 years, the former reading room in the south-western corner has been converted into a computer room, with a new café added to the east of the main entrance.

**记忆点：**这轮结构基本正确。coffee shop 可用，但 **café** 更贴近图例名称；**to the east of** 更明确。

### 第 8 轮｜西北角扩建 + 东南角设施替换 + 入口保留

**我的原句**

> Compared with 20 years ago, the children's area on the north-western of the library has been enlarged, the service center on the north-eastern side has been replaced by a cafe, whereas the main entrance has  remained unchanged.

**修正版**

> Over the past 20 years, the children's activity area in the north-western corner of the library has been expanded, while the enquiry desk in the south-eastern corner has been replaced by a café. However, the main entrance has remained in the same location.

**记忆点：**north-western **corner**；原题是东南角 **south-eastern**，不是东北；两个独立分句不能只靠逗号连接（comma splice）。

### 第 9 轮｜东北角停车场拆除 + 原址建游乐区

**我的原句**

> Over the last 20 years, the car park in the north-eastern corner has been removed, with a children's activity area built in its place, while the location of main entrance on the western of park has remained unchanged.

**修正版**

> Over the last 20 years, the car park in the north-eastern corner has been removed, with a children's play area built in its place, while the main entrance on the western side of the park has remained unchanged.

**记忆点：**with ... built in its place 正确；**the** main entrance；western **side** of **the** park。句子太长时可以拆成两句。

### 第 10 轮｜西北角花园改停车场 + 东南角泳池改健身中心

**我的原句**

> Over the last 20 years, the old garden in the north-western corner of the park has converted into a car park, while the swimming pool on the south-eastern side of the park has been replaced by a new sport centre.

**修正版**

> Over the past 20 years, the former garden in the north-western corner of the park has been converted into a car park, while the swimming pool in the south-eastern corner has given way to a new fitness centre.

**记忆点：****has been converted**（不能漏 been）；东南角应写 **in the south-eastern corner**；健身中心更贴近 **fitness centre**，而 sports centre 偏大型体育中心。

## 五、练习中的 20 个可复用例句（按功能分类）

> 本节以本次练习中给出的示范例句为基础，并补充了少量同结构的独立扩展示例；所有句子均为句型复习材料，不是用户的原始答题，也不保证对应任何真实考题地图。

### 5.1 改变用途 / converted into

1. The former car park has been converted into a public garden.
2. The former office has been converted into a meeting room.
3. The former library to the north of the main school building has been converted into a sports hall.
4. The former factory has been converted into a museum.

### 5.2 替换 / replaced by / given way to

5. The old fountain in the north-western corner of the park has been replaced by a children's play area.
6. The former car park in the south-eastern corner of the park has been replaced by a garden.
7. The old car park in the south-eastern corner of the park has given way to a new swimming pool.
8. The former car park on the southern side of the park has been replaced by a garden, while the entrance on the eastern side has remained unchanged.

### 5.3 扩建、保留 / expanded / unchanged

9. The car park on the western side has been expanded, while the main entrance has remained unchanged.
10. The old shops have been demolished, while the main road has remained unchanged.
11. The old car park has been replaced by a garden, while the entrance on the eastern side has remained unchanged.
12. The main entrance has remained in the same location, despite the redevelopment.

### 5.4 with 结构：新增、拆除、原址新建

13. The park has undergone significant changes, with a children's play area added.
14. The library has been redesigned, with the tables and chairs in the centre removed.
15. The park has been redesigned, with the old fountain removed and a new rose garden added.
16. The school has undergone significant changes, with a new sports hall added and the old library demolished.
17. The old swimming pool has been removed, with a sports hall built in its place.
18. The former office has been converted into a meeting room, with a car park added to the west of the building.

### 5.5 两个区域 / position + comparison

19. A café has been added in the south-eastern corner, while the car park on the northern side has been expanded.
20. The library has been modernised, with several new facilities added.

**背诵方式：**不建议逐字背 20 句，挑 6 个母句，每次替换设施和方位。最有价值的是：converted into / replaced by / remained unchanged / with ... added / built in its place / in the ... corner。

## 六、剑雅18 Test 3：Central Library 题的完整地图写作示例

这是根据本次评分时讨论的 **图书馆过去与现在** 概念整理出的训练稿，并非用户最初独立作文；与前面公园、学校等模拟翻译题分开。

### 6.1 题目变化速记

| 过去 | 现在 | 变化 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 中央 tables and chairs | 中央桌椅移除 | removed |
| 西北 CDs / videos / computer games room | 扩大的 children’s fiction / storytelling area | enlarged + converted |
| 西南 reading room | computer room | converted |
| 东北 children's books room | lecture room | replaced / converted |
| 东南 enquiry desk | café | replaced |
| 西侧 adult fiction | reference books | changed use |
| 东侧 adult non-fiction | adult fiction + information desk + self-service machines | reorganised |
| 主入口 | 原位置 | remained unchanged |

> **审题提醒：**地图题首先确保方位、对象和更替关系准确；这张地图中不要把“自助设备”错放到西侧，也不要把“入口”说成“被电脑室替代”。

### 6.2 Band 6.5+ 训练参考稿（四自然段）

The two maps compare the layout of a public library 20 years ago with its present-day layout.

Overall, the library has undergone significant changes, with most areas repurposed to provide a wider range of facilities. The tables and chairs in the centre have been removed, creating a more open space, while the entrance and the basic layout have remained unchanged.

Twenty years ago, two tables and several chairs stood in the centre of the library. On the western side, the adult fiction area was situated between a room containing CDs, videos and computer games in the north-western corner and a reading room for newspapers and magazines in the south-western corner. On the opposite side, the children's books room occupied the north-east, with the adult non-fiction section in the middle and an enquiry desk in the south-east.

Today, the former media room in the north-west has been enlarged and converted into a children's fiction and storytelling area, equipped with two sofas. The south-western reading room has become a computer room, while the north-eastern children's books room has been replaced by a lecture room and the enquiry desk by a café. The western adult fiction section now houses reference books, whereas the eastern adult non-fiction section has been reorganised into an adult fiction area with an information desk and self-service machines.

**这篇怎么学习：**先观察四段的作用，再分别背会 **Overview 的主要变化**、**Body 1 的 between A and B**、**Body 2 的 converted into / replaced by / whereas**。不要把该参考稿当作已认证的 Band 6.5 分数。

### 6.3 开头和 Overview 速背模板

- **Opening：**The maps compare the layout of [PLACE] [TIME A] with its present-day layout.
- **Overall change：**Overall, [PLACE] has undergone significant changes, with [MAIN CHANGE] and [NEW FACILITY].
- **Exception：**However, [ENTRANCE / ROAD / ORIGINAL FEATURE] has remained unchanged.
- **Central feature：**The central [OLD FEATURE] has been removed, creating a more open space.
- **Two groups：**On the western side, ... . On the eastern side, ... .

## 七、最近 10 轮错误规律 + 下一阶段练习重点

| 问题 | 真实出现的错误 | 正确表达 | 提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 缺少 been | has converted into | **has been converted into** | 设施被改造，完整句须被动 |
| with 后重复谓语 | with ... has been converted | **with ... converted** | with 后接名词 + 过去分词 |
| 方位缺名词 | the western of library | **the western side of the library** | western 是形容词 |
| 方位错向 | northern（题目是 southern） | **southern** | north/south 与 east/west 必须回图核对 |
| 角落 vs 侧面 | on the south-eastern side（题目是东南角） | **in the south-eastern corner** | 指向角落就写 corner |
| 缺冠词 | centre of library / main entrance | **centre of the library / the main entrance** | 特指设施通常加 the |
| 所有格 | children' books | **children's books** | children 为不规则复数 |
| 设施误指 | main entrance was replaced（原题是 reading room） | **the reading room was replaced** | 先确定旧设施是哪一个 |
| 长句逗号拼接 | A has changed, B has changed | **A has changed, while B ...** | 两个独立句用连接词、分号或拆句 |
| 设施名称 | a children area / sport centre | **children's play area / fitness centre** | 用图题指定的名称 |

### 7.1 进步

- 已能正确使用 **has undergone significant changes**、**has been replaced by**、**has remained unchanged**。
- **with + 名词 + 过去分词** 从第 3、4 轮错误，逐渐过渡到第 7、9 轮自然使用。
- 对 **in the ... corner** 和 **to the ... of** 的理解更清楚。

### 7.2 待巩固（按优先级）

1. **写前先核对：**设施名、方位、变化关系（A → B），杜绝信息写反。
2. **写时检查完整句：**has / have + been + V-ed；是否缺 the；主语单复数。
3. **写后检查复杂句：**两个完整句是否只有逗号；with 结构是否出现多余 has been。
4. **提分重点：**每段选择主要特征，不堆砌所有位置；Band 7 更看重准确和信息组织，而非一味写长句。

### 7.3 地图题 30 秒检查表

- [ ] 四个方向（NE / NW / SE / SW）有没有看反？
- [ ] **corner / side / to the ... of** 是否与地图位置一致？
- [ ] 每个 A → B 的旧设施和新设施是否对应？
- [ ] **has been converted / replaced / removed / expanded** 是否漏 been？
- [ ] **with + noun + V-ed** 后有没有误加 has been？
- [ ] a / an / the、children's、复数等是否正确？
- [ ] 是否有 comma splice（两个独立句仅靠逗号连接）？
- [ ] Overview 是否概括「主要变化 + 保留特征」？
