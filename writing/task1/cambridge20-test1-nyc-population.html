03 / Band 6.5+ 参考修订稿

四自然段，五色背景标记；基于保留数据重新撰写，非当时批改原文

The three tables compare the total population of New York City and the figures for Manhattan and the other four boroughs in 1800, 1900 and 2000.

Overall, New York City's population increased dramatically over the period, growing to more than 100 times its original size. However, Manhattan's share of the total fell steadily, while the combined share of the other boroughs rose substantially.

In 1800, New York City had 79,216 residents, of whom 60,515 (approximately 76%) lived in Manhattan. By 1900, the city's population had risen to around 3.44 million, with Manhattan accounting for just over half of the total. The borough's own population reached roughly 1.85 million.

By 2000, New York's population had grown to 8,009,185, but Manhattan had only 1,538,096 residents, around 19% of the total. In contrast, the other four boroughs accounted for approximately 81%, showing that most of the city's long-term population growth occurred outside Manhattan.

04 / Band 7.5 目标范文

重新编写的学习目标范文，非官方评分结果。

The tables provide information on the population of New York City and the distribution of its residents between Manhattan and the other four boroughs in 1800, 1900 and 2000.

Overall, the city's population expanded enormously over the two centuries, reaching more than 100 times its 1800 level. Manhattan initially housed the vast majority of residents, whereas by 2000 the population was concentrated in the other boroughs.

In 1800, the city had 79,216 inhabitants, including 60,515 in Manhattan. The borough therefore represented about 76% of the total, compared with just 24% for the other four areas combined. By 1900, the overall population had surged to approximately 3.44 million. Manhattan itself contained about 1.85 million people, yet its proportion had fallen to roughly 54%.

The city continued to grow, reaching 8,009,185 residents in 2000. However, Manhattan's population was down from its 1900 figure to approximately 1.54 million, reducing its share to only 19%. Meanwhile, the other boroughs collectively accounted for around 81% of the city population, a marked reversal of the situation in 1800.

05 / 高频错误与复用句型

  • 单位与拼写：growed → grew；80 thousands → 80 thousand；population share 而非 population rate
  • 变化对比：Manhattan 的绝对人口与占比不是同一概念
  • 关键特征：2000 年城市人口约为 1800 年的 101 倍；曼哈顿占比 76%→19%
