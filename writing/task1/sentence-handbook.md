## 〇、写作总框架 · 先看整体

原笔记的**四段结构**：Introduction（介绍图）→ Overview（最重要的特征）→ Body 1（第一组细节）→ Body 2（第二组细节）。分组可依据：趋势相同或相反、幅度大小、最高/最低、时间阶段。

| 段落 | 任务 | 建议词数 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | 改写图题、对象、时间、单位 | 20–25 |
| Overview | 概括主趋势/排名变化/步骤 | 30–40 |
| Body 1 | 第一组关键细节及数据对照 | 50–65 |
| Body 2 | 第二组细节、差异及对照 | 50–65 |

官方要求 Academic Task 1 至少 150 词，建议约 20 分钟完成。四段结构和各段词数是学习策略，不是官方强制格式。[IELTS 官方说明](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing)

## 一、Introduction · 开头段

**原笔记公式：**The [line chart / bar chart / pie chart / table] provides information on + topic。常用同义动词：shows / illustrates / compares / gives information about；时间：in 2000 / from 2000 to 2020 / between 2000 and 2020。

| 类型 | 示例 |
| --- | --- |
| 柱状图 | The bar chart compares employment shares across five sectors in 2000 and 2020. |
| 折线图 | The line graph illustrates changes in commuting patterns between 2000 and 2020. |
| 饼图 | The pie charts show how household spending was divided among five categories. |
| 流程图 | The diagram illustrates how recycled paper is produced. |
| 地图 | The maps compare the layout of a park in 1920 with its layout today. |

拓展：不用强行替换 chart、shows 等准确单词；题目里的时间、单位必须写对。

## 二、动态图 · Dynamic Charts

### 2.1 趋势词和程度副词

| 功能 | 原笔记关键词 | 可用例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 上升 | rise / grow / increase / climb / surge / soar | Bus use rose steadily. |
| 下降 | decrease / decline / drop / fall | The figure fell from 50% to 35%. |
| 持平 | remain stable / remain unchanged / level off | It levelled off at around 30%. |
| 波动 | fluctuate / vary | Sales fluctuated between 20,000 and 25,000. |
| 显著变化 | significantly / considerably / markedly / dramatically | The proportion declined significantly. |
| 平稳/逐渐 | steadily / gradually | The figure climbed steadily. |
| 小幅 | slightly / marginally | The percentage rose slightly. |

原笔记建议每类挑 2–3 个熟词。拓展：**moderately** 表示中等程度，不宜直接归入“小幅”；**soar / plunge** 应只用于幅度确实很大时。

### 2.2 峰值、相交、近似、差距

**峰值：**peak at 80 million / reach a peak of 80 million。**最低点：**fall to a low of 12%。**反超：**A overtook B in 2015。**相等：**A stood at the same level as B。**近似：**approximately / roughly / just over / just under。**差距收窄：**The gap narrowed from 20 to 5 percentage points.

原笔记提到“大胆估值”，这里补充风险：读数应遵守坐标轴精度，不能随意估计。

### 2.3 六类单阶段句型 · 原笔记的骨架

- **趋势动词：**The population of City M increased substantially from 5 million to 15 million.

- **时间作主语（目击句型）：**The period from 2000 to 2010 witnessed a significant rise in population.

- **There be：**There was a marked increase in the number of visitors.

- **趋势名词作主语：**A gradual decline occurred over the period.

- **比较：**The figure for 2010 was higher than that for 2000.

- **倍数：**The figure tripled / was three times as high as its original level.

补充：witness 句型并非提分必要条件；直白准确的 increased from X to Y 往往更稳定。

### 2.4 多阶段变化 · before / followed by / after which

**before V-ing：**The figure rose to 15 million in 2010, before declining to 10 million in 2015.

**followed by：**A sharp rise was followed by a gradual fall.

**after which：**The proportion peaked at 50% in 2010, after which it fell to 30%.

**whereas / while：**Car use declined, whereas public transport use increased.

**with + 名词 + 分词：**Employment shifted towards services, with its share rising to 38%.

句子重点：起点 → 转折点 → 终点；避免每个年份机械列数。

## 三、静态图 · Static Charts

原笔记定义：同一时间点的跨类别比较，核心是**占比、排名、差异与倍数**，通常不写随时间上升/下降。

| 功能 | 句型 |
| --- | --- |
| 最大占比 | A accounted for the largest proportion, at 40%. |
| 最小占比 | B made up the smallest share, at just 5%. |
| 排第二 | C ranked second, closely followed by D. |
| 两者对比 | The figure for A was higher than that for B. |
| 倍数 | The figure for A was twice that for B. |
| 差距 | There was a gap of 12 percentage points between A and B. |

注意：原笔记中的 A was twice that of B 比较对象不一致，改为 the figure for A was twice that for B。

## 四、流程图 · Process Diagrams

原笔记要点：描述**步骤顺序 + 被动语态**。开篇可用 The diagram illustrates how X is produced / processed。连接顺序使用 First / Next / Following this / Subsequently / Finally。

| 动作 | 可复用句型 |
| --- | --- |
| 收集与运输 | Raw materials are collected and transported to a factory. |
| 加热与加工 | The mixture is heated and then processed. |
| 完成与储存 | The final product is packaged and stored. |
| 同时 | Meanwhile, a second component is prepared. |
| 条件 | Once the material has cooled, it is transferred to a container. |
| 循环 | The cycle then begins again. |

**拓展：**人工制造过程常用一般现在时被动语态；自然循环可用主动，例如 Water evaporates and forms clouds。Overview 可写总阶段数、起点终点、是否循环。不要补图中没有的材料、温度和步骤。

## 五、地图题 · Maps

原笔记要点：地图比较**方位 + 拆除 / 新建 / 替换 / 保留**，通常采用被动语态。

| 功能 | 可复用句型 |
| --- | --- |
| 位置 | in the north-eastern corner / to the south of the entrance |
| 拆除 | The old stage was removed. |
| 新建 | A café was constructed on the eastern side. |
| 替换 | The fountain was replaced by a rose garden. |
| 改变用途 | The building was converted into a museum. |
| 扩建 | The car park was expanded. |
| 保留不变 | The two entrances remained unchanged. |

**Overview 例句：**Overall, the area underwent substantial redevelopment, with several new facilities added while the main entrances were retained.

**拓展：**1920 → Today 可以用现在完成时突出至今的结果，也可以用过去时描述当时的设施；避免“has been built in 1920”这类与确切过去时间冲突的表达。

[关联练习：Grange Park 地图题的原文、6.5+ 修订和 7.5 范文](cambridge14-test4-grange-park.html)

## 六、考前使用建议

- **每类词只背 2–3 个：**rise / increase、fall / decline、remain stable / fluctuate，优先保证拼写和搭配。

- **背句型骨架，而非整句：**对象 + 变化 + from X to Y + 时间；比较时用 the figure for A / that for B。

- **单独写 Overview：**抓整体变化和极端项，细节数据留给 Body；“禁止出现任何数字”并非官方硬性规则。

- **熟练多阶段衔接：**before V-ing / followed by / after which；不要牺牲语法准确性来堆复杂结构。

- **检查单位：**百分比、百分点、万人、千吨、年数；近似值也必须有图表根据。

- **建议时间分配：**看图和分组 2–3 分钟，写作 14–15 分钟，检查 2–3 分钟；根据实际速度调整。

## 七、拓展一 · Overview 直接套用的四类结构

以下为**本次新增**，不是上传笔记声称的视频逐字内容。IELTS 官方 Task Achievement 关注概括、关键特征、准确的数据和相关比较。[官方 Writing 准备资源](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/writing-test-resources/)

| 类型 | Overview 模板 |
| --- | --- |
| 动态图 | Overall, A increased while B declined. By the end, A had overtaken B. |
| 静态图 | Overall, A was the largest category, whereas B had the smallest share. |
| 流程图 | Overall, the process comprises six stages, starting with X and ending with Y. |
| 地图 | Overall, the area became more developed, although several original features were retained. |

不要在 Overview 反复重复 Body 的全部数据，也不要把无关细节算作主趋势。描述原因只在图表明确显示时进行。

## 八、拓展二 · 数据、倍数与比较

| 目的 | 推荐表达 | 实例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 达到某值 | rise to / fall to | rose to 40% |
| 增加或减少多少 | increase by / decrease by | rose by 13 percentage points |
| 从起点到终点 | from X to Y | fell from 40% to 25% |
| 对应两个数值 | respectively | 20% and 30%, respectively |
| 可数人数 | the number of visitors | The number of visitors reached 2 million. |
| 某个群体占比 | the proportion of / the percentage of | the proportion of employees in services |
| 倍数 | twice as high as / three times as many as | The figure for A was twice that for B. |
| 三个图/混合图 | check units before comparing | Million people cannot be treated as % directly. |

> **必须区分：**10% → 23% 是增加 **13 percentage points**（百分点），相对增长率则是 **130%**。不要直接写 an increase of 13% 来代替百分点。

### 拓展：饼图、表格和混合图

**饼图：**占比 account for / make up / represent；多个年份还要比较份额变化。**表格：**先找异常值、峰值及变化最大的行/列，按主要特征分组。**混合图：**先检查每张图的单位和基数，不要把人口绝对数量与人口占比直接当同类数值比较。

**复合句四选一：**A increased, **whereas** B declined. / A peaked at X **before falling** to Y. / The park was redeveloped, **with a café added**. / The figure reached 40%, **which was the highest level**.

## 九、原笔记表达纠错 · 推荐替换清单

这里对笔记中可能误用或不够自然的句型做**独立语言校订**，不是对视频讲者言论的评价。

| 原笔记 / 风险写法 | 推荐表达 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The number of population in M city... | **The population of City M increased...** | population 已包含人口数量的含义。 |
| A marked increase could be found... | **The figure increased markedly...** | 前者可以理解，但冗长且不自然。 |
| The number in 2010 was higher than that of 2000. | **The figure for 2010 was higher than that for 2000.** | 保持比较对象一致。 |
| A was twice that of B. | **The figure for A was twice that for B.** | 不能把类别名称直接与数字比。 |
| A is three times higher than B. | **A is three times as high as B.** | 避免倍数解释歧义。 |
| improved nearly threefold | **increased nearly threefold** | 上升不一定代表改善。 |
| amounted to the peak / same level with | **peaked at / the same level as** | 固定搭配。 |
| rose sharply（但图中只增加一点） | **rose slightly** | 程度必须符合图中变化。 |
| the figure for A increased by 13%（10%→23%） | **increased by 13 percentage points** | 百分比之差使用百分点。 |
| Overview never contains numbers | **Prefer an overall summary; details usually go in Body.** | “绝对不能写数字”不是官方硬性规则。 |
| 估值可随意大胆写 | **Use about / approximately when justified by the chart.** | 不能编造或超出坐标轴支持的精度。 |

## 十、专项练习 · 自测八题

先独立中译英，再展开参考答案。所有练习数据均为模拟数据。

- 汽车通勤比例从 60% 下降到 35%（2000–2020）。

- 科技就业占比从 10% 升至 23%，增加了多少个百分点？用英文写出。

- A 的占比 40%，B 为 20%，A 是 B 的两倍。

- 这一数据在 2010 年升至 50%，随后在 2020 年降至 30%。

- 原材料被收集并运输到工厂。

- 原来的舞台被新的露天剧场取代。

- 写 Overview：服务业和科技行业增长，其余三个行业下降。

- 纠错：The number of population growed dramatically in 2020.

查看参考答案（做完再展开）

- The proportion of commuters using cars fell from 60% in 2000 to 35% in 2020.

- The employment share in technology rose by 13 percentage points, from 10% to 23%.

- The figure for A was twice that for B, at 40% and 20%, respectively.

- The figure rose to 50% in 2010 before falling to 30% in 2020.

- The raw materials are collected and transported to a factory.

- The original stage was replaced by a new amphitheatre.

- Overall, employment shares in services and technology increased, whereas those in the other three sectors declined.

- The population of the city grew dramatically in 2020.（只有给出不同时点的数据才能判断是否 grew。）

## 十一、完整四段范例 · 就业占比柱状图

**模拟题：**某国五个行业 2000 和 2020 年就业占比。Agriculture 20%→8%，Manufacturing 28%→18%，Construction 15%→13%，Technology 10%→23%，Services 27%→38%。[查看此前的真实原文和修改对照](employment-sector-review.html)。

**下面的完整文章是本次新增的学习范文**，不是该视频的范文，也不是用户之前原稿。

The bar chart compares the percentages of people employed in five sectors of a country **in 2000 and 2020**.

**Overall**, the employment shares of technology and services **increased**, **whereas** the figures for agriculture, manufacturing and construction **declined**. Services became the largest employer, and technology **moved from last to second place**.

In 2000, manufacturing accounted for the highest proportion, **at 28%**, closely followed by services at **27%**. Technology employed the smallest share, at **10%**. **By 2020**, services had **risen** to **38%**, **while** technology had more than doubled to **23%**, **gains of 11 and 13 percentage points respectively**.

**By contrast**, agriculture **fell** **sharply** **from 20% to 8%**, becoming the smallest sector. Manufacturing also **declined**, **from 28% to 18%**, **whereas** construction changed relatively little, **slipping** **from 15% to 13%**. **These changes left manufacturing in third place by the end of the period**.
