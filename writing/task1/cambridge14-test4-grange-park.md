## 03 / Band 6.5+ 完整参考稿

前两段基于已提交内容修订，后两段为新增示范。

The two plans compare **the layout of Grange Park when it opened in 1920** with its present-day layout.

**Overall**, the park underwent **significant** redevelopment, **with most of its original features replaced by newer recreational facilities**. **However**, the main entrances from Arnold Avenue and Eldon Street **remained unchanged**, and a third access point to an underground car park was added.

**On the western side**, the former stage for musicians **was replaced** by an amphitheatre for concerts. The fountain **in the centre** **was removed** and a new rose garden **was created** in its place, **surrounded by seats on all four sides**. The north-western rose garden **was retained**, **while** the south-western one disappeared.

**In the eastern section**, a café and a children's play area **replaced** the former north-eastern rose garden and pond for water plants. The glasshouse **gave way to** a water feature, **and** a new entrance from an underground car park **was added** close to it.

## 04 / Band 7.5 目标范文

使用过去与现在的布局关系组织信息。

The diagrams compare the arrangement of Grange Park **when it opened in 1920** with the layout shown today.

**Overall**, Grange Park has been **substantially** redesigned to accommodate a wider range of recreational activities. **Although** the two original entrances and one rose garden have been preserved, most of the other facilities **have been replaced**, and an entrance linked to underground parking has been introduced.

In 1920, a fountain occupied the centre of the park, with a musicians' stage immediately to the west. **Today**, the fountain **has been replaced** by a square rose garden, **with seats arranged on all four sides**, **while** the former stage has **given way to** a large amphitheatre for concerts. Of the three original rose gardens, only the one **in the north-western corner** remains.

The eastern side has also **changed** considerably. The former rose garden and pond for water plants **have been replaced** by a café and a children's play area respectively. **Meanwhile**, the glasshouse **has been removed** and a water feature **has been installed** nearby. **Despite these alterations, the entrances on Arnold Avenue and Eldon Street still serve the park**, with an additional access point now provided from an underground car park.

## 05 / 地图题高频表达

- was replaced by / gave way to：设施被另一设施取代。

- was removed / was retained：拆除与保留。

- with + 名词 + 过去分词：with a new rose garden added。

- in the north-western corner / to the east of：清楚的方位描述。

- Despite the changes, both original entrances remained in place：Overview 概括保留部分。
