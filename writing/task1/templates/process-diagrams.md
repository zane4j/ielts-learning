# 流程图｜完整四段式模板、Overview 与句型（Process Diagrams）

[四类模板总目录](README.md) · [Task 1 目录](../README.md) · [Writing 目录](../../README.md) · [首页](../../../README.md)

> **定位：**在原 [Task 1 句型手册](../sentence-handbook.md) 的“步骤顺序 + 被动语态”基础上，结合这次 **6 轮已完成的流程图单句翻译**和正在练习的 Overview，形成可直接套用的完整笔记。下文的塑料、废纸、金属案例为**模拟教学示例**，不是剑雅真实流程图。模板中方括号表示替换内容。

## 1. 流程图先看四件事

**类型 → 起点 → 终点 → 主要阶段/变化。**

- **人造线性过程**（manufacturing / recycling）：一般现在时被动语态占主导，例：is collected / is washed / is processed。
- **自然过程**（water cycle / plant growth）：按主体真实动作使用主动或被动，例：Water evaporates；Seeds are dispersed。
- **循环过程**：Overview 强调 cyclical，步骤顺序应体现返回起点。
- **阶段数**：仅在流程图能明确数清时写 six/seven stages。不能依据自己人为拆句的数量随意编写阶段数。
- **Overview：**概述主要阶段、起点终点、线性/循环及显著操作，不展开全部微观细节。
- **四段建议：**Introduction → Overview → Body 1 → Body 2。四段式是训练策略，不是官方硬性规定。

## 2. 完整四段式模板 A：人造线性过程

### Introduction｜改写题干

The diagram illustrates **how [PRODUCT] is produced** through a series of stages.

或者：The diagram shows **the process of [PROCESS NAME]**.

**示例：**The diagram illustrates how waste plastic bottles are recycled into new products.

### Overview｜最稳定的 1–2 句

Overall, the process **consists of [NUMBER] main stages, beginning with [FIRST STAGE] and ending with [FINAL STAGE]**. The main operations include [TWO OR THREE MAJOR OPERATIONS].

**示例：**Overall, the process consists of six main stages, beginning with the collection of waste bottles and ending with the production of new plastic items. The main operations involve washing, sorting and processing the collected material.

**Overview 六点注意：**
- **beginning with + 名词 / V-ing**：beginning with **the collection of** waste bottles / **collecting** waste bottles。
- **ending with + 名词 / V-ing**：ending with **the production of** new items。
- **consists of six stages**（不能说 is consisted of）。
- “a linear process” 只有在图为非循环时才使用；“a cyclical process” 则必须有返回起点的箭头或明确循环关系。
- 不必为了强行写两句话而重复起点终点，信息不足时一句概括也可以。
- 不将温度、时间、设备、步骤、输出物等**图中没有的信息**当成事实补充。

### Body 1｜前半部分

Initially, [RAW MATERIALS] **are collected and transported to** [PLACE], **where they are** [OPERATION 1]. After being [OPERATION 2], the materials **are then** [OPERATION 3].

**示例：**Initially, waste plastic bottles are collected and transported to a recycling plant, where they are washed. Once cleaned, the bottles are sorted according to their type.

### Body 2｜后半部分

Following this, [MATERIAL] **is/are processed into** [INTERMEDIATE PRODUCT], **which is/are then** [NEXT OPERATION]. Finally, [FINAL PRODUCT] **is/are produced**.

**示例：**Following this, the sorted bottles are broken into small pieces, which are then heated. Finally, the resulting plastic is shaped into new products.

## 3. 模板 B：自然循环过程

**Introduction：**The diagram illustrates the stages involved in [NATURAL PROCESS].

**Overview：**Overall, this is a **cyclical process** in which [MAIN SUBJECT] moves through [KEY TRANSFORMATIONS] before returning to [STARTING STATE].

**Body 1：**Initially, [STAGE A]. As a result, [STAGE B], after which [STAGE C].

**Body 2：**Subsequently, [STAGE D]. Finally, [LAST EVENT] leads back to [STARTING CONDITION], allowing the cycle to repeat.

**自然过程语言示范（概念示例，非配图真题）：**

- Water **evaporates** from lakes and oceans.
- Water vapour **condenses** to form clouds.
- Rainwater **returns** to rivers and lakes, and the cycle begins again.

**注意：**不能把所有自然动作都改成被动语态；Water is evaporated 在自然蒸发场景中通常不如 Water evaporates 自然。

## 4. 六个核心连接句型（来自本次练习）

| 作用 | 句型 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 两步并列 | **is/are + V-ed and then + V-ed** | Waste paper is collected and then transported to a plant. |
| 在被处理之前 | **before being + V-ed** | Bottles are washed before being processed. |
| 在被处理之后 | **After being + V-ed**, ... | After being collected, waste paper is transported to a factory. |
| 上一阶段产物 | **which is/are then + V-ed** | Plastic is cut into pieces, which are then heated. |
| 完成某步骤后 | **Once + S + is/are + V-ed**, ... | Once the plastic is melted, it is poured into moulds. |
| 后续步骤 | **Following this, ...** | Following this, the mixture is cooled and packaged. |

### 另外三种“减少重复”的表达

**地点 + where：**

Waste paper is transported to a factory, **where it is cleaned and processed into pulp**.

**结果 + which：**

Wood is processed into pulp, **which is then fed into a machine**.

**目的 + to：**

The molten material is poured into moulds **to produce new products**.

### 单复数/代词务必对应

- **waste paper / pulp / liquid plastic / material** → **is**（作为材料时通常不可数）。
- **plastic bottles / pieces / pellets / products** → **are**。
- a solid material, **which is then** cut...
- small pieces, **which are then** packed...
- 避免 **it** 跨越多个材料时含糊不清；可写 **the resulting material / the liquid plastic / the pulp**。

### 主动与被动一眼区分

- ✅ **The bottles are washed** before being sorted.（塑料瓶被洗、被分类）
- ✅ **Workers sort the bottles** after washing them.（工人主动洗、分类）
- ❌ The bottles are washed **before sorting**.（句子表面上变成瓶子主动分类）
- ✅ **Once the plastic is melted**, it is poured into moulds.
- ❌ Once the plastic **will be heated**, ...（常规流程通常不使用 will）

## 5. 模拟完整范文（四自然段）

**模拟流程设定：六个主阶段**：①收集塑料瓶 → ②运送到厂 → ③清洗 → ④分类 → ⑤加工（切碎、融化）→ ⑥制成新产品。这里把“切碎、融化”定义为加工阶段中的操作，而非另算阶段；这样 Overview 的 six stages 与题目设定一致。

The diagram illustrates how waste plastic bottles are recycled into new plastic products through a series of stages.

Overall, the process consists of six main stages, beginning with the collection of used bottles and ending with the production of new plastic items. It is a linear process that involves transportation, cleaning, sorting and further processing before the finished products are made.

Initially, used plastic bottles are collected from different locations and transported to a recycling plant. Once they arrive at the factory, the bottles are washed to remove dirt and other unwanted materials. Following this, they are sorted into groups according to their type so that the plastic can be processed more effectively in the next stage.

The sorted bottles are then cut into small pieces, which are heated until the plastic becomes a liquid. After this, the molten material is transferred to moulds and shaped into new plastic products. The final items are allowed to cool before being removed from the moulds, completing the recycling process.

> **示范提示：**正式写作只能写图上确实出现的步骤；此处清洗、分类、融化、模具和冷却来自模拟设定/举例，不可直接挪用到其他真题。

## 6. 本次 6 轮单句训练：真实错误摘录与修正

| 轮次 | 用户实际提交中的片段 | 推荐修改 | 规律 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | “waste plastics ... to recycle factory” | **waste plastic bottles ... to a recycling plant** | 材料名称 + 冠词 + recycling |
| 2 | “to cycling factory ... before sorting” | **to a recycling plant ... before being sorted** | before + 被动 |
| 3 | “small pieces, which are then fed into a machine” | **原句正确** | which 指向 pieces，使用 are |
| 4 | “melted to liquid, it can be poured” | **melted into a liquid, the liquid plastic is then poured** | into + 清楚指代 |
| 5 | “solid materials, which is ... smaller trunks ... finally be packed” | **a solid material, which is ... small pieces ... finally packaged** | 单复数与并列谓语 |
| 6 | “waste papers ... subsequently processing into liquid, which are sent” | **waste paper ... subsequently processed into pulp, which is then fed** | 不可数 + V-ed + is |

这些片段来自本次对话，作为错题复盘；没有代替或修改你之前提交过的完整英文原句。

### 复习优先级

1. **被动结构：**is/are + V-ed；before/after **being** + V-ed。
2. **关系从句：**which 实际指代上一个材料或产物；单数 is，复数 are。
3. **材料词汇：**paper、pulp、liquid plastic 不可数；pieces / bottles 可数。
4. **Overview：**只写阶段数量、起终点和整体特征；不要写一串微观步骤。
5. **准确性：**真正考试以图为准，不增加图中没有的机械加工、温度或包装步骤。

## 7. 考前 30 秒检查

- [ ] 流程图是线性还是循环？阶段数是否有根据？
- [ ] Overview 概括了起点、终点及主要特点，而不是逐个描述？
- [ ] 人造步骤通常 is/are + V-ed；自然过程是否合理使用主动语态？
- [ ] before / after **being + V-ed** 是否正确？
- [ ] which 后的 **is / are** 是否与指代名词一致？
- [ ] pulp / paper / material 不可数，bottles / pieces 复数？
- [ ] 时间、温度、步骤、设备是否全部确实来自题图？
