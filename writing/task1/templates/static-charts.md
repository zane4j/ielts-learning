# 静态图｜完整四段式模板与句型（Static Charts）

[四类模板总目录](README.md) · [Task 1 目录](../README.md) · [Writing 目录](../../README.md) · [首页](../../../README.md)

> **定位：**由原 [Task 1 句型手册](../sentence-handbook.md) 的排名、占比、倍数和差距内容扩展。适用于**同一时间点**的柱状图、饼图、表格或多组横向比较。下述数值均为**模拟数据**，不代表用户原文或雅思真题。

## 1. 先判断：静态图要写什么？

**核心 = Highest / Lowest（最高最低）+ Ranking（排序）+ Gaps（差距）+ Similarities（相似性）。**

- **单一时间点**：不使用 rose / declined / increased over time，除非图中确有多个时间点。
- **两个群体**：优先按群体或按高低类别分组，避免逐行罗列。
- **动态图/静态图判别重点是是否跨时间比较**，不是图形长得像折线还是柱状。

## 2. 完整四段式模板（目标 Band 6.5–7）

### Introduction｜改写对象、年份和单位

The [bar chart / pie charts / table] compares [WHAT] across [GROUPS] in [YEAR], measured in [UNIT].

**例句：**The two pie charts compare how household spending was distributed across five categories in two cities in 2020.

### Overview｜最重要的两点

Overall, [CATEGORY A] accounted for the largest share in [GROUP A / BOTH GROUPS], whereas [CATEGORY B] represented the smallest proportion. The most noticeable difference between the groups was in [CATEGORY C].

**例句：**Overall, housing made up the largest share of expenditure in both cities, while other expenses accounted for the smallest proportion. The clearest differences were in food and transport.

### Body 1｜大类 + 高占比 + 关键比较

In [GROUP A], [CATEGORY A] accounted for [X%], followed by [CATEGORY B] at [Y%]. By comparison, the corresponding figures in [GROUP B] were [P%] and [Q%].

**例句：**In City A, housing accounted for 35% of spending, followed by food at 25%. The corresponding figures for City B were 30% and 20%.

### Body 2｜其余类别 + 差距 + 例外

Regarding the remaining categories, [A] stood at [X%] in [GROUP A], compared with [Y%] in [GROUP B]. [B] was [higher / lower], while [C] showed only a relatively small difference.

**例句：**Transport represented 20% of expenditure in City A, compared with 25% in City B. Leisure and other expenses were also slightly higher in City B.

## 3. 必背表达（按功能）

| 功能 | 母句 | 注意 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占比 | A **accounted for 40% of** the total. | accounted for，不能 accounted from |
| 最大占比 | A **made up the largest share**, at 40%. | 后面带数据 |
| 最小占比 | B **represented the smallest proportion**, at 8%. | 不用最极端的夸张词 |
| 排第二 | C **ranked second**, at 30%. | 排名有根据 |
| 两项相等 | A and B **accounted for equal shares**, at 20% each. | each 指每一项 |
| 大约两倍 | A was **approximately twice as high as** B. | 比较数值而非不同实体 |
| 数值相比 | **The figure for A was twice that for B**. | 明确比较对象 |
| 高出 10 个百分点 | A was **10 percentage points higher than** B. | 30% vs 20% |
| 最大差距 | The **largest gap** was observed in [CATEGORY]. | 没有时间变化也可用 was |
| 相近 | The figures for A and B were **similar**. | 不能完全相等时别写 identical |
| 两项分别 | A and B stood at X and Y, **respectively**. | 对应顺序清晰 |

### 排序类句型组合

- A accounted for the highest proportion, **closely followed by** B.
- C ranked second, **whereas** D had the smallest share.
- The figure for City A was higher **than that for** City B.

## 4. 模拟完整范文（四自然段）

**题目设定（模拟）：**2020 年两座城市家庭消费构成，Housing / Food / Transport / Leisure / Other：City A 为 35 / 25 / 20 / 12 / 8%；City B 为 30 / 20 / 25 / 15 / 10%。两组各自合计 100%。

The two pie charts compare how households in City A and City B allocated their spending among five categories in 2020.

Overall, housing accounted for the largest proportion of expenditure in both cities, whereas other expenses represented the smallest share. The two cities had fairly similar spending patterns, although City A allocated more money to housing and food, while City B spent higher proportions on transport and leisure.

In City A, housing was the dominant category, accounting for 35% of total spending. Food ranked second, at 25%, followed by transport at 20%. Together, these three categories made up 80% of household expenditure. The remaining 20% was divided between leisure, at 12%, and other expenses, at 8%.

In City B, housing still had the highest share, but at a slightly lower 30%. Transport came next at 25%, which was five percentage points higher than in City A. Food made up 20%, five points below the figure for City A. Leisure and other expenses accounted for 15% and 10% respectively, both slightly higher than their counterparts in City A.

## 5. 静态图特有错误

- ❌ The proportion **rose** to 35% in 2020.（只有 2020 一个时间点，不能断言 rose）
- ✅ The proportion **stood at** 35% in 2020.
- ❌ A accounted **from** 20% to 30%.
- ✅ A **accounted for** 30% of the total.
- ❌ A was twice **that of** B.（比较对象不一致）
- ✅ The figure for A was twice **that for** B.
- ✅ 20% vs 30%：**10 percentage points**，而不是差距为 10%。

## 6. 考前 30 秒检查

- [ ] 找出最大、最小、第二和最明显差距。
- [ ] 把类别按“主要 / 次要”或“群体 A / B”分组。
- [ ] 全文不存在没有数据支持的时间趋势。
- [ ] 百分比合计和单位正确，分别比较同类指标。
- [ ] Overview 有总体特征，Body 有数字证据。
