# 地图题｜完整四段式模板与句型（Maps）

[四类模板总目录](README.md) · [Task 1 目录](../README.md) · [Writing 目录](../../README.md) · [首页](../../../README.md)

> **定位：**从原 [Task 1 句型手册](../sentence-handbook.md) 和已完成的 [地图题 10 轮翻译复盘](../map-writing-practice-and-templates-2026-11-23.md) 中提炼稳定框架。这里的完整范文使用**明确标注的模拟公园地图**，不是 Cambridge 真题原图。地图分成**过去 → 现在**与**现在 → 未来规划**时，时态不能机械套用同一模板。

## 1. 地图题核心 = 方位 + 设施变化 + 总体特征

先读四组信息：

- **拆除：**be removed / be demolished / disappear
- **替换、改用途：**be replaced by / be converted into / give way to
- **新增、扩建：**be built / be added / be expanded
- **保留：**remain unchanged / remain in the same location / be retained

描述顺序推荐两种：**按过去和现在分组**，或**按东西、南北区域分组**。根据地图信息选择，不必同时用两种方法。

## 2. 完整模板 A：过去 vs 现在

### Introduction

The two maps compare the layout of [PLACE] [TIME AGO] with its present-day layout.

**例句：**The two maps compare the layout of a public library 20 years ago with its present-day layout.

### Overview

Overall, the area **has undergone significant changes**, with [MAIN CHANGE] and [NEW FACILITIES]. However, [UNCHANGED FEATURE] **has remained unchanged**.

**例句：**Overall, the park has undergone major redevelopment, with several original facilities replaced. However, the main entrance and central path have remained unchanged.

### Body 1｜过去布局（一般过去时）

Previously, [FEATURE] was located in the [POSITION] of [PLACE], while [FEATURE] occupied the [OTHER POSITION]. [CENTRAL FEATURE] stood in the centre.

**例句：**In 2000, a fountain stood in the northern part of the park, while a car park occupied the eastern side.

### Body 2｜现在变化（现在完成时 / 现在时）

Today, [OLD A] **has been replaced by** [NEW A], while [OLD B] **has been converted into** [NEW B]. In addition, [NEW C] **has been added** to the [POSITION].

**例句：**Today, the former car park has been replaced by a café, while the playground on the western side has been expanded.

## 3. 完整模板 B：按区域分组（Body 1 西、Body 2 东）

- **Introduction：**The maps illustrate how [PLACE] changed between [YEAR A] and [YEAR B].
- **Overview：**Overall, [MAJOR REDEVELOPMENT]. The [UNCHANGED FEATURES], however, were retained.
- **Body 1：**On the western side, [OLD A] was replaced by [NEW A], while [OLD B] was expanded.
- **Body 2：**In the eastern section, [OLD C] gave way to [NEW C], with [ADDITIONAL FEATURE] added nearby.

**适用条件：**各区域既有旧图又有新图的对照，且可以清楚分组；过去的描述使用过去时，现状可以使用现在时或现在完成时。

## 4. 完整模板 C：现在 vs 未来规划（如 Norbiton）

**Introduction**

The maps illustrate the current layout of [PLACE] and its proposed future development.

**Overview**

Overall, the area **is set to undergo significant redevelopment**, with [CURRENT USE] replaced by [PLANNED USE] and [NEW FACILITIES] added. However, [FEATURE] **will remain unchanged**.

**Body 1｜现状（一般现在时）**

Currently, [FEATURES] **are situated** [POSITIONS]. A road **runs** from [PLACE A] to [PLACE B].

**Body 2｜规划（将来时 / 规划表达）**

Under the proposed plan, [OLD FEATURE] **will be replaced by** [NEW FEATURE], while [ROAD] **will be extended** towards [DIRECTION]. A new [FACILITY] **is planned to be built** near [LANDMARK].

**易混点：**规划尚未实施时不能写 “has been replaced”。地图题并非固定只用现在完成时，必须根据题目时间决定：
- Past：was located / stood / used to be / was replaced（描述某已发生的变化）。
- Present result：has been converted / now contains / is located。
- Planned future：will be built / is planned to be constructed / is set to change。

## 5. 高分性价比表达

| 功能 | 推荐句型 |
| --- | --- |
| 重大变化 | The area **has undergone significant changes**. |
| 改造成 | The old library **has been converted into** a museum. |
| 被替代 | The former car park **has been replaced by** a garden. |
| 拆除 | The old fountain **has been removed**. |
| 扩建 | The playground **has been expanded**. |
| 新增 | A new café **has been added** near the entrance. |
| 入口保留 | The main entrance **has remained in the same location**. |
| 两个动作并列 | The park has been redesigned, **with a café added and the fountain removed**. |
| 原址新建 | The old pool has been removed, **with a sports hall built in its place**. |
| 目的 | The building was demolished **to make way for** a new car park. |
| 方位（角） | **in the north-eastern corner** of the park |
| 方位（侧） | **on the western side** of the park |
| 相对位置 | **to the east of** the entrance |
| 连接两区域 | A has been expanded, **whereas** B has remained unchanged. |

**两类语法的差别：**

- 完整句：The room **has been converted** into a computer room.
- with 结构：The library was redesigned, **with the room converted** into a computer room.
- ❌ with the room has been converted / ❌ the western of park / ❌ the centre of library。

## 6. 模拟完整范文：公园改造（四自然段）

**模拟地图设定：**2000 年公园北侧有 fountain，东侧有 car park，西侧有 children's playground，南侧 main entrance，中央有一条 path。2020 年：北侧 fountain → flower garden，东侧 car park → café，西侧 playground expanded 并新增 benches，入口和中央 path 不变。以下为**模拟训练题**，不是 Cambridge 试卷。

The two maps compare the layout of a public park in 2000 with its layout in 2020.

Overall, the park underwent substantial redevelopment, with its former fountain and car park replaced by facilities for visitors. The children's playground was also expanded, whereas the main entrance and central path remained in their original positions.

In 2000, a fountain occupied the northern section of the park, and a car park stood on the eastern side. A relatively small children's playground was located in the west. Visitors could enter the park through the main entrance in the south and follow a path through the central area to reach the other facilities.

By 2020, the fountain had been replaced by a flower garden, while a new café had taken the place of the eastern car park. The playground on the western side had been enlarged, with several benches added nearby. Despite these changes, the entrance on the southern side and the path running through the centre of the park were retained.

## 7. 常见错误（来自此前地图题训练）

| 错误 | 正确写法 | 检查重点 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the western of the library | **the western side of the library** | western 为形容词 |
| on the north-western of the park | **in the north-western corner of the park** | corner/side |
| in the northern of park | **on the northern side of the park** | 冠词 + 名词 |
| with a reading room has been converted | **with a reading room converted** | with + noun + V-ed |
| has converted into | **has been converted into** | 完整句被动 |
| main entrance 被描述成电脑室 | **reading room** 被转换 | 设施对象不能写反 |

## 8. 考前 30 秒检查

- [ ] 东西南北及角落位置是否读对？
- [ ] 旧设施 A → 新设施 B 是否准确，是否漏掉重要变化？
- [ ] Overview 是否有“最大变化 + 保留/例外”？
- [ ] 过去、现在、未来规划时态是否匹配？
- [ ] with 后没有多余 has been？
- [ ] the entrance / the park / children's 等细节正确？
