# 动态图｜完整四段式模板与句型（Dynamic Charts）

[四类模板总目录](README.md) · [Task 1 目录](../README.md) · [Writing 目录](../../README.md) · [首页](../../../README.md)

> **定位：**基于原 [Task 1 句型手册](../sentence-handbook.md) 的趋势、转折、比较、数据表达部分，扩展为可独立练习的四段式模板。折线图、跨年份柱状图、跨年份表格及饼图均可能属于动态图。下述图表数据为**模拟练习**，不是剑雅真题或用户原稿。模板中方括号是可替换内容，正式写作时须移除。

## 1. 先判断题型

- **同一指标在多个年份变化**：重点是趋势、起终点、最高/最低、交叉和排名变化。
- **只有两个年份**：仍可按动态图写，但不必强行使用 fluctuate / peak / plateau 之类需要多个观测点的词。
- **不同单位**：不能直接比较绝对值与百分比；只比较支持的趋势和指标。
- **四段建议**：Introduction → Overview → Body 1（第一组或前期）→ Body 2（第二组或后期）。不是官方规定段数。

## 2. 完整四段式模板（目标 Band 6.5–7）

### Introduction｜改写图题，不分析

The [line graph / bar chart / table] compares [WHAT] in [PLACE] between [YEAR A] and [YEAR B], measured in [UNIT].

**例句：**The line graph compares the proportions of commuters using cars, buses and trains in a city between 2000 and 2020.

### Overview｜用 1–2 句说最明显的变化

Overall, [CATEGORY A] [rose / fell] over the period, whereas [CATEGORY B] followed the opposite trend. By [END YEAR], [MAIN RANKING OR CHANGE].

**例句：**Overall, car use declined considerably, while bus use increased and became the most common option by the end of the period.

**使用规则：**通常不需要逐个罗列数值；如果图表的总体特点不是“相反趋势”，必须换成实际观察到的变化，不要硬套。

### Body 1｜第一组趋势 + 具体数字

In [START YEAR], [A] stood at [X], compared with [Y] for [B]. Over the following [PERIOD], [A] [rose / fell] to [Z], while [B] [increased / declined] to [W].

**例句：**In 2000, cars accounted for 60% of journeys, compared with 20% for buses. By 2010, the car figure had fallen to 55%, while the proportion for buses had risen to 25%.

### Body 2｜第二组趋势 + 终点 / 排名 / 比较

By [END YEAR], [A] had [fallen / risen] to [X], whereas [B] reached [Y]. Meanwhile, [C] [CHANGE], leaving [CONCLUDING RELATIONSHIP].

**例句：**By 2020, the proportion for cars had fallen to 35%, whereas the bus figure had climbed to 40%. Train use also increased slightly, reaching 25%.

## 3. 高频句型表

| 功能 | 母句 | 注意 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 从 X 到 Y | The figure rose **from X to Y** between A and B. | X、Y 必须对应 |
| 增加 X 个百分点 | The share increased **by X percentage points**. | 10% → 23% 是 +13 个百分点 |
| 连续下降 | The figure **declined steadily** over the period. | 仅在数据支持时用 steadily |
| 峰值 | A **reached a peak of X** in [YEAR]. | 不要对只有两点的数据乱用 peaked |
| 先升后降 | A rose to X **before falling to Y**. | before + V-ing |
| 随后变化 | A peaked in 2010, **after which** it declined. | which 指向此前时间/事件 |
| 对比趋势 | A increased, **whereas** B declined. | 比较同一维度 |
| 达到相同水平 | A **reached the same level as** B. | same level as |
| 两倍 | The figure for A was **twice that for** B. | 保持比较对象一致 |
| 近似值 | A stood at **approximately X**. | 有读图依据才估算 |
| 小幅变化 | A **edged up / fell slightly**. | 不要把大涨写成 slightly |
| 占比 | A **accounted for / made up X%**. | 不说 accounted from |

### 动词 + 程度副词（少量够用）

- 上升：increase / rise / grow；大幅上升：rise sharply / increase significantly。
- 下降：fall / decline / drop；略微下降：fall slightly。
- 持平：remain stable / remain unchanged；波动：fluctuate。
- 平稳变化：rise steadily / decline gradually。**程度必须与图一致。**

## 4. 模拟完整范文（四自然段）

**题目设定（模拟）：**某城市居民通勤方式占比，2000 / 2010 / 2020。Car 60% / 55% / 35%；Bus 20% / 25% / 40%；Train 20% / 20% / 25%。每年合计 100%。

The line graph compares the percentages of commuters travelling by car, bus and train in a city in 2000, 2010 and 2020.

Overall, the proportion of people using cars declined substantially, whereas bus use increased throughout the period. By 2020, buses had overtaken cars to become the most popular form of transport. Train use changed comparatively little.

In 2000, car travel accounted for 60% of all journeys, which was three times the figure for either buses or trains, at 20% each. Over the following decade, the proportion of car users dropped slightly to 55%, while the figure for buses rose by five percentage points to 25%. Train use, meanwhile, remained unchanged at 20%.

By 2020, car use had fallen further to 35%, representing a decline of 25 percentage points from its initial level. In contrast, the proportion travelling by bus had climbed to 40%, making it the largest category. The figure for trains also increased, although more modestly, to 25%.

## 5. 考前 30 秒检查

- [ ] Introduction 写对了对象、单位、时间。
- [ ] Overview 点出主要增减、最高最低或排名变化。
- [ ] Body 各组趋势有具体数据，不按年份机械列流水账。
- [ ] 区分 **increase to X** 与 **increase by X**。
- [ ] 百分比差值写 **percentage points**。
- [ ] A 和 B 比较的是相同单位与相同对象。
