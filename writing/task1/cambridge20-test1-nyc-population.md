## 04 / Band 7.5 目标范文

重新编写的学习目标范文，非官方评分结果。

The tables provide information on the population of New York City and the distribution of its residents between Manhattan and the other four boroughs **in 1800, 1900 and 2000**.

**Overall**, the city's population **expanded** enormously over the two centuries, **reaching more than 100 times its 1800 level**. Manhattan initially housed the vast majority of residents, **whereas** by 2000 the population was concentrated in the other boroughs.

In 1800, the city had **79,216 inhabitants**, including **60,515 in Manhattan**. The borough therefore represented **about 76% of the total**, **compared with** just 24% for the other four areas combined. **By 1900**, the overall population had **surged** to approximately **3.44 million**. Manhattan itself contained about 1.85 million people, yet its proportion had **fallen** to roughly 54%.

The city continued to **grow**, reaching **8,009,185 residents in 2000**. **However**, Manhattan's population was down from its 1900 figure to **approximately 1.54 million**, **reducing its share to only 19%**. **Meanwhile**, the other boroughs collectively accounted for **around 81% of the city population**, a marked reversal of the situation in 1800.
