## 04 / 修改后的完整文章

重新组织数据，目标 Band 6.5+；不在作文内部插入段落标题。

The bar chart compares the number of US households across five annual income brackets **in 2007, 2011 and 2015**.

**Overall**, the four income groups below $100,000 **maintained the same relative order** across the three years, **while** the highest-income category changed position. Households earning $100,000 or more formed the largest group **in 2007 and 2015** but ranked third in 2011. **The $75,000–$99,999 category remained the smallest throughout**.

Among the lower-income brackets, the number of households earning $25,000–$49,999 **rose** **from approximately 27 million in 2007 to nearly 30 million in 2011**, **before** **slipping** to about 28.5 million in 2015. A similar pattern occurred in the under-$25,000 group, **which increased from roughly 25 million to 28.5 million and then edged down to about 28 million**. **By contrast**, the figures for the $50,000–$74,999 and $75,000–$99,999 brackets **changed little**, at **around 21 million and 14–15 million respectively**.

**Turning to** the highest-income group, **approximately 29.5 million households** earned at least $100,000 in 2007, **making this the largest category**. Its figure then **fell** **slightly** to **around 27.5 million in 2011**, **placing it behind both lower-income groups**, **before** **recovering** **markedly** to **about 33 million in 2015**.

## 05 / Band 7.5 目标范文

新增精读参考稿，按主要趋势进行自然分组。

The bar chart illustrates how many households in the United States fell into five annual income categories **in 2007, 2011 and 2015**. Figures are given in millions.

**Overall**, households earning at least $100,000 constituted the largest group at the beginning and end of the period, **whereas** the $75,000–$99,999 bracket consistently contained the fewest households. The two lowest-income categories **increased** between 2007 and 2011, **before** **declining** slightly in 2015, **while the highest-income group followed the opposite pattern**.

In 2007, **about 29.5 million households** earned $100,000 or more, ahead of the $25,000–$49,999 and under-$25,000 groups, at roughly 27 million and 25 million respectively. **By 2011**, the latter two groups had **risen** to **around 29.5 million and 28.5 million**, **overtaking the top-income category, which had fallen to approximately 27.5 million**. **In 2015**, however, the highest-income group **rebounded** to **about 33 million**, regaining first place.

**By comparison**, the two middle brackets showed little overall change. Around **21 million households** earned $50,000–$74,999 in each year, **while** the figure for those on $75,000–$99,999 **fluctuated** **slightly** **between 14 million and 15 million**. These groups remained smaller than the two lowest-income brackets throughout.

## 06 / 错误与句型

### 高频错误

- char → chart；thoughout → throughout；agian → again。

- stood in 25 to 28 → ranged from 25 to 28。

- became the third → dropped to third place。

- 高频错误数据：$50,000–$74,999 并非约 26m，而是约 21m。

### 可复用表达

- A ranked first in 2007 before dropping to third place.

- The figure remained relatively stable at around X million.

- A increased from X to Y before edging down.

- With the latter overtaking the former ...
