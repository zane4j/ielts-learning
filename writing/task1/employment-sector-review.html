01

Introduction

Original / Minimal correction
你的原文 Original

The bar chart compares the percentages of employees in five different sectors in a country in 2000 and 2020.

修改后 Yellow = changed

The bar chart compares the percentages of people employed in five different sectors in a country in 2000 and 2020.

修改说明
  • 术语准确性：percentages of employees 可以理解；percentages of people employed 更贴合就业人口占比。
  • 本段策略：Introduction 只改写题目，不加入趋势或数据分析。
02

Overview

Original / Minimal correction
你的原文 Original

Overall, the percentages of employees in both technology and services industry had significant increases, whereas those in others showed different levels of decreases. The percentages of employees in agriculture declined with largest percentages and became the lowest one in 2020, meanwhile, the figure for technology had a largest improvement so that it became the second largest industry in 2020.

修改后 Yellow = changed

Overall, the proportions of employees working in technology and services increased significantly, whereas those in the other three sectors decreased. Agriculture experienced the largest decline and became the smallest sector in 2020, while technology recorded the biggest increase and rose to second place.

修改说明
  • 趋势搭配：had significant increases → increased significantly；different levels of decreases → decreased by varying amounts。
  • 主谓与冠词：both technology and services industry 不自然；a largest improvement 应改为 the biggest/largest increase。
  • 句子连接：不能用 “..., meanwhile, ...” 把两个独立句子直接拼接；这里改用 while。
  • 信息选择：农业下降 12 个百分点，科技上升 13 个百分点。还可以比较服务业与制造业的排名互换。
03

Body 1

Original / Minimal correction
你的原文 Original

In 2000, the percentages of employees in both technology and services stood at 10% and 27%, they rose to 23% and 38% in 2020, showing the obvious increase. During the same period, the figures for agriculture, manufacturing, construction declined from 20%, 28%, 15% to 8%, 18%, 13%, the gaps are in different levels.

修改后 Yellow = changed

In 2000, the figures for technology and services stood at 10% and 27%, respectively, before rising to 23% and 38% in 2020. Over the same period, the proportions employed in agriculture, manufacturing and construction declined from 20%, 28% and 15% to 8%, 18% and 13%, respectively.

修改说明
  • 逗号拼接句：10% and 27%, they rose... 是两个独立分句，用 before rising to... 连接。
  • 对应数字：三个类别与三组数字一一对应，末尾用 respectively。
  • 避免空泛句：the gaps are in different levels 信息不清，删除；已有具体数值足够。
04

Body 2

Original / Minimal correction
你的原文 Original

Regarding to agriculture and technology, the decrease percentages for agriculture was 12%, the largest one among the 3 industry which were declined, so that it became the last industry among all. However, the technology showed the largest increase by 13% from a low level and became the second largest industry.

修改后 Yellow = changed

Looking more closely at agriculture and technology, the former saw a decrease of 12 percentage points, the largest fall among the three declining sectors, dropping from third to fifth place. By contrast, technology experienced the largest increase, at 13 percentage points, moving from the smallest sector in 2000 to the second-largest in 2020.

修改说明
  • 介词与数词：Regarding to → Regarding / Looking more closely at；3 industry → three sectors。
  • 百分比 vs 百分点：20% → 8% 是下降 12 percentage points；10% → 23% 是上升 13 percentage points。
  • 动词用法：decline 是不及物动词，这里不写 which were declined；改为 declining sectors。
  • 结构优化：dropping from...、moving from... 用分词补充排名变化，更凝练。

03 / 修改后的完整文章

尽量保留原意，四自然段，五色背景精读。

逻辑连接趋势词程度副词数据表达复用句式

The bar chart compares the percentages of people employed in five different sectors in a country in 2000 and 2020.

Overall, the proportions of employees working in technology and services increased significantly, whereas those in the other three sectors decreased. Agriculture experienced the largest decline and became the smallest sector in 2020, while technology recorded the biggest increase and rose to second place.

In 2000, the figures for technology and services stood at 10% and 27%, respectively, before rising to 23% and 38% in 2020. Over the same period, the proportions employed in agriculture, manufacturing and construction fell from 20%, 28% and 15% to 8%, 18% and 13%, respectively.

Looking more closely at agriculture and technology, the former saw a decrease of 12 percentage points, the largest fall among the three declining sectors, dropping from third to fifth place. By contrast, technology experienced the largest increase, at 13 percentage points, moving from the smallest sector in 2000 to the second-largest in 2020.

保留原文分组逻辑，因此 Body 2 仍有部分对已描述变化的重复；重新估分约 6.0。

04 / Band 7.5 目标范文

先整体概括就业结构转移，再分组描述具体数据。

逻辑连接趋势词程度副词数据表达复用句式

The bar chart compares the proportions of the workforce employed in five sectors in a country in 2000 and 2020.

Overall, employment shifted towards technology and services, while the shares of the other three sectors declined. Services overtook manufacturing to become the largest employer, and technology recorded the biggest increase. Agriculture, meanwhile, experienced the sharpest fall and became the smallest sector.

In 2000, manufacturing accounted for the highest proportion of workers, at 28%, closely followed by services at 27%. Agriculture represented 20%, while construction and technology made up 15% and 10%, respectively. By 2020, the share employed in services had risen to 38%, overtaking manufacturing, whereas technology more than doubled its share to 23%, moving from last to second place.

By contrast, the proportion working in agriculture fell from 20% to 8%, a decrease of 12 percentage points. Manufacturing also saw a marked decline, dropping by 10 percentage points to 18%. Construction experienced the smallest change, with its share slipping only slightly from 15% to 13%.

Band 7.5 为学习目标，不是官方评分结果。

05 / 错误清单与必背句型

适合下次写作前快速复习。

高频错误

  • had significant increases → increased significantly
  • a largest improvement → the largest increase
  • Regarding to → Regarding / With regard to
  • which were declined → sectors that declined
  • 10% → 23%：上升 13 percentage points，不是 13%
  • A rose to X, B fell to Y → 用 whereas/while 连接

高频句型

  • Employment shifted from A towards B.
  • A accounted for the largest proportion, at X%.
  • A overtook B to become the largest sector.
  • The figure more than doubled from X to Y.
  • A rose, whereas B declined.
  • With its share slipping from X to Y, A recorded the smallest change.
