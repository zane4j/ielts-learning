> **母模板：Two-Part。必背：**四段功能与主干；**选背：**适用的让步/比较变体；**了解：**链接范文的题目词汇。下面是待填充骨架，不是260词成稿。[ ] 内必须填实质内容，正式考试删除标记。

## 四段连续模板

[PARAPHRASE OF THE ISSUE]. [DIRECT SHORT ANSWER TO Q1], and [DIRECT SHORT ANSWER TO Q2].

Regarding [QUESTION 1 TOPIC], [MAIN ANSWER TO Q1]. This is because [EXPLANATION]. When [CONDITION], [MECHANISM]. For example, [CASE]. Therefore, [DIRECT LINK TO Q1].

Turning to [QUESTION 2 TOPIC], [MAIN ANSWER TO Q2]. By [ACTION], [GROUP] can [OUTCOME]. For instance, [CASE]. However, [RELEVANT LIMITATION IF NEEDED]. Therefore, [DIRECT LINK TO Q2].

In conclusion, [BRIEF ANSWER TO Q1], while [BRIEF ANSWER TO Q2].

## 问法、替换与例句

两个直接问题，一问一段。不能默认第一问永远是原因，也不能默认第二问永远是解决方案。若问个人判断，必须在引言与结论明确回答。

| 问法 | 主体起句替换 |
| --- | --- |
| Why...? | One major reason for [ISSUE] is [CAUSE]. |
| What effects on studies...? | One important effect on [STUDIES] is [EFFECT]. |
| Who should be responsible...? | [ACTOR] should take primary responsibility because [REASON]. |
| Is it beneficial...? | I believe this is beneficial because [REASON]. |

**短套用示例：**Regarding [the effects on students' studies], [work experience can improve time management]. For example, [meeting work deadlines may help students plan university assignments].

**常见错误：**题目只问对 studies 的影响，却写所有旅游好处；第一问写太长，第二问只有一句；两问都机械套 agree。

## 字数与展开

练习目标 **260–290词**：Introduction 约45、Body 1 约95、Body 2 约100、Conclusion 约30，共约270。用背景/机制解释观点，再用具体例子说明结果；不要靠重复题目或模板连接词补足词数。

**通用展开：Point → Context → Action / Mechanism → Effect / Fact → Link back。**需要例子时自然插入，不要求每项各占一句。两个主体段各自完成一个任务。
