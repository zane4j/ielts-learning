## 必背｜先记功能，再记少量母句

| 功能 | 母句 | 使用要求 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facts 立场 | It is often argued that [STATEMENT]. I largely agree/disagree with this view because [REASONS]. | 判断命题，回应限定词 |
| Effects 评价 | Although [LIMITED OPPOSITE EFFECT], I believe [X] is largely positive/negative because [MAIN EFFECTS]. | 评价行为/趋势的结果 |
| Two-Part 两问 | [SHORT ANSWER TO Q1], and [SHORT ANSWER TO Q2]. | 主体一问一段 |
| 中心论点 | One major reason/benefit/problem is that [POINT]. | 选对功能词 |
| 解释背景 | This is because [MECHANISM]. | 后面完整句 |
| 行动机制 | By [V-ing], [ACTOR] can [RESULT]. | 动作执行者与主语一致 |
| 条件机制 | When [CONDITION], [RESULT]. | 因果合理，不绝对化 |
| 例子 | For example, [SPECIFIC CASE]. | 不编造研究或精确统计 |
| 结果 | As a result, [EFFECT]. | 不重复中心句 |
| 回扣 | Therefore, [DIRECT ANSWER TO THE QUESTION]. | 回扣题目而非泛泛说有益 |
| 结论 | In conclusion, [POSITION OR ANSWERS] because [BRIEF SUMMARY]. | 不加新论点 |

## 选背｜四个可选模块

1. **让步拉回：**Admittedly, [OPPOSITE VIEW] has some merit because [REASON]. However, this does not necessarily mean that [OPPOSITE CONCLUSION]. Therefore, [MAIN POSITION].
2. **轻重比较：**This benefit is more significant than [DRAWBACK] because [CONCRETE CRITERION].
3. **双边讨论：**On the one hand, [WHY A IS REASONABLE]. On the other hand, [WHY B IS REASONABLE]. 双方各有完整逻辑链。
4. **强限定词：**Although [X] can [BENEFIT], this does not mean it is the only/best way to [GOAL]. [ALTERNATIVE] can also [RESULT]. 支持 most important 时也要说明优先级。

## 短例子｜同一骨架迁移

By [increasing bus frequency], [transport authorities] can [make public transport more convenient]. As a result, [fewer commuters may choose to drive].

By [providing training at work], [employers] can [help staff develop practical skills]. As a result, [employees may complete unfamiliar tasks more effectively].

**了解：**不必为了词汇变化背十种 This is because；先能解释完整因果。避免 it is obvious that / every coin has two sides 等不能提供题目内容的句子。
