> **母模板：Two-Part。必背：**四段功能与主干；**选背：**适用的让步/比较变体；**了解：**链接范文的题目词汇。下面是待填充骨架，不是260词成稿。[ ] 内必须填实质内容，正式考试删除标记。

## 四段连续模板

[PROBLEM] is a growing concern in [CONTEXT]. It is mainly caused by [CAUSE 1] and [CAUSE 2], and [SOLUTION 1] and [SOLUTION 2] could help address it.

One major cause of [PROBLEM] is [CAUSE 1]. This is because [BACKGROUND]. When [MECHANISM], [PROBLEM BECOMES MORE LIKELY]. For example, [CASE]. Another contributing factor is [CAUSE 2], which [EXPLANATION OF ITS EFFECT].

One effective way to address this issue is to [SOLUTION 1]. By [CONCRETE ACTION], [RESPONSIBLE GROUP] can [CHANGE], reducing [PROBLEM CAUSED BY CAUSE 1]. In addition, [SOLUTION 2] could tackle [CAUSE 2] by [MECHANISM]. As a result, [EXPECTED IMPROVEMENT].

In conclusion, [PROBLEM] is largely caused by [CAUSE 1] and [CAUSE 2]. A combination of [SOLUTION 1] and [SOLUTION 2] is a practical response.

## 问法、替换与例句

先圈出确切问句：causes 是成因；problems 是后果；effects 是影响。题目问复数，优先覆盖两个可展开要点，每个措施对准前段原因/问题。

### Problems + Solutions｜完整四段替换版

[TREND] has created difficulties in [CONTEXT]. The main problems are [PROBLEM 1] and [PROBLEM 2], which could be addressed through [MEASURES].

One serious problem caused by [TREND] is [PROBLEM 1]. When [MECHANISM], [HARM]. For example, [CASE]. Another difficulty is [PROBLEM 2], which [SECOND MECHANISM AND EFFECT].

To address the first problem, [RESPONSIBLE GROUP] should [ACTION 1]. This would [HOW IT REDUCES PROBLEM 1]. In addition, [ACTION 2] could help tackle [PROBLEM 2] by [MECHANISM]. Together, these measures could [RESULT].

In conclusion, [TREND] can lead to [PROBLEMS]. [MEASURES] could help limit these effects.

**Causes + Effects：**保留原因段，Body 2 改为 One major consequence is [EFFECT 1]. [EXPLANATION AND EXAMPLE]. A further effect is [EFFECT 2]. [MECHANISM]. 引言与结论也改为原因+影响，不写未问的措施。

**短套用示例：**By [increasing bus frequency and keeping fares affordable], [transport authorities] can [encourage commuters to leave their cars at home], reducing [rush-hour traffic].

**常见错误：**问拥堵造成哪些问题却回答为什么堵；措施只说 government should take action，没说谁做什么、如何起效。

## 字数与展开

练习目标 **260–290词**：Introduction 约45、Body 1 约95、Body 2 约100、Conclusion 约30，共约270。用背景/机制解释观点，再用具体例子说明结果；不要靠重复题目或模板连接词补足词数。

**通用展开：Point → Context → Action / Mechanism → Effect / Fact → Link back。**需要例子时自然插入，不要求每项各占一句。两个主体段各自完成一个任务。
