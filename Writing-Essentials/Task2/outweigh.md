> **母模板：Effects + Comparison。必背：** 四段功能与主干；**选背：** 适用的让步/比较变体；**了解：** 链接范文的题目词汇。下面是待填充骨架，不是260词成稿。[ ] 内必须填实质内容，正式考试删除标记。

## 四段连续模板

Although [TREND] may cause [MAIN DRAWBACK], I believe its advantages outweigh the disadvantages because [DECISIVE BENEFIT].

Admittedly, [TREND] can create [DISADVANTAGE]. This is because [MECHANISM]. For example, [SPECIFIC CASE]. As a result, [NEGATIVE EFFECT]. However, [WHY THIS COST IS LIMITED OR CAN BE REDUCED].

More importantly, [TREND] can [MAJOR ADVANTAGE]. When [CONDITION], [HOW IT WORKS]. For instance, [EXAMPLE]. As a result, [BROADER OR LONGER-TERM BENEFIT]. This benefit is more significant than [DRAWBACK] because [EXPLICIT COMPARISON].

In conclusion, despite [DRAWBACK], I believe the advantages outweigh the disadvantages because [COMPARISON SUMMARY].

## 问法、替换与例句

Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? 两边都写，必须说明为什么一边更重。认为弊大于利时交换利弊与论证顺序，全篇改为 disadvantages outweigh the advantages。

**比较母句：** This benefit is particularly important because [BROADER / MORE FREQUENT / LONGER-LASTING IMPACT]. 优先选影响人数、程度、持续时间、可缓解程度中的一个具体标准。

**短套用示例：** This benefit is more significant than [the inconvenience of fewer local shops] because [saving travel time helps a much wider range of consumers on a regular basis]. 需在主体实际解释比较，不把“小店都能成功转型”当成必然。

**常见错误：** 数出两个优点一个缺点就断言利大于弊；只列双方却没比较轻重。

## 字数与展开

练习目标 **260–290词** ：Introduction 约45、Body 1 约95、Body 2 约100、Conclusion 约30，共约270。用背景/机制解释观点，再用具体例子说明结果；不要靠重复题目或模板连接词补足词数。

**通用展开：Point → Context → Action / Mechanism → Effect / Fact → Link back。** 需要例子时自然插入，不要求每项各占一句。两个主体段各自完成一个任务。
