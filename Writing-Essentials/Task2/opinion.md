> **母模板：Facts。必背：**四段功能与主干；**选背：**适用的让步/比较变体；**了解：**链接范文的题目词汇。下面是待填充骨架，不是260词成稿。[ ] 内必须填实质内容，正式考试删除标记。

## 四段连续模板

It is often argued that [STATEMENT]. I largely agree with this view because [REASON 1] and [REASON 2].

One important reason why I agree is that [POINT 1]. This is because [RELEVANT SITUATION]. When [ACTION OR CONDITION], [DIRECT RESULT]. For example, [SPECIFIC EXAMPLE]. As a result, [FURTHER EFFECT]. Therefore, [LINK TO THE ORIGINAL CLAIM].

Another reason is that [POINT 2]. In many cases, [EXPLANATION]. By [SPECIFIC ACTION], [PEOPLE OR ORGANISATIONS] can [RESULT]. For instance, [EXAMPLE]. This suggests that [WHY THE CLAIM IS VALID].

In conclusion, I largely agree that [STATEMENT] because [SUMMARY OF REASONS].

## 问法、替换与例句

To what extent do you agree or disagree? 必须给程度与一致立场。反对时把 agree 换成 disagree，末句 valid 换成 questionable，并同步改理由。

### 可选让步后拉回｜替换 Body 2，仍为四段

Admittedly, [OPPOSITE VIEW] has some merit because [REASON]. For example, [LIMITED CASE]. However, this does not necessarily mean that [OPPOSITE CONCLUSION]. When [KEY CONDITION], [YOUR POSITION] becomes more important because [REASON]. Therefore, although [OPPOSITE SIDE] should not be ignored, I still believe that [MAIN POSITION].

采用让步版时，Introduction 改成 **I largely agree with this view because [MAIN REASON], although [LIMITED CONCESSION].** 结论也只总结实际论证过的理由。完全单边不必硬加让步；承认有价值不等于同意 only / best / most important。

**短套用示例：**Admittedly, [basic research can be valuable even without immediate applications]. However, this does not necessarily mean that [improving people's lives should lose its priority]. [Its direct effects on health and safety] make this aim particularly important.

**常见错误：**只证明“有用”，没有判断“是否最重要”；正文转成平衡讨论，结论却说完全同意。

## 字数与展开

练习目标 **260–290词**：Introduction 约45、Body 1 约95、Body 2 约100、Conclusion 约30，共约270。用背景/机制解释观点，再用具体例子说明结果；不要靠重复题目或模板连接词补足词数。

**通用展开：Point → Context → Action / Mechanism → Effect / Fact → Link back。**需要例子时自然插入，不要求每项各占一句。两个主体段各自完成一个任务。
