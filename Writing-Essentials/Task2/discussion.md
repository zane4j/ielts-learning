> **母模板：Effects / Facts。必背：**四段功能与主干；**选背：**适用的让步/比较变体；**了解：**链接范文的题目词汇。下面是待填充骨架，不是260词成稿。[ ] 内必须填实质内容，正式考试删除标记。

## 四段连续模板

Some people believe that [VIEW A], while others argue that [VIEW B]. Although [LIMITED MERIT OF A], I believe that [VIEW B] is generally a better approach because [MAIN REASON].

On the one hand, one major reason why some people support [VIEW A] is that [REASON A]. This is because [MECHANISM]. For example, [SPECIFIC CASE]. As a result, [OUTCOME A]. This explains why [VIEW A] can be reasonable in [CONTEXT].

On the other hand, I believe that [VIEW B] is more beneficial because [REASON B]. When [CONDITION], [MECHANISM]. For instance, [SPECIFIC CASE]. This can lead to [RESULT B]. Therefore, [WHY B IS PREFERABLE].

In conclusion, while [VIEW A] may be useful in [CONTEXT], I believe that [VIEW B] is a better approach because [DECISIVE REASON].

## 问法、替换与例句

Discuss both views and give your own opinion：一段解释A成立的理由，一段解释B，并表明判断。偏好A时交换双方顺序，别漏改 Introduction / Conclusion。

**短套用示例：**On the one hand, one major reason why some people support [accepting a difficult job temporarily] is that [a regular salary protects financial stability]. For example, [a worker still needs to pay rent while looking for another position].

**常见错误（对应旧练习）：**一段写“改善的好处”、另一段写“接受的坏处”，两段都支持改善，没真正讨论接受方为何合理。不要把双边讨论写成对方全错。

## 字数与展开

练习目标 **260–290词**：Introduction 约45、Body 1 约95、Body 2 约100、Conclusion 约30，共约270。用背景/机制解释观点，再用具体例子说明结果；不要靠重复题目或模板连接词补足词数。

**通用展开：Point → Context → Action / Mechanism → Effect / Fact → Link back。**需要例子时自然插入，不要求每项各占一句。两个主体段各自完成一个任务。
