> **母模板：Effects。必背：**四段功能与主干；**选背：**适用的让步/比较变体；**了解：**链接范文的题目词汇。下面是待填充骨架，不是260词成稿。[ ] 内必须填实质内容，正式考试删除标记。

## 四段连续模板

[TREND] has become increasingly common in [CONTEXT]. It offers [MAIN ADVANTAGE], but it can also cause [MAIN DISADVANTAGE].

One major advantage of [TREND] is that [BENEFIT]. This is particularly useful when [CONTEXT]. By [ACTION], [TARGET GROUP] can [DIRECT EFFECT]. For example, [EXAMPLE]. As a result, [FURTHER POSITIVE EFFECT].

A significant disadvantage, however, is that [DRAWBACK]. When [CONDITION], [NEGATIVE MECHANISM]. For instance, [EXAMPLE]. Consequently, [NEGATIVE RESULT]. [EXPLANATION OF WHY THIS MATTERS].

In conclusion, [TREND] offers [ADVANTAGE], but it also creates [DISADVANTAGE].

## 问法、替换与例句

What are the advantages and disadvantages? 一段优点、一段缺点；未要求 outweigh 就不强加轻重判断。若题干没显示趋势变多，第一句直接改写题干，不编造 increasingly common。

**短套用示例：**One major advantage of [working from home] is that [employees can avoid long daily journeys]. As a result, [they have more time for rest and family responsibilities].

**常见错误：**只写优点；把“沟通困难”当成第二个优点；花整段讲解决方案却没展开缺点。

## 字数与展开

练习目标 **260–290词**：Introduction 约45、Body 1 约95、Body 2 约100、Conclusion 约30，共约270。用背景/机制解释观点，再用具体例子说明结果；不要靠重复题目或模板连接词补足词数。

**通用展开：Point → Context → Action / Mechanism → Effect / Fact → Link back。**需要例子时自然插入，不要求每项各占一句。两个主体段各自完成一个任务。
