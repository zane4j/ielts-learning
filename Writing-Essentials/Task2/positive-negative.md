> **母模板：Effects。必背：** 四段功能与主干；**选背：** 适用的让步/比较变体；**了解：** 链接范文的题目词汇。下面是待填充骨架，不是260词成稿。[ ] 内必须填实质内容，正式考试删除标记。

## 四段连续模板

[TREND] has become more common in [CONTEXT]. Although it may bring [LIMITED BENEFIT], I consider it a largely negative development because [EFFECT 1] and [EFFECT 2].

One important problem with [TREND] is that [NEGATIVE EFFECT 1]. [RELEVANT CONTEXT]. When [ACTION OR CHANGE], [MECHANISM]. For example, [CASE]. As a result, [HARMFUL OUTCOME]. Therefore, [LINK TO OVERALL JUDGMENT].

Another negative consequence is [EFFECT 2]. This is because [EXPLANATION]. For instance, [CASE]. This can [FURTHER RESULT]. Although [POSSIBLE BENEFIT], [WHY IT DOES NOT OVERTURN THE JUDGMENT].

In conclusion, despite [LIMITED BENEFIT], [TREND] is largely negative because [TWO MAIN EFFECTS].

## 问法、替换与例句

Is this a positive or negative development? 评价的是题中发展趋势。可用两条负面逻辑链；也可一正一负后判断总体。总体正面时，把问题、后果和结论一起切换为正面。

**短套用示例：** One important problem with [rural depopulation] is that [local businesses lose customers]. As a result, [essential services may become harder to maintain].

**常见错误：** 只描述人们为什么迁走，不评价这种趋势；把个人收入好处自动等同整体正面。

## 字数与展开

练习目标 **260–290词** ：Introduction 约45、Body 1 约95、Body 2 约100、Conclusion 约30，共约270。用背景/机制解释观点，再用具体例子说明结果；不要靠重复题目或模板连接词补足词数。

**通用展开：Point → Context → Action / Mechanism → Effect / Fact → Link back。** 需要例子时自然插入，不要求每项各占一句。两个主体段各自完成一个任务。
