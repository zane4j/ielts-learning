## 四段连续模板

The [CHART TYPE] compares [WHAT] in [PLACE] between [YEAR A] and [YEAR B], measured in [UNIT].

Overall, [A] increased, whereas [B] declined over the period. By [END YEAR], [MAIN RANKING CHANGE].

In [START YEAR], the figure for [A] stood at [X], compared with [Y] for [B]. Over the following [PERIOD], [A] rose to [Z], while [B] fell to [W]. Meanwhile, [C] remained stable at [VALUE].

By [END YEAR], [A] had reached [FINAL A], whereas [B] stood at [FINAL B]. The figure for [C] rose to [PEAK] in [YEAR] before falling to [FINAL C], leaving it [FINAL COMPARISON].

## 用法、母句与纠错

先看时间变化，再按相似/相反趋势分组。上升、下降、峰值等是可替换选项，不能默认每题都有。

| 功能 | 必背母句 | 使用边界 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 上升 / 下降 | The figure rose/fell from [X] to [Y]. | rose 后不加 was |
| 峰值 | It reached a peak of [X] in [YEAR]. | 需要多个观测点支持 |
| 波动 | The figure fluctuated between [X] and [Y]. | 需要反复变化，不能仅凭两点 |
| 持平 | It remained stable at [X]. | 核对时间范围 |
| 转折 | It rose to [X] before falling to [Y]. | before + V-ing |
| 增幅 | The share rose by [N] percentage points. | 20%→30% 是10个百分点，不是10% |

**例句（模拟）：** The share of bus users rose from 20% to 40%, overtaking cars by 2020.

**常见错误：** increase to 是最终值；increase by 是增量。number 用于可数人数/数量；proportion 用于占比。预测年份用 is projected to / is expected to，不能写成已经发生的事实。

## 字数与有效扩展

练习目标 **150–170词** ：Introduction 约20，Overview 约30，两个 Body 各约55，共约160。以总词数为准，不逐段凑数。增加有图据的关键数据、比较、方位和例外；不写主观原因或重复 Overview。

**完整应用与模拟设定：** [原有动态图四段范文与详细笔记](../../writing/task1/templates/dynamic-charts.md)。原资料保留，必背页负责提炼框架。

**自检：** Overview 是否概括两点？主体是否有数据/位置证据？比较对象、单位、时态是否一致？
