> **必背：**制造类 Introduction + 线性双句 Overview + before being / which / where。**选背：**原料转化、自然循环、分支流程变体。只写图中已有信息；阶段数不明就用 a series of stages。下列模板沿用原母句，吸收近期方便面练习；原图未随本任务提供，示例不是对原题步骤数的核定。

## 四段连续模板｜线性制造

The diagram illustrates the various stages involved in the process of manufacturing [PRODUCT] from [RAW MATERIAL].

Overall, it is a linear process consisting of [NUMBER] main stages, beginning with [START] and ending with [END]. The main stages involve [PHASE A], [PHASE B] and [PHASE C].

Initially, [RAW MATERIAL] is stored in [CONTAINER] before being transferred to [PLACE], where it is mixed with [OTHER MATERIAL] to form [INTERMEDIATE PRODUCT]. This is then [ACTION], producing [RESULT], which is subsequently [NEXT ACTION].

Following this, [PRODUCT] is [ACTION] before being [NEXT ACTION]. Once it has been [COMPLETED ACTION], it is placed in [CONTAINER], where [ADDITIONAL ACTION] takes place. Finally, [FINAL PRODUCT] is [FINAL ACTION], completing the process.

| --- | --- | --- |
| **制造类，优先背** | The diagram illustrates the various stages involved in the process of manufacturing [PRODUCT] from [RAW MATERIAL]. | The diagram illustrates the various stages involved in the process of manufacturing instant noodles from flour. |
| 原料转化 | The diagram illustrates the process by which [RAW MATERIAL] is processed and converted into [PRODUCT]. | The diagram illustrates the process by which waste paper is processed and converted into recycled paper. |

Introduction 只交代主题。from 后的材料、生命周期端点必须有图据；不明确时省略。约15–25词即可，较短的准确句子也可以。

## Overview｜扩展的是信息，不是套话

**A｜线性生产，优先背：**

Overall, it is a linear process consisting of [NUMBER] main stages, beginning with [START] and ending with [END]. The main stages involve [PHASE A], [PHASE B] and [PHASE C].

第二句将细步骤归为宏观阶段。例如 preparing the dough / processing the noodles / packaging the finished product。三项语法平行，全部用 V-ing；不能复述第一句的起点终点。

**B｜原料转化，选背：**

Overall, the process consists of [NUMBER] main stages, beginning with [START] and ending with [END]. Throughout the process, [RAW MATERIAL] undergoes [TYPES OF TREATMENT] before being converted into [PRODUCT].

只有图中确实体现时才写 mechanical / chemical treatments；不以常识补工艺。

**C｜自然循环，选背：**

Overall, this is a cyclical process in which [SUBJECT] passes through [MAIN TRANSFORMATIONS]. Following [LATER STAGE], [EVENT] returns the cycle to [STARTING CONDITION].

示例：Overall, this is a cyclical process in which water moves between bodies of water and the atmosphere. Following evaporation and condensation, precipitation returns water to the ground, allowing the cycle to repeat. 仅适用于题图展示这些环节时。

循环主体也用四段：Introduction / Overview / Initially, [EARLY STAGES]. After [EVENT], [NEXT STAGE]. / Subsequently, [LATER STAGES]. Finally, [RETURN TO START]. 自然动作优先用主动，如 Water evaporates；不把生物成长全部改成被动。

**D｜分支或多产物，了解：**Overall, the process involves [MAIN TRANSFORMATION], after which the material is divided into [BRANCHES / OUTPUTS]. 主体按分支分组，不强写单一路径。阶段数、分支数均须可确定。

## 从100词扩到150–170词｜五种有效补充

| 扩展功能 | 母句 | 教学例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 结果形态 | to form / into [PRODUCT] | Flour is mixed with water and oil to form dough. |
| 下一步动作 | which is/are then [V-ed] | The dough is rolled into sheets, which are then cut into strips. |
| 完成条件 | Once [S] has/have been [V-ed], ... | Once the noodles have been dried, they are placed into cups. |
| 设备或地点 | in [DEVICE] / where [S] is/are [V-ed] | Flour is stored in silos before being transferred to a mixer. |
| 宏观归纳 | The main stages involve [V-ing], [V-ing] and [V-ing]. | The main stages involve preparing the dough, processing the noodles and packaging the finished product. |

所有补充须来自图示；不另造机器、时间、温度、处理目的。**in order to ensure high quality** 等未给出的目的也不用于凑词。词数建议：20 + 35 + 50 + 55 = 160；不是每段必须达到固定长度。

## 完整模拟例子｜方便面生产（非原题核定版）

**为本页明确设定的八个主阶段：**①储存面粉；②加水油混合；③压片；④切条；⑤制成面饼；⑥油煮并干燥；⑦装杯并加蔬菜香料；⑧密封贴标。第⑥⑦⑧各含多个操作。本例不沿用历史答复中未核定的 nine stages，实际考试以原图编号为准。

The diagram illustrates the various stages involved in the process of manufacturing instant noodles from flour.

Overall, it is a linear process consisting of eight main stages, beginning with flour being stored in silos and ending with the cups being sealed and labelled. The main stages involve preparing the dough, processing the noodles and packaging the finished product.

Initially, flour is stored in silos before being mixed with water and oil to form dough. The dough is then rolled into sheets, which are subsequently cut into strips. These strips are shaped into noodle discs before moving on to the cooking stage.

Following this, the noodle discs are cooked in oil and then dried. Once they have been dried, they are placed into cups, where vegetables and spices are added. Finally, the cups containing the noodles and these additional ingredients are sealed and labelled, completing the manufacturing process at the factory.

## 易错点与自检

- 近期练习：**noodles**，不是 noddles；**into**，不是 in to；不用 we get 描述生产结果。
- **Once they have been dried**，避免 Once noodles are already dried；一般现在时 Once they are dried 也可用。
- **before being processed**，不是材料自己 before processing；which are 对应 strips，which is 对应 dough。
- **consists of**，不是 is consisted of；没有确定阶段数就删掉数字。
- 双句 Overview 是否增加了宏观信息？是否漏掉分支、回流或最终产物？
