# 混合图｜必背四段模板

[必背总目录](../README.md) · [本类目录](README.md) · [Writing 目录](../../writing/README.md) · [仓库首页](../../README.md)

> **必背：**一段引言介绍两图、一段总览覆盖两图；主体按图或共同主题分组。混合图是组合形式，可同时包含动态与静态信息。不同单位分别描述，不推断因果。

## 四段连续模板

The [FIRST CHART TYPE] shows [INDICATOR A] in [PLACE] over [PERIOD], while the [SECOND CHART TYPE] compares [INDICATOR B] across [GROUPS] in [YEAR].

Overall, [MAIN FEATURE OF FIRST CHART]. In the second chart, [MAIN FEATURE OF SECOND CHART].

Looking first at [INDICATOR A], the figure stood at [START VALUE] in [YEAR A] before rising to [END VALUE] in [YEAR B]. [CATEGORY OR INTERMEDIATE POINT] showed [SUPPORTED COMPARISON], at [VALUE].

Turning to [INDICATOR B], [CATEGORY A] accounted for the largest share, at [X%], followed by [CATEGORY B] at [Y%]. The remaining [Z%] was attributed to [CATEGORY C]. [ADDITIONAL COMPARISON SUPPORTED BY THE CHART].

## 母句与分组

- **Looking first at [INDICATOR], ... / Turning to [SECOND INDICATOR], ...**：切换图表。
- **The first chart shows ..., while the second compares ...**：一次介绍两图。
- **Overall, [FEATURE 1]. Meanwhile, [FEATURE 2].**：同时覆盖两图。
- **选背：按同一类别跨图比较。**仅当两图对象和时间可对应时使用，不要求强行找联系。
- 如果两图都是静态：把 rose to 换成 stood at / was higher than。如果两图都是动态：两段均交代趋势与数值。

## 完整模拟例子（非真题）

**数据设定：**一家博物馆访客量，2010/2015/2020 年分别为40/50/80万人；2020年访客来源占比：本地50%、国内其他地区30%、海外20%。两图分别为折线图和饼图。

The line graph shows the number of visitors to a museum between 2010 and 2020, while the pie chart presents their origins in the final year.

Overall, museum attendance doubled over the period, with a larger increase in the second half. In 2020, local residents formed the largest group of visitors, accounting for the same share as the other two groups combined.

Looking first at attendance, the museum received 400,000 visitors in 2010. This figure rose to 500,000 in 2015, an increase of 100,000. Over the following five years, it climbed by a further 300,000 to reach 800,000, which was twice the initial figure.

Turning to the visitors' origins, local residents made up 50% of the total in 2020. Visitors from elsewhere in the country accounted for 30%, compared with 20% for those from overseas. The domestic non-local share was therefore ten percentage points higher than the overseas figure, while local residents outnumbered either group individually.

## 字数、错误与自检

目标150–170词：引言约25、总览约30、主体各约50–55。优先补比较，不重复题干。上文英文正文词数见总目录的核验说明。

**常见错误：**只概括一张图；将人数与百分比直接比大小；因为两图一起出现就推断“本地访客导致总量上升”；缺少来源数据仍计算历年各来源人数。

**自检：**两图是否均有 Overview 与主体证据？年份是否对应？两图能否直接关联？
