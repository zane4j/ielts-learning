# Task 1｜最少必背母句

**第一优先：每个功能只背一个稳定表达。** [ ] 换为图中事实；时态、单位、主谓单复数随题调整。

| 功能 | 母句 | 对应类型 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 图表引言 | The [CHART] compares [WHAT] in [PLACE] over [PERIOD]. | 动态/静态/混合 |
| 趋势总览 | Overall, [A] increased, whereas [B] declined. | 动态 |
| 占比总览 | Overall, [A] accounted for the largest share. | 静态 |
| 数据起点 | The figure for [A] stood at [X]. | 数据图 |
| 数据对比 | [A] stood at [X], compared with [Y] for [B]. | 数据图 |
| 变化 | The figure rose from [X] to [Y]. | 动态 |
| 倍数 | The figure for [A] was twice that for [B]. | 同单位比较 |
| 流程引言 | The diagram illustrates the various stages involved in the process of manufacturing [PRODUCT]. | 制造流程 |
| 流程总览 | Overall, the process consists of [NUMBER] main stages, beginning with [START] and ending with [END]. | 线性流程 |
| 流程衔接 | [MATERIAL] is [V-ed] before being [V-ed]. | 同一材料连续操作 |
| 产物衔接 | [A] is processed into [B], which is then [V-ed]. | 单复数随B调整 |
| 地图变化 | [A] was replaced by [B], while [C] remained unchanged. | 两个过去时期 |

**选背：** peaked at / fluctuated between / was converted into / is planned to be built。**了解：** 罕见同义词；先保准确，再考虑变化。

## 三组最值得记的对照

- **rose to 30% / rose by 10 percentage points** ：终值 / 增量。
- **which is then / which are then** ：看最近产物的单复数。
- **was replaced / has been replaced / will be replaced** ：过去 / 截至现在的结果 / 未来。

**例句（模拟）：** The figure for buses rose from 20% to 40%, whereas car use declined. / The old car park was replaced by a café.
