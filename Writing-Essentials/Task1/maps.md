> **必背：**下列四段骨架和功能母句；**选背：**与题图匹配的变体；**了解：**原笔记的更多表达。[ ] 内填题目事实，考场去掉标记。模板不是填完就自动达到150词的范文。

## 四段连续模板

The maps illustrate how [PLACE] changed between [YEAR A] and [YEAR B].

Overall, the area underwent significant changes, with [MAIN CHANGE] and [NEW FACILITIES]. However, [UNCHANGED FEATURE] remained unchanged.

On the western side, [OLD A] was replaced by [NEW A], while [OLD B] was expanded. A new [FACILITY] was also added near [LANDMARK]. Previously, this part of the area had contained [OLD FEATURE].

In the eastern section, [OLD C] was converted into [NEW C], and [OLD D] was demolished to make way for [NEW D]. Meanwhile, [RETAINED FEATURE] remained in its original location, and [ROAD OR PATH] continued to connect [A] with [B].

## 用法、母句与纠错

模板默认两个过去时间，主体按区域对比。若没有保留设施，就把 Overview 的第二句改成另一项主要变化，不能虚构 unchanged。

| 时间 / 功能 | 替换母句 |
| --- | --- |
| 过去→现在 | The area has undergone significant changes. [A] has been replaced by [B]. |
| 现在→未来 Introduction | The maps illustrate the current layout of [PLACE] and its proposed future development. |
| 未来 Overview | Overall, the area is set to undergo major redevelopment, with [CURRENT USE] replaced by [PLANNED USE]. |
| 未来主体 | Under the proposed plan, [A] will be replaced by [B], while [ROAD] will be extended towards [DIRECTION]. |
| 方位 | in the north-eastern corner / on the western side / to the east of [LANDMARK] |
| 新增 / 扩建 | A new [FACILITY] was built. [FACILITY] was expanded. |
| 原址替换 | [A] was removed, with [B] built in its place. |

**例句（模拟）：**The car park was replaced by a café, while the playground on the western side was expanded.

**选背：两处静态地点对比。**The maps compare the layouts of [A] and [B]. Overall, [MAIN SIMILARITY], but [MAIN DIFFERENCE]. 主体一段写共同布局，一段写差异；不能凭两张不同地点的图推断改建。

**常见错误：**with a room converted，不是 with a room has been converted；has been converted 是完整句谓语。规划未落地不能写 has been built。地图无方位标识时优先用 left/right/top/bottom。

## 字数与有效扩展

练习目标 **150–170词**：Introduction 约20，Overview 约30，两个 Body 各约55，共约160。以总词数为准，不逐段凑数。增加有图据的关键数据、比较、方位和例外；不写主观原因或重复 Overview。

**完整应用与模拟设定：**[原有地图题四段范文与详细笔记](../../writing/task1/templates/maps.md)。原资料保留，必背页负责提炼框架。

**自检：**Overview 是否概括两点？主体是否有数据/位置证据？比较对象、单位、时态是否一致？
