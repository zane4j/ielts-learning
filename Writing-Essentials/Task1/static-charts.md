## 四段连续模板

The [CHART TYPE] compares [WHAT] across [GROUPS] in [YEAR], measured in [UNIT].

Overall, [A] accounted for the largest share in [GROUP], whereas [B] represented the smallest proportion. The most noticeable difference between the groups was in [C].

In [GROUP A], [A] accounted for [X%], followed by [B] at [Y%]. By comparison, the corresponding figures in [GROUP B] were [P%] and [Q%], respectively. Together, these categories made up [TOTAL%] of [GROUP A TOTAL].

Regarding the remaining categories, [C] stood at [X%] in [GROUP A], compared with [Y%] in [GROUP B]. The figure for [D] was [MULTIPLE] that for [E], while [F] showed only a relatively small difference between the groups.

## 用法、母句与纠错

按大类/小类或群体分组；只有同一时间的数据，不写上升下降。不是占比数据时，把 accounted for / share 换为 had the highest figure / stood at。

| 功能 | 必背母句 |
| --- | --- |
| 占比 | [A] accounted for [X%] of the total. |
| 排名 | [A] ranked second, at [X]. |
| 倍数 | The figure for [A] was twice that for [B]. |
| 相等 | [A] and [B] accounted for equal shares, at [X%] each. |
| 差距 | The proportion for [A] was [N] percentage points higher than that for [B]. |

**例句（模拟）：**Housing accounted for 35% of expenditure in City A, five percentage points higher than in City B.

**常见错误：**accounted for，不是 accounted from；不要把城市本身与数字比较；respectively 前后的顺序必须一致。

## 字数与有效扩展

练习目标 **150–170词**：Introduction 约20，Overview 约30，两个 Body 各约55，共约160。以总词数为准，不逐段凑数。增加有图据的关键数据、比较、方位和例外；不写主观原因或重复 Overview。

**完整应用与模拟设定：**[原有静态图四段范文与详细笔记](../../writing/task1/templates/static-charts.md)。原资料保留，必背页负责提炼框架。

**自检：**Overview 是否概括两点？主体是否有数据/位置证据？比较对象、单位、时态是否一致？
