## Q1

### Why is reliable public transport important?

侧重点：原因｜时间可预测。主回答 85 词。

I think reliability matters because **people arrange other activities around the time they expect to arrive**. For example, someone going to play badminton may miss part of a court booking because their bus gets stuck in traffic. **The problem is not just spending longer on the road; it also means losing something they have already planned and paid for. A service that takes slightly longer but arrives predictably may be easier to use. It allows passengers to organize their day with less uncertainty and frustration.**

复述提示：`people arrange other activities around the time they expect to arrive` → `court booking` → `stuck in traffic`

## Q2

### How can cities encourage people to use public transport?

侧重点：解决｜改善服务。主回答 70 词。

I think cities need to **make public transport a practical choice, not simply tell people that it is better**. For example, someone going to play badminton may miss part of a court booking because their bus gets stuck in traffic. **More reliable services, useful route information and convenient connections could make that journey less stressful. Low fares help too, but price alone may not persuade someone who repeatedly arrives late.**

复述提示：`make public transport a practical choice, not simply tell people that it is better` → `court booking` → `stuck in traffic`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**People are more likely to change their habits when the service fits their actual daily needs.**

## Q3

### Why do some adults find it difficult to exercise regularly?

侧重点：原因｜场地与时间障碍。主回答 60 词。

I think a major difficulty is **fitting exercise around work, travel and other commitments**. For example, someone going to play badminton may miss part of a court booking because their bus gets stuck in traffic. **If reaching the venue becomes tiring or unpredictable, the activity can start to feel like another obligation. That doesn't necessarily mean the person dislikes sport.**

复述提示：`fitting exercise around work, travel and other commitments` → `court booking` → `stuck in traffic`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**A closer facility or a more convenient time could make regular exercise easier, because the effort required to get there is part of the overall experience.**

## Q4

### Is it important to make plans for leisure activities?

侧重点：评价｜安排与弹性。主回答 68 词。

I'd say planning is useful **when an activity involves bookings or other people, but it should leave room for delays**. For example, someone going to play badminton may miss part of a court booking because their bus gets stuck in traffic. **Allowing extra travel time and checking the route beforehand could reduce that risk. Still, trying to schedule every minute of a free afternoon may create unnecessary pressure.**

复述提示：`when an activity involves bookings or other people, but it should leave room for delays` → `court booking` → `stuck in traffic`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**A simple plan with some flexibility can protect the main activity without making leisure feel exactly like another day at work.**
