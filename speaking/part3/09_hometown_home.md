## Q1

### Why do many young people move to big cities?

侧重点：原因｜工作机会。主回答 66 词。

I think one major reason is **access to jobs that match their skills and future plans**. For example, someone may enjoy visiting relatives in a quieter hometown but still choose to live in Shenzhen because their job is there. **For certain careers, a larger city may offer more employers and more chances to change roles. That can outweigh the attraction of a slower pace of life.**

复述提示：`access to jobs that match their skills and future plans` → `quieter hometown` → `their job is there`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**Still, the choice involves trade-offs, because higher housing costs and longer journeys can reduce some of the benefits of earning a better salary.**

## Q2

### What should people consider when choosing where to live?

侧重点：特征｜日常需求。主回答 73 词。

I think people should **consider their daily needs, especially work, transport, costs and family responsibilities**. For example, someone may enjoy visiting relatives in a quieter hometown but still choose to live in Shenzhen because their job is there. **A spacious home is less useful if getting to work becomes exhausting. On the other hand, living close to relatives can be valuable when someone needs support. So there isn't one ideal location for everyone.**

复述提示：`consider their daily needs, especially work, transport, costs and family responsibilities` → `quieter hometown` → `their job is there`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**The best choice is usually the place that fits the person's current stage of life.**

## Q3

### Is visiting a place different from living there?

侧重点：比较｜体验与责任。主回答 66 词。

Yes, because **a short visit mainly shows the enjoyable parts, while daily life includes practical responsibilities**. For example, someone may enjoy visiting relatives in a quieter hometown but still choose to live in Shenzhen because their job is there. **During a visit, a relaxed afternoon with family can be enough to make the place appealing. Living there would also involve finding work and managing everyday routines.**

复述提示：`a short visit mainly shows the enjoyable parts, while daily life includes practical responsibilities` → `quieter hometown` → `their job is there`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**That is why someone can sincerely like a place without wanting to move there permanently or make it their main home.**

## Q4

### How can people maintain family relationships when they live far apart?

侧重点：解决｜稳定联系。主回答 77 词。

I think people need to **make regular contact a habit and give each other proper attention when they meet**. For example, someone may enjoy visiting relatives in a quieter hometown but still choose to live in Shenzhen because their job is there. **Video calls can help them share ordinary news between visits, rather than only speaking when there is a problem. When they do meet, putting phones away during a meal can make the time more meaningful.**

复述提示：`make regular contact a habit and give each other proper attention when they meet` → `quieter hometown` → `their job is there`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**The distance matters, but the effort both sides make matters as well.**
