## Q1

### Why do people enjoy visiting street markets?

侧重点：原因｜选择与气氛。主回答 73 词。

I think people enjoy street markets because **they can try different foods and experience a lively atmosphere in one place**. For example, a night market may offer plenty of food stalls, but loud music and a narrow walkway can make it difficult to enjoy a meal. **So the attraction is often the variety and the chance to explore, rather than comfort alone. Some visitors enjoy that energy, while others prefer to arrive earlier.**

复述提示：`they can try different foods and experience a lively atmosphere in one place` → `food stalls` → `loud music` → `a narrow walkway`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**The same place can feel exciting or tiring depending on how busy it is and what someone expects.**

## Q2

### What problems can overcrowding cause?

侧重点：问题｜通行与交流。主回答 72 词。

Overcrowding can **make ordinary activities slower and more stressful, especially when the space wasn't designed for large numbers of visitors**. For example, a night market may offer plenty of food stalls, but loud music and a narrow walkway can make it difficult to enjoy a meal. **Friends may lose sight of each other, and customers can spend more time waiting than eating. It also becomes harder to move freely through the area.**

复述提示：`make ordinary activities slower and more stressful, especially when the space wasn't designed for large numbers of visitors` → `food stalls` → `loud music` → `a narrow walkway`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**So attracting more visitors isn't always an improvement if the basic layout cannot comfortably handle the extra people.**

## Q3

### How can noise in public places be reduced?

侧重点：解决｜针对噪音来源。主回答 71 词。

I think managers should **identify avoidable sources of noise and set reasonable limits on them**. For example, a night market may offer plenty of food stalls, but loud music and a narrow walkway can make it difficult to enjoy a meal. **Turning down speakers would be a practical start, and a separate seating area could help people have conversations. There is no need to remove every sound from a lively market.**

复述提示：`identify avoidable sources of noise and set reasonable limits on them` → `food stalls` → `loud music` → `a narrow walkway`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**The aim should be to let people enjoy the atmosphere without having to shout throughout their visit.**

## Q4

### How can small businesses attract repeat customers?

侧重点：解决｜整体体验。主回答 65 词。

I think small businesses need to **offer reliable quality and make the whole visit pleasant, rather than only attracting attention once**. For example, a night market may offer plenty of food stalls, but loud music and a narrow walkway can make it difficult to enjoy a meal. **A stall with clear prices, friendly service and an orderly queue gives customers fewer reasons to leave frustrated.**

复述提示：`offer reliable quality and make the whole visit pleasant, rather than only attracting attention once` → `food stalls` → `loud music` → `a narrow walkway`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**Good food may bring someone in the first time, but an easy, enjoyable experience can make them willing to return and recommend the business to friends.**
