## Q1

### Why are some people afraid of making changes?

侧重点：原因｜失去确定性。主回答 81 词。

I think people are often afraid of **losing something reliable before they know whether the new option will work**. For example, a travel video might encourage someone to consider studying English abroad, even though they worry about losing a stable income. **The possible benefits may seem distant, while rent and daily expenses are immediate concerns. So their hesitation isn't necessarily a lack of ambition. They may simply need savings and a clearer plan before they feel comfortable taking the next step.**

复述提示：`losing something reliable before they know whether the new option will work` → `studying English abroad` → `losing a stable income`

## Q2

### How can famous people influence young people?

侧重点：影响｜把选择具体化。主回答 84 词。

I think famous people can **make unfamiliar choices seem possible by showing their own experiences**. For example, a travel video might encourage someone to consider studying English abroad, even though they worry about losing a stable income. **Seeing a real person communicate in another country can be more encouraging than hearing general advice. However, online content often shows only selected moments. Young viewers should treat it as inspiration and investigate the costs and difficulties themselves before making an important decision about their own future.**

复述提示：`make unfamiliar choices seem possible by showing their own experiences` → `studying English abroad` → `losing a stable income`

## Q3

### Should people take risks to achieve their goals?

侧重点：评价｜计算风险。主回答 84 词。

I'd say **some risk can be worthwhile, but it should be manageable rather than completely unplanned**. For example, a travel video might encourage someone to consider studying English abroad, even though they worry about losing a stable income. **Before leaving work, that person could compare course fees, save money and think about future job options. These steps wouldn't remove every risk, but they would reduce the chance of serious financial trouble. For me, being brave also means preparing carefully for what might go wrong.**

复述提示：`some risk can be worthwhile, but it should be manageable rather than completely unplanned` → `studying English abroad` → `losing a stable income`

## Q4

### Why do people sometimes change their opinions?

侧重点：原因｜新信息。主回答 84 词。

I think people change their opinions **when new information makes them question an earlier assumption**. For example, a travel video might encourage someone to consider studying English abroad, even though they worry about losing a stable income. **They may previously have believed that keeping the same job was always the safest choice. The video could help them see that developing a skill also has long-term value. Still, changing an opinion doesn't mean acting immediately; it can simply mean becoming willing to explore another option.**

复述提示：`when new information makes them question an earlier assumption` → `studying English abroad` → `losing a stable income`
