## Q1

### How has technology changed the way people remember things?

侧重点：变化｜外部记录。主回答 76 词。

I think technology has **made it easier to store practical information outside our own memory**. For example, a person can save appointments on their phone and set reminders, but the same device can interrupt them with unrelated notifications. **Compared with a paper note, a digital reminder can actively alert someone at the right time. That is useful when they are busy or moving between places. However, it also means they need to manage the information carefully.**

复述提示：`made it easier to store practical information outside our own memory` → `set reminders` → `unrelated notifications`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**Saving everything somewhere is not very helpful if they cannot find it when needed.**

## Q2

### Do smartphones make people more productive?

侧重点：评价｜用途决定效果。主回答 78 词。

They can, but I think **the benefit depends on how people manage the device**. For example, a person can save appointments on their phone and set reminders, but the same device can interrupt them with unrelated notifications. **Useful features can prevent missed tasks, while constant checking can break concentration. Someone who turns off unnecessary alerts may get more benefit than someone who responds to every sound. So I wouldn't describe the phone itself as either productive or distracting.**

复述提示：`the benefit depends on how people manage the device` → `set reminders` → `unrelated notifications`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**The way it is used can push the result in either direction.**

## Q3

### How can people reduce distractions when studying?

侧重点：解决｜调整环境。主回答 71 词。

I think people should **remove predictable distractions before they start, instead of relying entirely on willpower**. For example, a person can save appointments on their phone and set reminders, but the same device can interrupt them with unrelated notifications. **During a study session, they could silence non-essential alerts and put the phone in a bag. They can also decide when to check it again, so they don't keep wondering about messages.**

复述提示：`remove predictable distractions before they start, instead of relying entirely on willpower` → `set reminders` → `unrelated notifications`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**These small changes make it easier to finish one task, although the task itself still needs to be clear and manageable.**

## Q4

### Why do some older people find new technology difficult to use?

侧重点：原因｜经验与设计。主回答 75 词。

I think some older people struggle because **the controls are unfamiliar and there may be too many options at once**. For example, a person can save appointments on their phone and set reminders, but the same device can interrupt them with unrelated notifications. **For a new user, it may be unclear which alerts matter or how to change them. Clear labels and patient, step-by-step help can make a difference. However, age alone doesn't determine ability.**

复述提示：`the controls are unfamiliar and there may be too many options at once` → `set reminders` → `unrelated notifications`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**Someone with regular practice may become very confident, while a younger beginner can also feel confused.**
