## Q1

### Are libraries still useful when information is available online?

侧重点：评价｜空间价值。主回答 63 词。

Yes, I think libraries still matter because **they provide more than access to information**. For example, someone whose apartment is noisy can study English in a public library without having to buy a drink or order food. **The internet may provide the material, but it doesn't guarantee a suitable place to concentrate. A library can offer that separation between studying and everyday distractions.**

复述提示：`they provide more than access to information` → `apartment is noisy` → `public library`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**Its role may change as reading habits change, but a quiet, shared space can remain useful even when many visitors bring their own devices.**

## Q2

### What makes a good place to study?

侧重点：特征｜环境与习惯。主回答 67 词。

I think a good study space should **be comfortable and have as few distractions as possible**. For example, someone whose apartment is noisy can study English in a public library without having to buy a drink or order food. **Good lighting and a suitable seat help, but personal habits matter too. If the learner checks messages every few minutes, even a quiet room won't solve the problem.**

复述提示：`be comfortable and have as few distractions as possible` → `apartment is noisy` → `public library`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**Putting the phone away and choosing one clear task can make the environment much more useful for getting some work done.**

## Q3

### How can young people be encouraged to read more?

侧重点：解决｜兴趣与门槛。主回答 71 词。

I think the best approach is to **make reading accessible and let young people choose material they actually enjoy**. For example, someone whose apartment is noisy can study English in a public library without having to buy a drink or order food. **The same principle applies to younger readers: easy access helps, but the books need to match their interests. Short stories, comics or practical guides can be useful starting points.**

复述提示：`make reading accessible and let young people choose material they actually enjoy` → `apartment is noisy` → `public library`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**If reading always feels like another test, they may be less willing to do it in their free time.**

## Q4

### How might libraries change in the future?

侧重点：预测｜纸质与数字并存。主回答 64 词。

I think libraries may **combine quiet reading areas with more support for digital learning**. For example, someone whose apartment is noisy can study English in a public library without having to buy a drink or order food. **That need for space is unlikely to disappear, even if more books become digital. Some libraries might also offer help with online research or basic computer skills.**

复述提示：`combine quiet reading areas with more support for digital learning` → `apartment is noisy` → `public library`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**However, they should keep genuinely quiet areas, because adding more activities could make the building less useful for visitors who mainly want to concentrate.**
