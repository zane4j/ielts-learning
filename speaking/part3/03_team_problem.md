## Q1

### Why is teamwork important in the workplace?

侧重点：原因｜并行分工。主回答 81 词。

I think teamwork matters because **people can share the workload instead of leaving one person to handle everything**. For example, when an app becomes slow, one colleague can check the logs while another checks the database. **Both parts of the problem can be investigated at the same time. The colleagues can then compare what they find and agree on a solution. This can save time and reduce individual pressure, especially when customers are waiting and the team needs to respond quickly.**

复述提示：`people can share the workload instead of leaving one person to handle everything` → `check the logs` → `checks the database`

## Q2

### What qualities should a good leader have?

侧重点：特征｜冷静与安排。主回答 82 词。

I'd say a good leader needs to **stay calm and give people clear responsibilities when something goes wrong**. For example, when an app becomes slow, one colleague can check the logs while another checks the database. **The leader should make that division clear and check whether anyone needs support. Otherwise, everyone might rush into the same task while another important task gets ignored. Being the leader doesn't mean knowing every answer; it means helping the team use its skills effectively under pressure.**

复述提示：`stay calm and give people clear responsibilities when something goes wrong` → `check the logs` → `checks the database`

## Q3

### Why is communication important at work?

侧重点：原因｜共享发现。主回答 83 词。

I think communication is important because **people need to understand what others have already done and what still needs attention**. For example, when an app becomes slow, one colleague can check the logs while another checks the database. **If they don't share their findings, they may investigate the same issue repeatedly or miss a useful connection. Brief updates help them combine the evidence and decide what to do next. That is why clear communication can be just as useful as individual technical knowledge.**

复述提示：`people need to understand what others have already done and what still needs attention` → `check the logs` → `checks the database`

## Q4

### Is it always better to make decisions quickly?

侧重点：评价｜速度与检查。主回答 83 词。

Not always. I think **the right speed depends on the consequences of making a mistake**. For example, when an app becomes slow, one colleague can check the logs while another checks the database. **They need to respond quickly, but they should still test the fix before releasing it. A rushed change could create another problem and affect more users. So I would separate acting promptly from skipping necessary checks. In an urgent situation, a short, careful check can still be worth the time.**

复述提示：`the right speed depends on the consequences of making a mistake` → `check the logs` → `checks the database`
