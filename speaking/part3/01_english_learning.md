## Q1

### Why do adults learn new skills?

侧重点：原因｜职业需求。主回答 78 词。

I'd say many adults learn new skills **to create more career opportunities, especially when their current job offers little room for growth**. For example, a working adult can take a short English lesson before work and get feedback from a teacher. **Regular practice can gradually make job interviews in English less stressful. The important point is that the skill has a clear purpose. That gives people a reason to keep studying, even when they are busy or tired.**

复述提示：`to create more career opportunities, especially when their current job offers little room for growth` → `short English lesson` → `feedback from a teacher`

## Q2

### What qualities make someone a good teacher?

侧重点：特征｜具体反馈。主回答 82 词。

I'd say a good teacher should **be patient and explain clearly what a learner needs to improve**. For example, a working adult can take a short English lesson before work and get feedback from a teacher. **Instead of simply saying an answer is wrong, the teacher can offer a better expression and let the learner try again. This makes the advice easier to use. Encouragement matters too, because people may stop practicing if they feel embarrassed every time they make a mistake.**

复述提示：`be patient and explain clearly what a learner needs to improve` → `short English lesson` → `feedback from a teacher`

## Q3

### What are the advantages of online learning?

侧重点：利弊｜时间便利。主回答 82 词。

I think the main advantage is **that learning can fit around people's existing schedules**. For example, a working adult can take a short English lesson before work and get feedback from a teacher. **With online lessons, there is no need to travel to a language school, so even a small amount of free time can be useful. However, learners still need a quiet space and a reliable connection. The convenience helps, but it doesn't automatically mean they will concentrate or practice regularly.**

复述提示：`that learning can fit around people's existing schedules` → `short English lesson` → `feedback from a teacher`

## Q4

### How can people stay motivated when learning something difficult?

侧重点：解决｜短目标与进步。主回答 84 词。

I think people should **set small goals and make their progress visible**. For example, a working adult can take a short English lesson before work and get feedback from a teacher. **Rather than expecting to speak fluently immediately, the learner could aim to explain one familiar topic more clearly each week. When they can hear the improvement in a recording, the effort feels worthwhile. A regular appointment also helps turn studying into a habit instead of something they only do when they feel motivated.**

复述提示：`set small goals and make their progress visible` → `short English lesson` → `feedback from a teacher`
