## Q1

### Why do people enjoy spending time in nature?

侧重点：原因｜环境切换。主回答 69 词。

I think nature appeals to people because **it gives them a change from busy streets and constant screen use**. For example, after a stressful week, an office worker can put their phone away and walk along a shaded path in a nearby park. **There is no message to answer or task to finish for a while. Paying attention to trees and birds can make the afternoon feel less rushed.**

复述提示：`it gives them a change from busy streets and constant screen use` → `put their phone away` → `a shaded path`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**For many people, that simple change of pace is the attraction, rather than doing anything exciting or expensive.**

## Q2

### Should cities spend more money on public parks?

侧重点：评价｜可负担的休闲。主回答 66 词。

Generally, yes, because **parks offer a place to relax without requiring people to buy anything**. For example, after a stressful week, an office worker can put their phone away and walk along a shaded path in a nearby park. **Families and older residents can use the same space for different activities. However, spending should include maintenance, lighting and accessible paths, rather than just an impressive entrance.**

复述提示：`parks offer a place to relax without requiring people to buy anything` → `put their phone away` → `a shaded path`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**A modest park that people can safely use every day may be more valuable than a beautiful space that is difficult to reach.**

## Q3

### How can cities encourage people to spend more time outdoors?

侧重点：解决｜减少实际障碍。主回答 72 词。

I think cities should **make outdoor spaces convenient and comfortable to use**. For example, after a stressful week, an office worker can put their phone away and walk along a shaded path in a nearby park. **That is much easier when the park is within walking distance and has benches, shade and safe crossings nearby. Simply telling residents to go outside is less useful if reaching a green space takes an hour.**

复述提示：`make outdoor spaces convenient and comfortable to use` → `put their phone away` → `a shaded path`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**Removing those practical barriers could make short outdoor visits part of people's normal routines.**

## Q4

### Do people of different ages use parks differently?

侧重点：比较｜活动需求。主回答 65 词。

I'd say they often do, although **personal interests matter as well as age**. For example, after a stressful week, an office worker can put their phone away and walk along a shaded path in a nearby park. **A family with young children might look for a playground, while some older visitors may prefer benches and a place to meet friends. These activities require different facilities.**

复述提示：`personal interests matter as well as age` → `put their phone away` → `a shaded path`

可选补充（需要更完整的限制或收尾时再加）：**So a well-designed park should offer several choices, rather than assuming that everyone wants to exercise in exactly the same way.**
