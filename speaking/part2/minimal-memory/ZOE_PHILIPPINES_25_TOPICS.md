## P01 — 做成困难事情的人

**Topic:** Describe a person who achieved something difficult.

**Cue Card:** Who this person is / What difficult thing they achieved / How they succeeded / And explain why you admire this achievement.

### 1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who managed to overcome a **difficult challenge**. Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

### 2 — WHAT

Speaking of what she achieved, the most impressive thing about her is that she became confident enough to **interview foreign travellers** in English. She had struggled with English for years and used to feel nervous whenever someone asked her a question.

At first, she wasn't confident speaking English, especially when talking to foreigners. Instead of giving up, she decided to create an **immersive English-speaking environment**. She watched English videos, practised speaking every day and tried to use English whenever she could. After several months, she made significant progress and became much more confident. Later, she shared her learning experience in a travel video, which left a deep impression on me.

### 3 — WHY / HOW

When it comes to why I admire this achievement, she **kept practising** even when she made mistakes, and eventually used English in her travel videos. At that time, I was considering quitting my job to go to the Philippines to study English, but I was afraid of losing my stable income.

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. Her experience encouraged me to take my **plan to study English in the Philippines** more seriously.

**1 分钟准备：5 行关键词对应 5 段**

1. **difficult challenge**
2. **interview foreign travellers**
3. **immersive English-speaking environment**
4. **kept practising**
5. **plan to study English in the Philippines**

**扣题核对：**困难：英语交流；行动：沉浸学习；结果：能采访外国旅客。
