## P16 — Describe a person who is good at learning languages

### 1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a travel content creator who is really good at learning languages.

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter, and she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

### 2 — WHAT

Speaking of how she learns languages, the most impressive thing about her is that she believes the environment plays a key role in language learning.

She tries to create an immersive English-speaking environment by watching English videos, speaking with foreigners and using English in her daily life. At first, she wasn't confident speaking English, but after months of practice, she made significant progress.

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she explained this method and encouraged her followers to use English every day.

### 3 — WHY / HOW

When it comes to why I admire her, I think her method is both practical and inspiring.

At that time, I was considering quitting my job to go to the Philippines to study English, but I was afraid of losing my stable income. Her video encouraged me to take the plan more seriously and made me realise that I could step outside my comfort zone.

### 1 分钟准备

`Zoe → learning languages → immersive environment → daily English → progress → Philippines / income worry`

**题卡检查：** Zoe 是谁？会什么语言（这里至少明确英语；若原题用复数 languages，应补一门实际会的语言）；怎样学习？我为什么欣赏？新写的 Zoe 学习成果属于练习设定。

## P01 — Describe a person who achieved something difficult

### 1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a travel content creator who managed to overcome a major challenge.

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

### 2 — WHAT

Speaking of what she achieved, the most impressive thing about her is that she managed to become confident in English after struggling with it for years.

At first, she felt nervous when talking to foreigners and could hardly keep a conversation going. Instead of giving up, she created an immersive English-speaking environment. She watched English videos, spoke English every day and kept practising even when she made mistakes.

After several months, she became confident enough to interview travelers in English for her videos. That was a big achievement for her.

### 3 — WHY / HOW

When it comes to why I admire her, I think it's mainly because she didn't give up when things got difficult.

At that time, I was considering quitting my job to go to the Philippines to study English, but I was worried about losing my stable income. Her experience encouraged me to take the plan more seriously and step outside my comfort zone.

### 1 分钟准备

`Zoe → poor English → immersive environment → daily practice → interviews in English → inspired my Philippines plan`

**题卡检查：** 要明确困难是什么、怎么克服、成果是什么；单靠“百万粉丝”不足以解释如何做成困难事。

## 使用规则

1. **保留句：** she created an immersive English-speaking environment / she practised every day / her experience inspired me / I was considering going to the Philippines.
2. **切换主角：** 人物成功题主角是 Zoe；决定、想法、目标等题主角可能是“我”；视频题主体是 video。
3. **不要把因果链顺序乱改：** 学习方法 → 取得进步 → 分享视频 → 我受到启发。
4. **不要把所有人物身份全部叠加到同一人设：**运动员、医护职业、童年好友等用相互独立情景，不能声称这些全是一个 Zoe 已发生的经历。
5. **目标是尽可能少的独立事件和句子**，不是用一个万能结尾代替 Topic 真正要求的内容。
