P15｜有成功生意的人

Describe a person who has a successful business. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
关键词完整稿Cue Card改动说明通用模板

1 分钟准备 · 6 组关键词

  1. 人物 / 身份：online travel-accessories business
  2. 核心特点：followers kept asking
  3. 视频 / 场景：packing cubes
  4. 动作 / 方法：customer feedback
  5. 结果 / 细节：steady group of customers
  6. 原因 / 感受：practical idea

完整英文口语稿

粗体＝固定框架，浅绿＝关键词；五段换行。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is a popular content creator and the owner of a small online travel-accessories business.

To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair.

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of how she built her business, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares travel tips, product ideas and lessons from running a shop.

She started selling useful travel items after her followers kept asking where to buy them.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about the first year of her online shop.

She explained that she began with a few products, such as packing cubes and simple travel organizers. She tested each item on her own trips and listened to customer feedback. At first, sales were slow, but she kept improving the products. After a year, she had a steady group of customers and was able to hire someone to help with orders.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to why I consider her business successful, she turned a practical idea into a growing business by listening to what people needed.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.
