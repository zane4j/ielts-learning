19｜有健康习惯和作息的人

2609 02-12 · Describe a person you know who has healthy habits and a healthy daily routine

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person you know who has healthy habits and a healthy daily routine

叙述焦点： 具体健康习惯、维持方式、影响

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who has a disciplined and healthy daily routine.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what her healthy habits are,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share her morning routine, exercise and everyday life.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about how she balances exercise, work and sleep during a busy week.

She usually gets up at a regular time, does some stretching or badminton practice and prepares simple meals at home. She also tries not to stay up too late, even when she has videos to edit. What impressed me was that she focuses on simple habits she can maintain rather than extreme fitness plans.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I admire her healthy lifestyle,

● SDa: reasons

She is self-disciplined and understands that keeping healthy habits requires consistency.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. Her routine has encouraged me to spend less time looking at my phone late at night and to exercise more regularly.

考前核实： 健康习惯与视频内容为练习设定；确保不与“常熬夜赶工”人物设定冲突。
