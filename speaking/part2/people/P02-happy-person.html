P02｜快乐的人

Describe a happy person you know. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
题卡要点1 分钟关键词英文完整稿改动说明通用模板

01 / Cue Card 检查

Describe a happy person you know.

  • Who this person is
  • How you know them
  • What shows they are happy
  • Why you think they are happy

按照仓库现有练习题意整理，非逐字官方题卡。

02 / 考前 1 分钟关键词

按下列 6 个提示在草稿纸上做简短记录。关键词已在完整英文原文里用浅绿色背景高亮，请练习只看提示复述。

  1. 人物 / 身份：cheerful lifestyle blogger
  2. 核心特点：positive attitude
  3. 视频 / 场景：missed a train
  4. 动作 / 方法：explored the streets
  5. 结果 / 细节：laughed about the whole experience
  6. 原因 / 感受：something positive

03 / 完整英文口语稿

只按五段框架换行；同一段内部不人为断行。粗体＝固定模板，浅绿＝本题关键词。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is a cheerful lifestyle blogger I follow on Douyin. To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair. Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of her positive attitude, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares funny moments from her daily life and trips. She smiles a lot and seems to enjoy even the small things.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about a trip that did not go as planned. She said she had missed a train and had to spend the afternoon in a small town. Instead of getting upset, she explored the streets, tried some local food and took funny pictures. She laughed about the whole experience and told her followers that unexpected changes can be interesting. Her energy made me smile, too.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to why I think she is such a happy person, she doesn't let small problems ruin her day, and she always looks for something positive.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.

04 / Topic 改动点

以她面对意外仍然快乐的具体反应，而不是只说笑容，来证明 happy。

固定 Zoe 的外貌、性格和五段式句型，主要替换身份说明、视频主题、故事动作与 WHY / FEELING。人物经历为练习性设定，请按自己的实际认识和熟悉的素材调整。
