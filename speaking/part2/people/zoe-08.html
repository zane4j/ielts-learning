08｜本地名人

2609 02-1 · Describe a famous person in your local area

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a famous person in your local area

叙述焦点： 本地联系、本地受欢迎的原因

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who is also well known in my local area in Shenzhen.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of why she became well known locally,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share local food, everyday life and travel around Shenzhen.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about visiting small restaurants and hidden places in Shenzhen.

She spoke to a few local shop owners and explained what made their businesses special. The video received a lot of attention because local viewers recognized the streets and restaurants. I liked that she focused on ordinary places rather than only famous tourist attractions.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I think she deserves local recognition,

● SDa: reasons

She is really hard-working and spends a lot of time and effort on things that she is keen on, which really inspires me.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. I think she is popular locally because she helps people rediscover their own city and gives small local businesses more attention.

考前核实： 必须确保她与本地确有联系；深圳知名度不能只用全国粉丝数证明。
