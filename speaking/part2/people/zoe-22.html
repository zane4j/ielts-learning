22｜在成功公司工作的人

2605 02-3 · Describe a person you know who works in a successful company

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person you know who works in a successful company

叙述焦点： 公司做什么、她的职位、工作体验

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who works as the creative director of a successful small media company.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what the company does and what her role is,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share her team, company projects and the process of creating videos.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about how her media company works with brands and produces short videos.

She explained that the company has a small team responsible for planning, filming, editing and communicating with clients. Her job is to develop creative ideas and make sure projects are delivered on schedule. I could relate to the teamwork because I also work on projects with colleagues in a technology company.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I think she enjoys working there,

● SDa: reasons

She enjoys working with creative people and seeing a project develop from an idea into something customers can use.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. I admire how she combines leadership with practical work, and I think that is one reason the company has built a good reputation.

考前核实： 这里的 successful company 可以是 Zoe 自己联合创办并任职的公司，但需公司实际成功的证据。
