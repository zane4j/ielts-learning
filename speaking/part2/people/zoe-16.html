2609 02-9 · Describe a person who taught you a new skill

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person who taught you a new skill

叙述焦点： 技能是什么、怎么教、怎么实践

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who taught me a useful skill through her online videos.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what she taught me and how I learned it,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share video production, photography and practical tutorials.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about how to record better travel videos using only a phone.

She explained three simple steps: choose good natural light, keep the camera steady and record several short clips instead of one long video. I followed her instructions during a weekend trip and managed to make a short video that looked much better than my earlier attempts.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to how I felt after learning the skill,

● SDa: reasons

I appreciate her teaching style because she explains everything clearly and makes difficult things feel manageable.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. The skill is practical, and I still use her tips whenever I record videos while travelling.

考前核实： 必须说明“具体技能＋教学步骤＋实际学会”；线上教学也可以符合 taught you。
