14｜学习并喜欢历史的人

2609 02-7 · Describe a person who learns history and loves history

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person who learns history and loves history

叙述焦点： 学习历史的方法、历史例子、个人影响

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who is also very interested in learning about history.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of how she studies history and why she enjoys it,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share historical places, local traditions and travel experiences.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about the history of an old neighbourhood she visited.

Before the trip, she read articles and watched documentaries about the area. In the video, she explained how the buildings had changed over time and why some streets were important to local people. I was surprised because it felt more like a short history lesson than a typical travel vlog.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I admire her interest in history,

● SDa: reasons

I like that she does not visit historical places just to take pictures. She tries to understand the stories behind them.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. Her enthusiasm has made me more interested in learning about the history of places I visit.

考前核实： 历史爱好需具体学习方式：书、纪录片、历史场所。
