P05｜敬佩的年长者

Describe an older person you admire. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
关键词完整稿Cue Card改动说明通用模板

1 分钟准备 · 6 组关键词

  1. 人物 / 身份：in her fifties
  2. 核心特点：getting older
  3. 视频 / 场景：too old to learn
  4. 动作 / 方法：went to the Philippines
  5. 结果 / 细节：laughed at herself
  6. 原因 / 感受：age should not stop anyone

完整英文口语稿

粗体＝固定框架，浅绿＝关键词；五段换行。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is a content creator in her fifties and someone I got to know through Douyin. To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair. Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of her attitude towards getting older, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares her travels, language lessons and experiences of starting new things. Although she is in her fifties, she keeps challenging herself.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about her decision to study English abroad. She explained that she once worried she was too old to learn a new language. But she went to the Philippines, attended English classes and practiced speaking with younger students. In the video, she made mistakes and laughed at herself instead of giving up. Seeing her try something new at that age really stayed with me.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to why I admire this older person, she reminds me that age should not stop anyone from learning or following a dream.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.
