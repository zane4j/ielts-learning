2605 02-5 · Describe a person who loves to grow plants at home or in the garden

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person who loves to grow plants at home or in the garden

叙述焦点： 植物品种、养护方式、为什么喜欢

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who also loves growing plants on her balcony.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what she grows and why she enjoys gardening,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share her home garden, daily routine and DIY projects.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about growing tomatoes and herbs in handmade wooden planters on her balcony.

She showed viewers how she prepared the soil, watered the plants and made sure they got enough sunlight. She seemed genuinely excited when the first tomatoes started to grow. She said looking after plants helped her relax after long hours of filming and editing videos.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I think she enjoys growing plants,

● SDa: reasons

She is patient and likes seeing small improvements over time, which is probably why gardening suits her so well.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. Watching her care for those plants made me realize that a simple hobby can be a good way to slow down and take a break from screens.

考前核实： 园艺设定可与手工花盆 Example 复用；需补充植物、养护过程和乐趣。
