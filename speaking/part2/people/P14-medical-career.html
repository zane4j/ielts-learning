P14｜想从事医疗行业的人

Describe a person who would like to work in the medical field. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
题卡要点1 分钟关键词英文完整稿改动说明通用模板

01 / Cue Card 检查

Describe a person who would like to work in the medical field.

  • Who this person is
  • What medical career they want
  • How they are preparing
  • Why the career interests them

按照仓库现有练习题意整理，非逐字官方题卡。

02 / 考前 1 分钟关键词

按下列 6 个提示在草稿纸上做简短记录。关键词已在完整英文原文里用浅绿色背景高亮，请练习只看提示复述。

  1. 人物 / 身份：healthcare assistant
  2. 核心特点：qualified nurse
  3. 视频 / 场景：evening classes
  4. 动作 / 方法：communicating with patients
  5. 结果 / 细节：elderly patient
  6. 原因 / 感受：support people

03 / 完整英文口语稿

只按五段框架换行；同一段内部不人为断行。粗体＝固定模板，浅绿＝本题关键词。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is a lifestyle creator and a healthcare assistant hoping to become a nurse. To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair. Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of her plan to work as a nurse, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares her studies, hospital experiences and everyday life. She wants to become a qualified nurse because she enjoys caring for people.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about how she prepares for nursing school. She explained that she takes evening classes while working during the day. She reads basic medical textbooks, practices communicating with patients and asks experienced nurses for advice. In one video, she talked about helping an elderly patient feel less nervous before a check-up. She said that small moments like that make her want to continue.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to why this career matters to her, she wants a job where she can support people when they are worried or unwell.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.

04 / Topic 改动点

职业方向、具体准备和动机都要明确。这是 Zoe 的医疗职业变体。

固定 Zoe 的外貌、性格和五段式句型，主要替换身份说明、视频主题、故事动作与 WHY / FEELING。人物经历为练习性设定，请按自己的实际认识和熟悉的素材调整。
