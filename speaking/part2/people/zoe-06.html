2605 01-2 · Describe a time when you sent a message or an email to someone but received no reply for a long time

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a time when you sent a message or an email to someone but received no reply for a long time

叙述焦点： 发送内容、等多久、感受、最终怎样

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator to whom I once sent a message and waited weeks for a reply.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what I wrote and why the reply took so long,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share her travels and experiences of trying new things.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she talked about studying and travelling abroad, so I decided to send her a private message.

I asked whether taking a career break to improve my English was worth the risk. At first, I checked my phone quite often, but I heard nothing for almost three weeks. I felt slightly disappointed, although I knew that someone with over a million followers must receive many messages. Eventually, she replied briefly and suggested that I plan my budget carefully.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to how I felt about waiting for her reply,

● SDa: reasons

I understood that she was probably very busy, so I did not take the delay personally.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. The experience reminded me not to expect an immediate response, especially from someone with a large online audience.

考前核实： 长时间未回复为必须呈现的事实；私信及最终回复是练习设定。
