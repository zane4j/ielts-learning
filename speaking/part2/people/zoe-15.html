2609 02-8 · Describe a person who is good at making things by hand

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person who is good at making things by hand

叙述焦点： 做了什么、具体步骤、手工能力

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who is also really good at making simple things by hand.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what kind of handmade things she makes,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share DIY projects and creative ideas for her videos.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about making a wooden planter for the small garden on her balcony.

She measured the pieces carefully, put them together and decorated the planter with a simple design. It did not look expensive, but the result was neat and practical. What impressed me most was that she could turn a basic idea into something useful without buying a ready-made product.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I think she is good with her hands,

● SDa: reasons

She pays attention to small details and does not give up when something takes several attempts.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. Her DIY videos make me want to try a small project myself instead of always buying things online.

考前核实： 新增例子：DIY 木质花盆；可与园艺题复用，属于练习设定。
