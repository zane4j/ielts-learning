P06｜擅长手工的人

Describe a person who is good at making things by hand. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
关键词完整稿Cue Card改动说明通用模板

1 分钟准备 · 6 组关键词

  1. 人物 / 身份：DIY videos
  2. 核心特点：making things by hand
  3. 视频 / 场景：miniature house
  4. 动作 / 方法：cut the cardboard
  5. 结果 / 细节：tiny windows
  6. 原因 / 感受：getting the details right

完整英文口语稿

粗体＝固定框架，浅绿＝关键词；五段换行。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is a lifestyle creator known for her DIY videos. To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair. Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of her skill at making things by hand, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares simple handmade decorations and creative home projects. She is patient and always pays attention to tiny details.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about making a miniature house out of cardboard. First, she drew a design and cut the cardboard into small pieces. Then she glued the walls together and painted the roof. She even made tiny windows and a little garden outside. It took her several hours, but the finished house looked surprisingly realistic. What I liked was that she explained each step slowly enough for beginners to follow.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to why I think she is good at handicrafts, she is creative, careful and willing to spend time getting the details right.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.

  • What they make
