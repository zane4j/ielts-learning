通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a time when someone talked about something you were not interested in but you kept listening

叙述焦点： 话题是什么、为什么没兴趣、为何继续听

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator whose livestream I once watched even though I was not interested in the subject.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what she talked about and why I kept listening,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share travel videos and advice about content creation.

● example

One livestream that left the deepest impression on me was when she spent nearly half an hour talking about camera lenses and technical settings.

To be honest, I am not particularly interested in camera equipment, and I almost left the livestream. However, I stayed because she explained the topic patiently, and I thought I might learn something useful for recording videos on my own trips. By the end, I understood why lighting can matter more than expensive equipment.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to how I felt about listening to something unfamiliar,

● SDa: reasons

I was a little bored at first, but I appreciated how clearly she explained difficult ideas.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. It taught me that a topic does not have to be exciting to be useful. I was glad I kept listening.

考前核实： 核心是“不感兴趣但继续听”的行为，不是单纯夸 Zoe。
