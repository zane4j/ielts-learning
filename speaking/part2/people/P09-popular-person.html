P09｜受欢迎的人

Describe a popular person you know. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
关键词完整稿Cue Card改动说明通用模板

1 分钟准备 · 6 组关键词

  1. 人物 / 身份：most popular creators
  2. 核心特点：almost every week
  3. 视频 / 场景：questions from followers
  4. 动作 / 方法：free travel checklist
  5. 结果 / 细节：warm and approachable
  6. 原因 / 感受：listens to people

完整英文口语稿

粗体＝固定框架，浅绿＝关键词；五段换行。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is one of the most popular creators I follow on Douyin. To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair. Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of why so many people like her, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares helpful travel advice, everyday moments and honest personal stories. I watch her videos almost every week, and she replies to her followers in a friendly way.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about her answering questions from followers. A lot of people asked how she planned trips on a budget. She collected their questions and made a detailed video instead of giving short answers. She explained how to save on transport and shared a free travel checklist. The comment section was full of people thanking her. She seemed warm and approachable, even with such a large audience.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to why she is popular, she is not just entertaining; she listens to people and shares advice they can actually use.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.
