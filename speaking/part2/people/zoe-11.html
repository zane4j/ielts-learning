11｜钦佩的运动员

2609 02-4 · Describe a successful sportsperson you admire

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a successful sportsperson you admire

叙述焦点： 赛事成绩、训练、比赛细节、钦佩原因

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who is also a successful badminton player.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of her achievements in badminton and why I admire her,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share her badminton training, competitions and daily life.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about winning a regional badminton doubles tournament.

She explained that she had trained regularly for months while managing her online channel. During the final, she and her partner lost the first game, but they stayed focused, adjusted their strategy and came back to win. I enjoy playing badminton myself, so I could understand how much discipline and teamwork that required.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I admire her as a sportsperson,

● SDa: reasons

I admire the way she stays focused under pressure and continues training even when her schedule is busy.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. Her experience reminds me that success in sport takes patience, teamwork and consistent practice.

考前核实： 需给 Zoe 明确真实比赛成绩；“网红也打羽毛球”本身不足以证明是成功运动员。
