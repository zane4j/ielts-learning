2605 02-1 · Describe a friend from your childhood

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a friend from your childhood

叙述焦点： 小时候怎样认识、一起做什么、现在关系

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who was also one of my closest childhood friends.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of how we became friends and what we did together,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share her daily life, travels and memories of growing up.

● example

One memory that left the deepest impression on me was playing badminton with her after school when we were children.

We lived in the same neighbourhood and often spent weekends playing sports or talking about our favourite TV programmes. We lost touch for a few years after leaving school, but later reconnected online. I was surprised to find that she had become a successful content creator. Despite that, she still talks to me in the same friendly way.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why this childhood friendship matters to me,

● SDa: reasons

I have always liked her positive attitude and the way she treats old friends, even after becoming successful.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. She reminds me of a simpler period of my life, so I hope we can stay in touch for many years.

考前核实： 与“只见过一次的人”在关系上不兼容：本题采用独立的童年好友版本。
