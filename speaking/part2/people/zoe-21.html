2605 02-2 · Describe a person you know who has a successful business

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person you know who has a successful business

叙述焦点： 业务内容、怎样发展、为何成功

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who also runs a successful small media business.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what her business does and why it is successful,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share travel content, online courses and behind-the-scenes business updates.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about starting a small content production studio with a friend.

At first, they worked from a tiny rented office and handled most tasks themselves. Later, they started offering video production services and online courses to paying customers. Over time, the studio grew into a small team with steady projects. I was impressed that she learned how to manage clients and budgets instead of relying only on her online popularity.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I think her business has done well,

● SDa: reasons

She is ambitious and willing to put in the effort to turn creative ideas into a practical service.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. Her experience shows me that a successful business depends on useful products, reliable service and consistent work, not just a large number of followers.

考前核实： 必须有实际经营主体与商业结果；百万粉丝不等于商业成功。
