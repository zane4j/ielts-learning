通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a time when a person did something to help you solve a problem

叙述焦点： 你的困难、Zoe 如何实际帮助、结果

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who once helped me deal with a difficult personal decision.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what my problem was and how she helped,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share travel experiences and advice about personal growth.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about taking a short break from work to explore new opportunities.

At that time, I was considering quitting my job to study English in the Philippines, but I was afraid of losing my stable income. I asked her a question during a livestream. She suggested comparing the cost of studying abroad with my savings and setting a clear goal before making a decision. I followed her advice and made a realistic budget and study plan.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to how I felt about the help she gave me,

● SDa: reasons

I appreciated that she offered practical steps rather than simply telling me to be brave.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. Her advice helped me make a more informed decision and feel much less anxious about my future.

考前核实： “帮助解决问题”需要具体互动；直播提问和答复均为练习设定。
