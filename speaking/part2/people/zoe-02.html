02｜快乐人士

2609 01-2 · Describe a happy person you know

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a happy person you know

叙述焦点： 快乐的具体表现、例子、对自己的影响

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who always seems cheerful and full of energy.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of why she seems to be such a happy person,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share daily life, positive moments and travel experiences.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about a trip where she tried local food and explored a new city.

She kept smiling and laughing when things did not go exactly as planned. For example, she got lost on her way to a restaurant but treated it as a chance to discover an interesting street. Watching her enjoy such a simple experience made me feel less stressed after work.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I think she is a happy person,

● SDa: reasons

What I like most is that she seems to enjoy the little things rather than expecting everything to be perfect.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. Her positive attitude reminds me to enjoy everyday experiences instead of worrying about every small problem.

考前核实： 从视频中只能判断她“看起来快乐”；请用实际可见的行为支撑。
