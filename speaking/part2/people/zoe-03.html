03｜尊敬的比你年长的人

2609 01-3 · Describe someone who is older than you that you admire

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe someone who is older than you that you admire

叙述焦点： 年龄关系、成就、值得尊敬的品质

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who is a few years older than me and whom I really admire.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of why I look up to her,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share her daily life, career and experience of learning new things.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about how she changed her career direction in her thirties.

Instead of staying in a comfortable routine, she spent months learning video production and gradually built a successful channel. As someone who is also thinking about changing jobs, I could understand why that decision must have been difficult.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I admire her as an older person,

● SDa: reasons

She is really hard-working and spends a lot of time and effort on things that she is keen on, which really inspires me.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. I admire her maturity and determination, and I hope I can stay open to new opportunities as I get older.

考前核实： 前提：Zoe 确实比你年长；职业转换为练习设定。
