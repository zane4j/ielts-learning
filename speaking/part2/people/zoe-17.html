2609 02-10 · Describe a course that impressed you a lot

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a course that impressed you a lot

叙述焦点： 课程内容、学习形式、印象深刻的原因

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who created an online course that impressed me a lot.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what the course was about and why it impressed me,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share travel photography and short video production.

● example

One lesson that left the deepest impression on me was when she showed us how to turn a few ordinary phone clips into a short travel video.

The course had short lessons, clear examples and small tasks we could practise immediately. I learned how to arrange clips, improve the lighting and add simple subtitles. After finishing one assignment, I realized that making a decent video was not as difficult as I had expected.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why this course impressed me,

● SDa: reasons

I liked that the lessons focused on practical skills instead of long explanations that were hard to apply.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. The course gave me confidence to try something creative outside my normal programming work, so I still remember it clearly.

考前核实： 课程是主角，Zoe 只是授课者；新增在线课程为练习设定。
