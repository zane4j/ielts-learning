P04｜爱拍照的人

Describe a person you know who enjoys taking photos. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
关键词完整稿Cue Card改动说明通用模板

1 分钟准备 · 6 组关键词

  1. 人物 / 身份：keen photographer
  2. 核心特点：interest in photography
  3. 视频 / 场景：soft morning light
  4. 动作 / 方法：old buildings
  5. 结果 / 细节：small change in angle
  6. 原因 / 感受：everyday places look special

完整英文口语稿

粗体＝固定框架，浅绿＝关键词；五段换行。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is a travel content creator and a keen photographer.

To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair.

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of her interest in photography, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares photos of city streets, natural scenery and local people.

She takes her camera almost everywhere and pays attention to light and small details.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about taking photos during a trip to an old town.

She got up early to catch the soft morning light. She walked through narrow streets and photographed old buildings, markets and ordinary people going about their day. She then compared the original pictures with her edited versions and explained how a small change in angle could make a big difference. The final photos looked simple but beautiful.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to how I feel about her interest, her photos make everyday places look special, and they encourage me to notice more details when I travel.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.

Describe a person you know who enjoys taking photos.

  • Who this person is and how you know them
  • Where they take photos
  • What they photograph
  • How you feel about their interest
