2609 02-5 · Describe a person you know who is very organized

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person you know who is very organized

叙述焦点： 如何有条理、具体行为、效果

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who is extremely organized when managing her video projects.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of how she organizes her work,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share travel videos and the process of making them.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about preparing a detailed filming schedule for a trip.

She had a checklist for locations, equipment and editing tasks, and she kept a backup plan in case something went wrong. When her camera stopped working, she was able to switch to her phone without losing the whole day. That made me realize how useful good planning can be.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why being organized matters to her,

● SDa: reasons

She is really hard-working and spends a lot of time and effort on things that she is keen on, which really inspires me.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. I admire how she turns complicated work into manageable steps, and I have started using checklists more often at work.

考前核实： 复用 Example B；必须突出计划/清单/备用方案，不只说努力。
