P12｜喜欢在家种植物的人

Describe a person who enjoys growing plants at home or in a garden. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
题卡要点1 分钟关键词英文完整稿改动说明通用模板

01 / Cue Card 检查

Describe a person who enjoys growing plants at home or in a garden.

  • Who this person is
  • What they grow and where
  • How they care for plants
  • Why they enjoy growing plants

按照仓库现有练习题意整理，非逐字官方题卡。

02 / 考前 1 分钟关键词

按下列 6 个提示在草稿纸上做简短记录。关键词已在完整英文原文里用浅绿色背景高亮，请练习只看提示复述。

  1. 人物 / 身份：plants on her balcony
  2. 核心特点：green corner
  3. 视频 / 场景：basil and mint
  4. 动作 / 方法：checked the soil
  5. 结果 / 细节：new leaves
  6. 原因 / 感受：slow down and relax

03 / 完整英文口语稿

只按五段框架换行；同一段内部不人为断行。粗体＝固定模板，浅绿＝本题关键词。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is a lifestyle creator who loves growing plants on her balcony. To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair. Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of her love of home gardening, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares videos about flowers, herbs and caring for plants at home. She has turned a small balcony into a green corner.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about growing basil and mint at home. She showed how she put small pots near the window so the plants could get sunlight. She watered them carefully, checked the soil and removed dry leaves. A few weeks later, she filmed the new leaves and even used some mint to make a drink. I could tell she was excited about seeing the plants grow.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to why she enjoys growing plants, looking after them helps her slow down and relax after a busy day.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.

04 / Topic 改动点

直接回答种什么、种在哪里、怎么照顾、为什么喜欢。

固定 Zoe 的外貌、性格和五段式句型，主要替换身份说明、视频主题、故事动作与 WHY / FEELING。人物经历为练习性设定，请按自己的实际认识和熟悉的素材调整。
