P08｜教你新技能的人

Describe a person who taught you a new skill. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
题卡要点1 分钟关键词英文完整稿改动说明通用模板

01 / Cue Card 检查

Describe a person who taught you a new skill.

  • Who this person is
  • What skill they taught you
  • How they taught you and how you practiced
  • What you gained from learning the skill

按照仓库现有练习题意整理，非逐字官方题卡。

02 / 考前 1 分钟关键词

按下列 6 个提示在草稿纸上做简短记录。关键词已在完整英文原文里用浅绿色背景高亮，请练习只看提示复述。

  1. 人物 / 身份：video-editing tutorials
  2. 核心特点：clear
  3. 视频 / 场景：add subtitles
  4. 动作 / 方法：paused the video
  5. 结果 / 细节：first short travel video
  6. 原因 / 感受：turn my travel clips into a short story

03 / 完整英文口语稿

只按五段框架换行；同一段内部不人为断行。粗体＝固定模板，浅绿＝本题关键词。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is a content creator famous for practical video-editing tutorials. To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair. Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of how she teaches people new skills, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares easy-to-follow tutorials for making short videos. Her instructions are clear, even for complete beginners like me.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about editing a travel video on my phone. She showed how to import clips, cut out boring parts and add subtitles using a simple editing app. I paused the video after every step and tried it with my own travel footage. At first, the subtitles were out of sync, so I replayed that part. Finally, I finished my first short travel video and sent it to a friend. I felt genuinely proud.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to what I gained from learning this skill, I can now turn my travel clips into a short story instead of leaving them untouched on my phone.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.

04 / Topic 改动点

关键是她怎样教、你怎样练、你最终会做什么，不能只讲视频好看。

固定 Zoe 的外貌、性格和五段式句型，主要替换身份说明、视频主题、故事动作与 WHY / FEELING。人物经历为练习性设定，请按自己的实际认识和熟悉的素材调整。
