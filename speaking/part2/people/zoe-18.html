2609 02-11 · Describe a time you watched a famous person being interviewed

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a time you watched a famous person being interviewed

叙述焦点： 采访时间场景、问答内容、感受

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who was once interviewed on a popular online programme.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what the interview was about and what I learned,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share her career, travels and experience of running a channel.

● example

One interview that left the deepest impression on me was when she talked about building her career while dealing with uncertainty.

The host asked how she handled the pressure of producing content regularly. She admitted that she sometimes felt stressed, but she tried to plan her work and learn from mistakes. I watched the interview after work, and her answer caught my attention because I was also thinking about changing my career direction and improving my English.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to how I felt after watching the interview,

● SDa: reasons

I appreciated that she did not pretend everything had been easy. Her answers sounded practical and honest.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. The interview encouraged me to take my own plans more seriously while still being realistic about the risks.

考前核实： 必须是别人采访 Zoe 的节目，不能直接把她自己的 vlog 当采访。
