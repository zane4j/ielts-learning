P01｜做成困难事情的人

Describe a person who achieved something difficult. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
关键词完整稿Cue Card改动说明通用模板

1 分钟准备 · 6 组关键词

  1. 人物 / 身份：language barrier
  2. 核心特点：afraid of speaking English
  3. 视频 / 场景：language school in the Philippines
  4. 动作 / 方法：practiced with her teachers every day
  5. 结果 / 细节：hold a full conversation
  6. 原因 / 感受：real courage

完整英文口语稿

粗体＝固定框架，浅绿＝关键词；五段换行。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who became a popular content creator after overcoming a serious language barrier. To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair. Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of the difficult challenge she faced, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares her English-learning journey and travel experiences. She used to be afraid of speaking English, but she decided to change that.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about her time at a language school in the Philippines. In the video, she said she could barely order food in English when she first arrived. She felt embarrassed, but she practiced with her teachers every day. She also pushed herself to talk to local people instead of using a translation app. After a few months, she was able to hold a full conversation and even record a video in English. I could see how proud she was.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to why I admire her achievement, I think it takes real courage to leave your comfort zone and keep practicing when you make mistakes.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.
