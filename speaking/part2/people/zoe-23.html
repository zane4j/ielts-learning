23｜想从事医疗行业的人

2605 02-4 · Describe a person you know who would like to choose a career in the medical field

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person you know who would like to choose a career in the medical field

叙述焦点： 医疗职业目标、兴趣来源、准备行动

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who hopes to become a physiotherapist in the future.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of why she wants to enter the medical field,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share her badminton training, health habits and personal experiences.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about recovering from a sports injury and becoming interested in physiotherapy.

She said that a physiotherapist had helped her recover and return to badminton. That experience made her curious about how the human body works. Although she currently creates online content, she has started learning basic health science and looking into the qualifications she would need to work in rehabilitation one day.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I think this career choice suits her,

● SDa: reasons

She is patient, hard-working and genuinely interested in helping people recover and stay active.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. I respect her decision because moving into healthcare would require serious study, but she seems ready to work towards it.

考前核实： “未来想进入医疗行业”不等于已经获得执照；只写计划和学习，不编造现有职业资质。
