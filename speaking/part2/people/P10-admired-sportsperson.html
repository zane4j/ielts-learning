Speaking Part 2 · Zoe Story · P10

P10｜钦佩的运动员

Describe a successful sportsperson you admire. 本题采用固定 Zoe 人物框架，以一个具体视频为 STORY 主体，WHAT 与 WHY 直接扣题。

完整示范稿 · 5 段结构
关键词完整稿Cue Card改动说明通用模板

1 分钟准备 · 6 组关键词

  1. 人物 / 身份：professional badminton player
  2. 核心特点：national-level tournament
  3. 视频 / 场景：lost the first game
  4. 动作 / 方法：changed her tactics
  5. 结果 / 细节：friendly and modest
  6. 原因 / 感受：determination on the court

完整英文口语稿

粗体＝固定框架，浅绿＝关键词；五段换行。

1 — INTRO

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped into my mind was Zoe, who is a professional badminton player as well as a popular sports creator on Douyin.

To tell you more about her, she's slim and of average height, with a round face and long straight hair.

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter.

2 — WHAT

Speaking of her achievements in badminton, the most impressive thing about her is that she has over one million followers on Douyin, where she shares training videos, match highlights and her life outside the court.

She won a national-level tournament after years of hard training.

3 — STORY

I still remember one time when I watched one of her videos about the final of an important badminton competition.

She shared a clip from a match where she had lost the first game. She stayed focused, changed her tactics and eventually won. In another part of the video, she showed her morning training routine and thanked her coach and teammates. Outside competition, she came across as friendly and modest. That contrast made a strong impression on me.

4 — WHY / FEELING

When it comes to why I admire her as an athlete, she combines determination on the court with a humble attitude in everyday life.

5 — ENDING

Overall, I really admire her because she's always learning new things and sharing what she knows with others.
