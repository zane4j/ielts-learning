13｜喜欢拍照的人

2609 02-6 · Describe a person you know who really likes taking photos

原文件规则：加粗英文＝Topic 改动或新增内容；非加粗英文＝固定模板核心句。页面按原稿三段式显示，不擅自改写原文。

通用模板完整手册定位原始 MD

Topic: Describe a person you know who really likes taking photos

叙述焦点： 拍照类型、习惯、为什么喜欢

1 - INTRO

● TS: intro

When I saw this topic, the first person that popped up in my mind was Zoe, a content creator who really enjoys taking photos while travelling.

● SDb: personalities

Actually, she's really ambitious and self-disciplined, a go-getter. And she seems to have a very positive attitude towards life.

2 - WHAT

● TS: what

Speaking of what kind of photos she likes taking,

● fact

The most impressive thing about her is that she has over 1 million followers on Douyin, the Chinese version of TikTok. She has a channel to share travel photography and everyday moments.

● example

One video that left the deepest impression on me was when she shared stories about taking photos of small streets, local food and beautiful landscapes during a trip.

She explained that she prefers capturing natural moments instead of asking people to pose all the time. Even when her camera had a problem, she continued taking pictures with her phone because she did not want to miss the experience. Her photos made ordinary places look interesting.

3 - WHY / HOW

● TS: why

When it comes to why I like her approach to photography,

● SDa: reasons

I think she is genuinely passionate about taking pictures because she enjoys the process, not just the final result.

● SDb: influences

To conclude, Zoe has had a real impact on me. She has encouraged me to take more photos when I travel so that I can remember those small moments later.

考前核实： 必须补充明确拍照片行为；拍视频不自动等于喜欢拍照。
