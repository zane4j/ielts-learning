# L01 嘈杂的地方

Topic: A noisy place

> 根据原稿扩写的练习稿；新增场景细节请按真实经历调整。粗体为可复用框架，其余为本题内容。正文约 246 词（按空格计数）；标题、说明不读。时长请以录音为准。

**The first place that comes to mind is** a night market in Shenzhen that I visited with a friend last month. It's easy to get to, so we thought it would be a convenient place to have dinner and try some local snacks.

**Speaking of what made it so noisy,** there were food stalls on both sides of a narrow street. **What stood out to me was** the mixture of loud music, sellers calling out to customers, and people chatting. Even standing right beside my friend, I couldn't always hear what he was saying.

**I remember one time when** we were waiting for our food on a Saturday evening. I asked my friend what he wanted to drink, but he thought I was asking whether he wanted to leave. We both laughed when we finally understood each other. At first, the noise felt exciting because everyone seemed to be enjoying themselves. After a while, though, repeating every sentence became tiring. We eventually took our food to a quieter spot nearby, where we could actually have a proper conversation.

**Looking back,** I enjoyed the food more once we had moved away from the busiest area. The experience made me realize that the atmosphere matters just as much as the food.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I like a lively market, but I don't want to shout throughout a meal.

**To conclude,** I'd go back earlier in the evening, before it gets too busy.
