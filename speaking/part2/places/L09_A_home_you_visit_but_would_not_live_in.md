# L09 喜欢拜访但不想住的家

Topic: A home you enjoy visiting but would not live in

> 根据原稿扩写的练习稿；新增场景细节请按真实经历调整。粗体为可复用框架，其余为本题内容。正文约 254 词（按空格计数）；标题、说明不读。时长请以录音为准。

**The first place that comes to mind is** a relative's house in my hometown, Shanwei. I visit it on some of my trips home, and it's a place where I always feel welcome.

**Speaking of why I enjoy visiting,** the relaxed family atmosphere means a lot to me. **The most impressive thing about it is that** there's enough space for everyone to sit together comfortably. Nobody seems to be in a hurry, which feels different from my usual routine in Shenzhen.

**I remember one time when** I visited with the intention of staying for only a short while. We sat down, shared some snacks, and started catching up on family news. One story reminded us of something funny from the past, and soon everyone was laughing and adding their own memories. I put my phone away because I didn't want to miss the conversation. Before I knew it, I'd stayed much longer than planned. There wasn't any special activity, but spending that time together made the visit feel worthwhile.

**Looking back,** what I enjoyed most was the company rather than the house itself. However, enjoying a visit doesn't mean I'd want to move in permanently.

**When it comes to how I feel about living there,** it wouldn't suit my current situation because my job and daily life are in Shenzhen. Moving would mean making major changes to both.

**To conclude,** I'd happily keep visiting for short breaks. That way, I can enjoy time with my relatives while keeping the routine that works for me.
