# L06 喜欢的高楼

Topic: A tall building you like

> 根据原稿扩写的练习稿；新增场景细节请按真实经历调整。粗体为可复用框架，其余为本题内容。正文约 256 词（按空格计数）；标题、说明不读。时长请以录音为准。

**The first building that comes to mind is** a tall office building I noticed in central Shenzhen last month. I don't actually know its name, but I remember its appearance clearly because it caught my attention straight away.

**Speaking of what I like about it,** it has a simple shape rather than lots of decoration. **The most impressive thing about it is that** its glass walls reflect the sky and nearby buildings, so it seems to change depending on where you're standing.

**I remember one time when** I was waiting outside for a friend on a sunny afternoon. I had a few minutes to spare, so I looked up instead of checking my phone. The sunlight was reflected in the glass, and the upper part of the building looked much brighter than the bottom. I walked a short distance down the street and noticed that the reflection changed completely. I took a few photos from different angles and showed them to my friend when he arrived. We ended up talking about how easy it is to miss interesting details in places we pass every day.

**Looking back,** that experience made an ordinary afternoon more memorable. Before that, I rarely paid much attention to office buildings unless I needed to go inside.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I like the way it fits into Shenzhen's modern city view, although I've only seen it from outside.

**To conclude,** I'd enjoy seeing it again in the evening to compare how it looks under different lighting.
