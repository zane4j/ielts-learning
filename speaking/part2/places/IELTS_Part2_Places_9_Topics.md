## L01

**Topic:** A noisy place（嘈杂的地方）

**When I saw this topic, the first place that popped up in my mind was** a <u>night market in Shenzhen</u> that I visited <u>with a friend last month</u>. 

**Speaking of** what made it so noisy, there were <u>food stalls on both sides</u> of a narrow street. **what stood out to me was** the mixture of <u>loud music</u>, sellers calling out to customers, and people chatting. Even standing right beside my friend, I <u>couldn't always hear</u> what he was saying. 

**Speaking of my experience there,** we were waiting for our food on a <u>Saturday evening</u>. I asked my friend what he <u>wanted to drink</u>, but he thought I was asking whether he wanted to leave. We had to <u>repeat almost everything</u> we said, We eventually took our food to <u>a quieter spot nearby</u>, where we could finally have a proper conversation.

I enjoyed the food more once we had moved away from the busiest area. The experience made me realize that the <u>atmosphere matters</u> just as much as the food.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I like a lively market, but I <u>don't want to shout</u> throughout a meal.

**To conclude,** I'd go back <u>earlier in the evening</u>, before it gets too busy. I would still <u>recommend the food</u>, just not the noise.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜night market in Shenzhen → with a friend last month
2. **WHAT**｜food stalls on both sides → loud music → couldn't always hear
3. **STORY**｜Saturday evening → wanted to drink → repeat almost everything → a quieter spot nearby
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜atmosphere matters → don't want to shout
5. **CONCLUSION**｜earlier in the evening → recommend the food

## L02

**Topic:** A natural place in a city（城市里的自然之地）

**When I saw this topic, the first place that popped up in my mind was** a <u>public park in Shenzhen</u> that I visit about <u>twice a month</u>. I usually go on my own, especially when I need some time away from work.

**Speaking of** what I like about it, the <u>peaceful atmosphere</u> is the main attraction. **the most impressive thing about it is that** there are plenty of <u>trees and shaded paths</u>, even though busy roads surround the park. 

**Speaking of my experience there,** I went there after a <u>stressful week at work</u>. At first, I kept <u>reaching for my phone</u> out of habit. Then I <u>put it in my bag</u> and started paying attention to what was around me. I walked slowly along a shaded path and found a bench where I could <u>hear birds singing</u>. When I finally got up to leave, I <u>felt much lighter</u> than I had that morning.

that simple visit helped me <u>relax more</u> than spending another afternoon scrolling on my phone.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** the park gives me a welcome <u>break from screens</u> without having to travel far.

**To conclude,** I'd like to keep visiting whenever I need some <u>fresh air</u> and a quieter afternoon. For me, it's a <u>small escape from city life</u>.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜public park in Shenzhen → twice a month
2. **WHAT**｜peaceful atmosphere → trees and shaded paths
3. **STORY**｜stressful week at work → reaching for my phone → put it in my bag → hear birds singing → felt much lighter
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜relax more → break from screens
5. **CONCLUSION**｜fresh air → small escape from city life

## L03

**Topic:** A crowded place（拥挤的地方）

**When I saw this topic, the first place that popped up in my mind was** a busy <u>night market in Shenzhen</u> that I visited with a friend on a <u>Saturday evening</u>. We wanted to try a few different snacks, and we'd expected the visit to be fairly relaxed. 

**Speaking of** how crowded it was, the walkway between the food stalls was quite narrow. **what stood out to me was** that almost every popular stall had <u>a long line</u>. There was <u>barely enough room</u> for two people to walk side by side.

**Speaking of my experience there,** we could only move forward <u>a few steps at a time</u>. The street was lined with food stalls, and the smell of grilled meat was everywhere. My friend spotted a stall ahead and walked toward it, but a group of people moved between us. I <u>couldn't see him</u> at all. I called him and suggested <u>meeting beside a sign</u> near the entrance. we decided to buy just a couple of snacks instead of trying everything we'd planned. We eventually took our food to <u>a quieter spot nearby</u>, where we could stand without being pushed around.

choosing a Saturday evening was probably our biggest mistake. The food itself was enjoyable, but we spent <u>more time waiting than eating</u>.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I understand why the market is popular, but I prefer <u>having more space</u>.

**To conclude,** I'd choose a <u>weekday evening</u> next time so I could explore <u>at my own pace</u>. 

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜night market in Shenzhen → Saturday evening
2. **WHAT**｜a long line → barely enough room
3. **STORY**｜a few steps at a time → couldn't see him → meeting beside a sign → a quieter spot nearby
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜more time waiting than eating → having more space
5. **CONCLUSION**｜weekday evening → at my own pace

**Speaking of** why I dislike it, the main problem is the <u>unpredictable traffic</u>. **what stood out to me was** how a journey that should take twenty minutes could sometimes take <u>twice as long</u>. 

**Speaking of my experience there,** we had <u>booked a court</u> for a Saturday afternoon. I left home thinking I had plenty of time, but the bus soon <u>got stuck in traffic</u>. I kept <u>checking the time</u> and sending messages to my friends. By the time I arrived, I'd already <u>missed part of our booking</u>. I was stressed before we'd even started playing.

I should have <u>allowed more time</u>, although it's frustrating to leave very early for such a short trip.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I enjoy the badminton but dislike the <u>uncertainty of getting there</u>. 

**To conclude,** I'd prefer <u>a more reliable route</u>, even if it involved a little more walking. At least then I could <u>plan my afternoon</u> properly.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜bus trip to a sports center → almost every week
2. **WHAT**｜unpredictable traffic → twice as long
3. **STORY**｜booked a court → got stuck in traffic → checking the time → missed part of our booking
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜allowed more time → uncertainty of getting there
5. **CONCLUSION**｜a more reliable route → plan my afternoon

## L07

**When I saw this topic, the first place that popped up in my mind was** a <u>shopping mall in Shenzhen</u> where I waited for a friend last month. We'd arranged to meet there before dinner, but he was <u>delayed by nearly an hour</u>. 

**Speaking of** why I found it boring, there was very little that matched my interests. **what stood out to me was** that many of the shops seemed to sell <u>similar clothes and accessories</u>. I <u>wasn't planning to buy anything</u>, so there wasn't much to keep me interested. 

**Speaking of my experience there,** I tried to make the best of the <u>extra time</u>. After a while, I realized I was passing the <u>same kinds of stores</u> again. I kept <u>checking the time</u> and was surprised that only about fifteen minutes had gone by. Eventually, I <u>found somewhere to sit</u> and started looking at my phone. When my <u>friend finally arrived</u>, I was relieved. 

the mall wasn't necessarily a bad place.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I disliked the feeling that I was <u>wasting a free afternoon</u> and had <u>nothing interesting to do</u>.

**To conclude,** next time I'd <u>bring a book</u> or arrange to meet somewhere that <u>suits my interests better</u>. I wouldn't choose that mall just to pass the time.

## L08

**Topic:** A place you would recommend visiting（推荐的旅行地点）

**When I saw this topic, the first place that popped up in my mind was** <u>Samarkand</u>, a city I visited during my trip to <u>Uzbekistan in October</u>. 

**Speaking of** why I'd recommend it, the architecture is the main attraction for me. **the most impressive thing about it is that** the historic buildings around <u>Registan Square</u> have <u>beautiful blue tiles</u> and detailed patterns. From a distance the buildings look impressive, but the details are much more interesting up close. 

**Speaking of my experience there,** I spent time <u>walking around the square</u> and taking photos. I had a few minutes to spare, so I <u>looked up</u> instead of checking my phone. I took a few <u>photos from different angles</u>, but the pictures couldn't really capture the scale of the buildings. Instead of rushing off, I <u>stayed a little longer</u> and simply enjoyed being there. 

I'm glad I didn't treat it as just another place to take a quick photo. That <u>slower pace</u> made the visit much more memorable.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I enjoyed seeing somewhere so different from the cities I'm familiar with, especially the mix of <u>history and architecture</u>.

**To conclude,** I'd recommend <u>visiting in person</u> and taking your time. It's a place where the <u>small details</u> really deserve your attention.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜Samarkand → Uzbekistan in October
2. **WHAT**｜Registan Square → beautiful blue tiles
3. **STORY**｜walking around the square → looked up → photos from different angles → stayed a little longer
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜slower pace → history and architecture
5. **CONCLUSION**｜visiting in person → small details
