## L01

**Topic:** A noisy place（嘈杂的地方）

**The first place that comes to mind is** a ***night market in Shenzhen*** that I visited ***with a friend last month***. It's easy to get to, so we thought it would be a convenient place to have dinner and try some local snacks. We'd heard good things about the food, and we were both looking forward to it.

**Speaking of what made it so noisy,** there were food stalls on both sides of a narrow street. **What stood out to me was** the mixture of ***loud music***, sellers calling out to customers, and people chatting. Even standing right beside my friend, I ***couldn't always hear*** what he was saying. It felt as if everyone was trying to speak louder than everyone else.

**I remember one time when** we were waiting for our food on a ***Saturday evening***. The street was lined with food stalls, and the smell of grilled meat was everywhere. At first, we were excited because there were so many snacks to try. I asked my friend what he ***wanted to drink***, but he thought I was asking whether he wanted to leave. We both laughed when we finally understood each other. After a while, though, it became difficult to enjoy ourselves. We had to ***repeat almost everything*** we said, and even ordering food was tiring. We eventually took our food to ***a quieter spot nearby***, where we could finally have a proper conversation.

**Looking back,** I enjoyed the food more once we had moved away from the busiest area. The experience made me realize that the ***atmosphere matters*** just as much as the food. **When it comes to how I feel about it,** I like a lively market, but I ***don't want to shout*** throughout a meal.

**To conclude,** I'd go back ***earlier in the evening***, before it gets too busy. I would still ***recommend the food***, just not the noise.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行关键词**

1. **INTRO**｜night market in Shenzhen → with a friend last month
2. **WHAT**｜loud music → couldn't always hear
3. **STORY**｜Saturday evening → wanted to drink → repeat almost everything → a quieter spot nearby
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜atmosphere matters → don't want to shout
5. **CONCLUSION**｜earlier in the evening → recommend the food

## L02

**Topic:** A natural place in a city（城市里的自然之地）

**The first place that comes to mind is** a ***public park in Shenzhen*** that I visit about ***twice a month***. It's close enough for a short weekend visit, so I don't have to plan a whole day around going there. I usually go on my own, especially when I need some time away from work.

**Speaking of what I like about it,** the ***peaceful atmosphere*** is the main attraction. **The most impressive thing about it is that** there are plenty of ***trees and shaded paths***, even though busy roads surround the park. Once I'm inside, the city feels much farther away. There are also benches where people can sit without feeling they have to buy anything.

**I remember one time when** I went there after a ***stressful week at work***. I had spent the whole week staring at a screen, so I really needed a ***change of scenery***. At first, I kept reaching for my phone out of habit. Then I ***put it in my bag*** and started paying attention to what was around me. I walked slowly along a shaded path and found a bench where I could ***hear birds singing***. I stayed there for quite a while, watching people walk their dogs and children play. I didn't do anything special, but I gradually stopped thinking about work. When I finally got up to leave, I felt much lighter than I had that morning.

**Looking back,** that simple visit helped me ***relax more*** than spending another afternoon scrolling on my phone. It was also completely free. **When it comes to how I feel about it,** the park gives me a welcome ***break from screens*** without having to travel far.

**To conclude,** I'd like to keep visiting whenever I need some ***fresh air*** and a quieter afternoon. For me, it's a ***small escape from city life***.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行关键词**

1. **INTRO**｜public park in Shenzhen → twice a month
2. **WHAT**｜peaceful atmosphere → trees and shaded paths
3. **STORY**｜stressful week at work → change of scenery → put it in my bag → hear birds singing
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜relax more → break from screens
5. **CONCLUSION**｜fresh air → small escape from city life

## L03

**Topic:** A crowded place（拥挤的地方）

**The first place that comes to mind is** a busy ***night market in Shenzhen*** that I visited with a friend on a ***Saturday evening***. We wanted to try a few different snacks, and we'd expected the visit to be fairly relaxed. It was easy to reach, which is probably one reason so many people go there.

**Speaking of how crowded it was,** the walkway between the food stalls was quite narrow. **What stood out to me was** that almost every popular stall had ***a long line***. Some people were waiting for food, while others were stopping in the middle of the walkway to take photos. There was ***barely enough room*** for two people to walk side by side.

**I remember one moment when** we could only move forward ***a few steps at a time***. The street was lined with food stalls, and the smell of grilled meat was everywhere. At first, we were excited because there were so many snacks to try. My friend spotted a stall ahead and walked toward it, but a group of people moved between us. For a few seconds, I ***couldn't see him*** at all. I called him and suggested ***meeting beside a sign*** near the entrance. After we found each other, we decided to buy just a couple of snacks instead of trying everything we'd planned. After a while, though, it became difficult to enjoy ourselves. We eventually took our food to ***a quieter spot nearby***, where we could stand without being pushed around.

**Looking back,** choosing a Saturday evening was probably our biggest mistake. The food itself was enjoyable, but we spent ***more time waiting than eating***. **When it comes to how I feel about it,** I understand why the market is popular, but I prefer ***having more space***.

**To conclude,** I'd choose a ***weekday evening*** next time so I could explore ***at my own pace***. The same market might feel completely different when it's less crowded.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行关键词**

1. **INTRO**｜night market in Shenzhen → Saturday evening
2. **WHAT**｜a long line → barely enough room
3. **STORY**｜a few steps at a time → couldn't see him → meeting beside a sign → a quieter spot nearby
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜more time waiting than eating → having more space
5. **CONCLUSION**｜weekday evening → at my own pace

## L04

**Topic:** A short journey you often make but dislike（不喜欢的短途行程）

**The first journey that comes to mind is** my regular ***bus trip to a sports center*** in central Shenzhen. I make this journey ***almost every week*** because I play badminton there with my friends. I enjoy the game, so it's a trip I have to make even though I don't particularly look forward to it.

**Speaking of why I dislike it,** the main problem is the ***unpredictable traffic***. **What stood out to me was** how a journey that should take twenty minutes could sometimes take ***twice as long***. The distance isn't really the issue; it's not knowing when I'll arrive. The bus can be crowded, and there's often nowhere comfortable to sit.

**I remember one time when** we had ***booked a court*** for a Saturday afternoon. I left home thinking I had plenty of time, but the bus soon ***got stuck in traffic***. Every time it moved a little, I thought we were finally getting somewhere, and then it stopped again. I kept ***checking the time*** and sending messages to my friends. I tried to make the best of the extra time by listening to an English podcast, but I couldn't really concentrate. My friends told me not to worry, but I still felt bad because they were waiting for me. By the time I arrived, I'd already ***missed part of our booking***. I was stressed before we'd even started playing.

**Looking back,** I should have ***allowed more time***, although it's frustrating to leave very early for such a short trip. **When it comes to how I feel about it,** I enjoy the badminton but dislike the ***uncertainty of getting there***. It's the waiting, rather than the distance, that bothers me.

**To conclude,** I'd prefer ***a more reliable route***, even if it involved a little more walking. At least then I could ***plan my afternoon*** properly.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行关键词**

1. **INTRO**｜bus trip to a sports center → almost every week
2. **WHAT**｜unpredictable traffic → twice as long
3. **STORY**｜booked a court → got stuck in traffic → checking the time → missed part of our booking
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜allowed more time → uncertainty of getting there
5. **CONCLUSION**｜a more reliable route → plan my afternoon

## L05

**Topic:** A public building you like visiting（喜欢的公共建筑）

**The first place that comes to mind is** a ***public library in Shenzhen*** that a friend recommended to me. I started visiting because I wanted somewhere quiet to read and ***study English on weekends***. It's easy to reach by public transport, and I don't have to spend money just to find a comfortable seat.

**Speaking of what I like about it,** the reading area is comfortable and well lit. **The most impressive thing about it is that** it has ***large windows*** and ***plenty of seats***, so I can concentrate without having to buy a drink or order food. The whole building feels calm, even when there are quite a few people inside.

**I remember one time when** my ***apartment was noisy*** and I couldn't focus on an English book. I had spent the whole week staring at a screen, so I really needed a ***change of scenery***. I decided to go to the library rather than stay at home. At first, I kept reaching for my phone out of habit. Then I ***put it in my bag*** and started paying attention to the book in front of me. I found a seat near a window and gave myself time to work through each page. I wrote down a few useful expressions instead of checking every unfamiliar word. After about an hour, I'd finished a section that I'd been putting off. That gave me ***a real sense of progress***.

**Looking back,** seeing other people quietly reading helped me ***stay focused***. I felt less tempted to check my phone when everyone around me was concentrating. **When it comes to how I feel about it,** the library gives me a welcome ***break from screens*** and a clear separation between studying and resting at home.

**To conclude,** I plan to visit more often to read and ***review my English notes***. It's a place where I can slow down and ***get something useful done***.

1. **INTRO**｜public library in Shenzhen → study English on weekends
2. **WHAT**｜large windows → plenty of seats
3. **STORY**｜apartment was noisy → change of scenery → put it in my bag → a real sense of progress
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜stay focused → break from screens
5. **CONCLUSION**｜review my English notes → get something useful done

## L06

**Topic:** A tall building you like（喜欢的高楼）

**The first building that comes to mind is** a ***tall office building*** I noticed in ***central Shenzhen last month***. I don't actually know its name, but I remember its appearance clearly because it caught my attention straight away. I pass through that part of the city quite often, so it surprised me that I hadn't noticed it before.

**Speaking of what I like about it,** it has a simple shape rather than lots of decoration. **The most impressive thing about it is that** its ***glass walls reflect the sky*** and nearby buildings, so it seems to change depending on where you're standing. From a distance it looks quite ordinary, but the ***details*** are much more interesting up close.

**I remember one time when** I was waiting outside for a friend on a ***sunny afternoon***. I had a few minutes to spare, so I ***looked up*** instead of checking my phone. The sunlight was reflected in the glass, and the upper part of the building looked much brighter than the bottom. I walked a short distance down the street and noticed that the reflection changed completely. I took a few ***photos from different angles*** and showed them to my friend when he arrived. Instead of rushing off, I ***stayed a little longer*** to look at the building from across the road. We ended up talking about how easy it is to miss interesting details in places we pass every day.

**Looking back,** that experience made an ***ordinary afternoon more memorable***. I realized I usually pay very little attention to the buildings around me. **When it comes to how I feel about it,** I like the way it fits into Shenzhen's ***modern city view***, although I've only seen it from outside.

**To conclude,** I'd enjoy seeing it again ***in the evening*** to compare how it looks under different lighting. I think it would be worth ***another visit***.

1. **INTRO**｜tall office building → central Shenzhen last month

3. **STORY**｜sunny afternoon → looked up → photos from different angles → stayed a little longer
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜ordinary afternoon more memorable → modern city view

## L07

**Topic:** A place you found boring（去过的无聊地方）

**The first place that comes to mind is** a ***shopping mall in Shenzhen*** where I waited for a friend last month. We'd arranged to meet there before dinner, but he was ***delayed by nearly an hour***. I went there mainly because it was a convenient meeting point, not because I wanted to go shopping.

**Speaking of why I found it boring,** there was very little that matched my interests. **What stood out to me was** that many of the shops seemed to sell ***similar clothes and accessories***. I ***wasn't planning to buy anything***, so there wasn't much to keep me interested. The building was clean and modern, but that didn't make the wait any shorter.

**I remember that afternoon clearly because** I tried to make the best of the ***extra time***. I walked around one floor, looked into a few shops, and then went upstairs hoping to find something different. After a while, I realized I was passing the ***same kinds of stores*** again. I kept ***checking the time*** and was surprised that only about fifteen minutes had gone by. I thought about buying a coffee, but there was a long line at the counter, so I gave up. Eventually, I ***found somewhere to sit*** and started looking at my phone. When my friend finally arrived, I was relieved. We left straight away and found a small restaurant nearby, which turned out to be the best part of the evening.

**Looking back,** the mall wasn't necessarily a bad place. Someone who enjoys shopping might have had a great time there. **When it comes to how I feel about it,** I disliked the feeling that I was ***wasting a free afternoon*** and had ***nothing interesting to do***.

**To conclude,** next time I'd ***bring a book*** or arrange to meet somewhere that ***suits my interests better***. I wouldn't choose that mall just to pass the time.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行关键词**

1. **INTRO**｜shopping mall in Shenzhen → delayed by nearly an hour
2. **WHAT**｜similar clothes and accessories → wasn't planning to buy anything
3. **STORY**｜extra time → same kinds of stores → checking the time → found somewhere to sit
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜wasting a free afternoon → nothing interesting to do

## L08

**Topic:** A place you would recommend visiting（推荐的旅行地点）

**The first place that comes to mind is** ***Samarkand***, a city I visited during my trip to ***Uzbekistan in October***. I'd seen pictures before going, but visiting in person left a much stronger impression on me. It was one of the highlights of the trip, and I still talk about it when friends ask me for travel ideas.

**Speaking of why I'd recommend it,** the architecture is the main attraction for me. **The most impressive thing about it is that** the historic buildings around ***Registan Square*** have ***beautiful blue tiles*** and detailed patterns. From a distance the buildings look impressive, but the details are much more interesting up close. You don't need to know much about history to appreciate what you're seeing.

**I remember one afternoon when** I spent time ***walking around the square*** and taking photos. I had a few minutes to spare, so I ***looked up*** instead of checking my phone. At first, I was mostly trying to find a good angle to fit the buildings into one picture. Then I put my phone down and started looking more closely at the patterns. I took a few ***photos from different angles***, but the pictures couldn't really capture the scale of the buildings. Instead of rushing off, I ***stayed a little longer*** and simply enjoyed being there. By the time I left, I felt I'd experienced the place rather than just collected photographs.

**Looking back,** I'm glad I didn't treat it as just another place to take a quick photo. That ***slower pace*** made the visit much more memorable. **When it comes to how I feel about it,** I enjoyed seeing somewhere so different from the cities I'm familiar with, especially the mix of ***history and architecture***.

**To conclude,** I'd recommend ***visiting in person*** and taking your time. It's a place where the ***small details*** really deserve your attention.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行关键词**

1. **INTRO**｜Samarkand → Uzbekistan in October
2. **WHAT**｜Registan Square → beautiful blue tiles
3. **STORY**｜walking around the square → looked up → photos from different angles → stayed a little longer
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜slower pace → history and architecture
5. **CONCLUSION**｜visiting in person → small details

## L09

**Topic:** A home you enjoy visiting but would not live in（喜欢拜访但不想住的家）

**The first place that comes to mind is** a relative's house in my ***hometown, Shanwei***. I visit it on some of my trips home, and it's a place where I ***always feel welcome***. I've been there many times over the years, so I know the family and the house very well.

**Speaking of why I enjoy visiting,** the ***relaxed family atmosphere*** means a lot to me. **The most impressive thing about it is that** there's enough space for everyone to ***sit together comfortably***. Nobody seems to be in a hurry, which feels different from my usual routine in Shenzhen. It's not a fancy house, but the people make it feel warm and familiar.

**I remember one time when** I visited with the intention of staying for only a short while. We sat down, shared some snacks, and started ***catching up on family news***. One story reminded us of something funny from the past, and soon everyone was laughing and adding their own memories. At first, I kept reaching for my phone out of habit. Then I ***put it in my bag*** and started paying attention to the conversation. Before I knew it, I'd stayed ***much longer than planned***. We even made tea and talked about what everyone had been doing lately. There wasn't any special activity, but spending that time together made the ***visit feel worthwhile***.

**Looking back,** what I enjoyed most was ***the company rather than the house*** itself. That doesn't mean I'd want to move in permanently, though. **When it comes to how I feel about living there,** it wouldn't suit my current situation because my ***job and daily life are in Shenzhen***. I would miss my routine and the convenience of living near work.

**To conclude,** I'd happily keep ***visiting for short breaks***. That way, I can enjoy time with my relatives while keeping the ***routine that works for me***.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行关键词**

1. **INTRO**｜hometown, Shanwei → always feel welcome
2. **WHAT**｜relaxed family atmosphere → sit together comfortably
3. **STORY**｜catching up on family news → put it in my bag → much longer than planned → visit feel worthwhile
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜the company rather than the house → job and daily life are in Shenzhen
5. **CONCLUSION**｜visiting for short breaks → routine that works for me
