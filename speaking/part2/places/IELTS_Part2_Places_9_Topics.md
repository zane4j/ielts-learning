## L01

**Topic:** A noisy place（嘈杂的地方）

**When I saw this topic, the first place that popped up in my mind was** a `night market in Shenzhen` that I visited `with a friend last month`. 

**Speaking of** what made it so noisy, there were `food stalls on both sides` of `a narrow street`. **what stood out to me was** the mixture of `loud music`, `sellers calling out to customers`, and people chatting. Even standing right beside my friend, I `couldn't always hear` what he was saying. 

**Speaking of my experience there,** we were waiting for our food on a `Saturday evening`. I asked my friend what he `wanted to drink`, but `he thought I was asking whether he wanted to leave`. We had to `repeat almost everything` we said. We eventually took our food to `a quieter spot nearby`, where we could finally `have a proper conversation`.

`I enjoyed the food more` once we had moved away from the busiest area. The experience made me realize that the `atmosphere matters` just as much as the food.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I like a lively market, but I `don't want to shout` throughout a meal.

**To conclude,** I'd go back `earlier in the evening`, before it gets too busy. I would still `recommend the food`, just not the noise.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜night market in Shenzhen → with a friend last month
2. **WHAT**｜food stalls on both sides → a narrow street → loud music → sellers calling out to customers → couldn't always hear
3. **STORY**｜Saturday evening → wanted to drink → he thought I was asking whether he wanted to leave → repeat almost everything → a quieter spot nearby → have a proper conversation
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜atmosphere matters → I enjoyed the food more → don't want to shout
5. **CONCLUSION**｜earlier in the evening → recommend the food

## L02

**Topic:** A natural place in a city（城市里的自然之地）

**When I saw this topic, the first place that popped up in my mind was** a `public park in Shenzhen` that I visit about `twice a month`. I usually `go on my own`, especially when I need some time away from work.

**Speaking of** what I like about it, the `peaceful atmosphere` is the main attraction. **the most impressive thing about it is that** there are plenty of `trees and shaded paths`, even though `busy roads surround the park`. 

**Speaking of my experience there,** I went there after a `stressful week at work`. At first, I kept `reaching for my phone` out of habit. Then I `put it in my bag` and `started paying attention` to what was around me. I walked slowly along a shaded path and `found a bench` where I could `hear birds singing`. When I finally got up to leave, I `felt much lighter` than I had that morning.

That simple visit helped me `relax more` than spending another afternoon scrolling on my phone.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** the park gives me a welcome `break from screens` without having to travel far.

**To conclude,** I'd like to keep visiting whenever I need some `fresh air` and a `quieter afternoon`. For me, it's a `small escape from city life`.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜public park in Shenzhen → twice a month → go on my own
2. **WHAT**｜peaceful atmosphere → trees and shaded paths → busy roads surround the park
3. **STORY**｜stressful week at work → reaching for my phone → put it in my bag → started paying attention → found a bench → hear birds singing → felt much lighter
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜relax more → break from screens
5. **CONCLUSION**｜fresh air → quieter afternoon → small escape from city life

## L03

**Topic:** A crowded place（拥挤的地方）

**When I saw this topic, the first place that popped up in my mind was** a busy `night market in Shenzhen` that I visited `with a friend` on a `Saturday evening`. We wanted to try a few different snacks, and we'd expected the visit to be fairly relaxed. 

**Speaking of** how crowded it was, the walkway between the food stalls was quite narrow. **what stood out to me was** that almost every popular stall had `a long line`. There was `barely enough room` for two people to `walk side by side`.

**Speaking of my experience there,** we could only move forward `a few steps at a time`. The street was lined with food stalls, and the smell of grilled meat was everywhere. My friend spotted a stall ahead and walked toward it, but `a group of people moved between us`. I `couldn't see him` at all. `I called him` and suggested `meeting beside a sign` near the entrance. We decided to `buy just a couple of snacks` instead of trying everything we'd planned. We eventually took our food to `a quieter spot nearby`, where we could stand `without being pushed around`.

Choosing a Saturday evening was probably our biggest mistake. The food itself was enjoyable, but we spent `more time waiting than eating`.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I understand why the market is popular, but I prefer `having more space`.

**To conclude,** I'd choose a `weekday evening` next time so I could explore `at my own pace`. 

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜night market in Shenzhen → with a friend → Saturday evening
2. **WHAT**｜a long line → barely enough room → walk side by side
3. **STORY**｜a few steps at a time → a group of people moved between us → couldn't see him → I called him → meeting beside a sign → buy just a couple of snacks → a quieter spot nearby → without being pushed around
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜more time waiting than eating → having more space
5. **CONCLUSION**｜weekday evening → at my own pace

## L04

**Topic:** A short journey you often make but dislike（不喜欢的短途行程）

**When I saw this topic, the first journey that popped up in my mind was** my regular `bus trip to a sports center` in central Shenzhen. I make this journey `almost every week` because I `play badminton` there with my friends. 

**Speaking of** why I dislike it, the main problem is the `unpredictable traffic`. **what stood out to me was** how a journey that should take `twenty minutes` could sometimes take `twice as long`. 

**Speaking of my experience there,** we had `booked a court` for a Saturday afternoon. I left home thinking I had `plenty of time`, but the bus soon `got stuck in traffic`. I kept `checking the time` and `sending messages to my friends`. By the time I arrived, I'd already `missed part of our booking`. I was `stressed before we'd even started playing`.

I should have `allowed more time`, although it's frustrating to leave very early for such a short trip.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I enjoy the badminton but dislike the `uncertainty of getting there`. 

**To conclude,** I'd prefer `a more reliable route`, even if it involved `a little more walking`. At least then I could `plan my afternoon` properly.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜bus trip to a sports center → almost every week → play badminton
2. **WHAT**｜unpredictable traffic → twenty minutes → twice as long
3. **STORY**｜booked a court → plenty of time → got stuck in traffic → checking the time → sending messages to my friends → missed part of our booking → stressed before we'd even started playing
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜allowed more time → uncertainty of getting there
5. **CONCLUSION**｜a more reliable route → a little more walking → plan my afternoon

## L08

**Topic:** A place you would `recommend` visiting（推荐的旅行地点）

**When I saw this topic, the first place that popped up in my mind was** `Samarkand`, a city I visited during my trip to `Uzbekistan in October`. 

**Speaking of** why I'd recommend it, the architecture is the main attraction for me. **the most impressive thing about it is that** the historic buildings around `Registan Square` have `beautiful blue tiles` and detailed patterns. From a distance `the buildings` look impressive, but the details are much more interesting up close. 

**Speaking of my experience there,** I spent time `walking around the square` and taking photos. I had a few minutes to spare, so I `looked up` instead of checking `my phone`. I took a few `photos from different angles`, but the pictures couldn't really capture the scale of the buildings. Instead of rushing off, I `stayed a little longer` and simply enjoyed being there. 

I'm glad I didn't treat it as just another place to take a quick photo. That `slower pace` made the visit much more memorable.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I enjoyed seeing somewhere so different from the cities I'm familiar with, especially the mix of `history and architecture`.

**To conclude,** I'd recommend `visiting in person` and taking your time. It's a place where the `small details` really deserve your attention.

**1 分钟准备｜5 行记忆触发词**

1. **INTRO**｜recommend → Samarkand → Uzbekistan in October
2. **WHAT**｜Registan Square → beautiful blue tiles → the buildings
3. **STORY**｜walking around the square → looked up → my phone → photos from different angles → stayed a little longer
4. **WHY / FEELINGS**｜slower pace → history and architecture
