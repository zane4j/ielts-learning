# L02 喜欢的城市自然之地

Topic: A natural place in a city

> 根据原稿扩写的练习稿；新增场景细节请按真实经历调整。粗体为可复用框架，其余为本题内容。正文约 249 词（按空格计数）；标题、说明不读。时长请以录音为准。

**The first place that comes to mind is** a public park in Shenzhen that I visit about twice a month. It's close enough for a short weekend visit, so I don't have to plan a whole day around going there.

**Speaking of what I like about it,** the peaceful atmosphere is the main attraction. **The most impressive thing about it is that** there are plenty of trees and shaded paths, even though busy roads surround the park. Once I'm inside, the city feels much farther away.

**I remember one time when** I went there after a stressful week at work. I'd spent several days looking at computer screens and dealing with problems, so I didn't feel like going to a crowded shopping mall. At first, I kept reaching for my phone out of habit. Then I put it in my bag and started paying attention to what was around me. I walked slowly along a shaded path and found a bench where I could hear birds. I didn't do anything special, but after sitting there for a while, I noticed that I wasn't thinking about work anymore.

**Looking back,** that simple visit helped me relax more than spending another afternoon scrolling on my phone. It also cost me nothing.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** the park gives me a welcome break from screens without having to travel far.

**To conclude,** I'd like to keep visiting whenever I need some fresh air and a quieter afternoon.
