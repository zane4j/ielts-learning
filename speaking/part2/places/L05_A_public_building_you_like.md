# L05 喜欢去的公共建筑

Topic: A public building you like visiting

> 根据原稿扩写的练习稿；新增场景细节请按真实经历调整。粗体为可复用框架，其余为本题内容。正文约 251 词（按空格计数）；标题、说明不读。时长请以录音为准。

**The first place that comes to mind is** a public library in Shenzhen that a friend recommended to me. I started visiting because I wanted somewhere quiet to read and study English on weekends.

**Speaking of what I like about it,** the reading area is comfortable and well lit. **The most impressive thing about it is that** it has large windows and plenty of seats, so I can concentrate without having to buy a drink or order food.

**I remember one time when** my apartment was noisy and I couldn't focus on an English book. I kept reading the same paragraph without really understanding it, so I decided to go to the library. I found a seat near a window and put my phone in my bag. At first, I still found the book difficult, but I gave myself time to work through each page. I wrote down a few useful expressions instead of checking every unfamiliar word. After about an hour, I'd finished a section that I'd been putting off at home. That gave me a real sense of progress.

**Looking back,** seeing other people quietly reading helped me stay focused. I was less tempted to pick up my phone when everyone around me was concentrating.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I find the library both useful and relaxing. It gives me a clear separation between studying and resting at home.

**To conclude,** I plan to visit more often to read and review my English notes.
