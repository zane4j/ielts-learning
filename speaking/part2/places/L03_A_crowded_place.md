# L03 拥挤的地方

Topic: A crowded place

> 根据原稿扩写的练习稿；新增场景细节请按真实经历调整。粗体为可复用框架，其余为本题内容。正文约 250 词（按空格计数）；标题、说明不读。时长请以录音为准。

**The first place that comes to mind is** a busy night market in Shenzhen that I visited with a friend on a Saturday evening. We wanted to try a few different snacks, and we'd expected the visit to be fairly relaxed.

**Speaking of how crowded it was,** the walkway between the food stalls was quite narrow. **What stood out to me was** that almost every popular stall had a long line. Some people were waiting for food, while others were stopping in the middle of the walkway to take photos.

**I remember one moment when** we could only move forward a few steps at a time. My friend spotted a stall ahead and walked toward it, but a group of people moved between us. For a few seconds, I couldn't see him at all. I called him and suggested meeting beside a sign near the entrance. After we found each other, we decided to buy just a couple of snacks instead of trying everything we'd planned. Even finding somewhere to stand and eat was difficult, so we left earlier than expected.

**Looking back,** I think choosing a Saturday evening was the main problem. The food itself was enjoyable, but we spent more time waiting and squeezing through people than actually eating.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I understand why the market is popular, but I prefer having more space.

**To conclude,** I'd choose a weekday evening next time so I could explore at my own pace.
