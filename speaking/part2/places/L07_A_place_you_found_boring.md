# L07 去过的无聊地方

Topic: A place you found boring

> 根据原稿扩写的练习稿；新增场景细节请按真实经历调整。粗体为可复用框架，其余为本题内容。正文约 251 词（按空格计数）；标题、说明不读。时长请以录音为准。

**The first place that comes to mind is** a shopping mall in Shenzhen where I waited for a friend last month. We'd arranged to meet there before dinner, but he was delayed by nearly an hour.

**Speaking of why I found it boring,** there was very little that matched my interests. **What stood out to me was** that many of the shops seemed to sell similar clothes and accessories. I wasn't planning to buy anything, so there wasn't much to keep me interested.

**I remember that afternoon clearly because** I tried to make the best of the extra time. I walked around one floor, looked into a few shops, and then went upstairs hoping to find something different. After a while, I realized I was passing the same kinds of stores again. I checked the time and was surprised that only about fifteen minutes had gone by. Eventually, I found somewhere to sit and started looking at my phone. When my friend finally arrived, I was much happier to leave and find somewhere to eat than to spend any more time exploring.

**Looking back,** the mall wasn't necessarily a bad place. Someone who enjoys shopping could have had a great time, but I had no clear reason to be there on my own.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I disliked the feeling that I was wasting a free afternoon.

**To conclude,** next time I'd bring a book or arrange to meet somewhere that suits my interests better.
