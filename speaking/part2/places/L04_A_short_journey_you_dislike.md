# L04 常走却不喜欢的短途

Topic: A short journey you often make but dislike

> 根据原稿扩写的练习稿；新增场景细节请按真实经历调整。粗体为可复用框架，其余为本题内容。正文约 251 词（按空格计数）；标题、说明不读。时长请以录音为准。

**The first journey that comes to mind is** my regular bus trip to a sports center in central Shenzhen. I make this journey almost every week because I play badminton there with my friends.

**Speaking of why I dislike it,** the main problem is the unpredictable traffic. **What stood out to me was** how a journey that should take twenty minutes could sometimes take twice as long. The distance isn't really the issue; it's not knowing when I'll arrive.

**I remember one time when** we had booked a court for a Saturday afternoon. I left home thinking I had plenty of time, but the bus soon got stuck in traffic. Every time it moved a little, I thought we were finally getting somewhere, and then it stopped again. I kept checking the time and sending messages to my friends. They told me not to worry, but I still felt bad because they were waiting for me. By the time I arrived, I'd already missed part of our booking, and I felt stressed before we'd even started playing.

**Looking back,** I should have allowed more time, although it's frustrating to leave very early for such a short trip. These days, I sometimes listen to an English podcast on the bus to make the waiting more useful.

**When it comes to how I feel about it,** I enjoy the badminton but dislike the uncertainty of getting there.

**To conclude,** I'd prefer a more reliable route, even if it involved a little more walking.
