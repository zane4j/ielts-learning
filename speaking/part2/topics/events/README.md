# 事件类｜17 个 Topic 与 Cue Card

[返回首页](../../../../README.md) · [分类总览](../README.md)

> 仅保留此前整理的中国大陆基线题。以下为根据原手册题意与扣题要点整理的英文练习卡，非逐字官方题卡；本次未重新核验题库。全部题卡在本页直接展示。

## E01｜结果满意的重要决定

Describe an important decision you made that had a good result.

You should say:

- What the decision was
- Why you made it
- Whether it was difficult to make

And explain why you were pleased with the result.

---

## E02｜看过的名人采访

Describe a time you watched a famous person being interviewed.

You should say:

- Who was interviewed
- When and where you watched it
- What the interview was about

And explain how you felt about it.

---

## E03｜攒钱买特别物品

Describe a time you saved money to buy something special.

You should say:

- What you wanted to buy
- Why you wanted it
- How you saved the money

And explain how you felt after buying it.

---

## E04｜小时候学会的技能

Describe a skill you learned as a child.

You should say:

- What the skill was
- How old you were
- Who taught you and how you learned

And explain how you felt about learning it.

---

## E05｜好的购物服务

Describe a time you received good service in a shop.

You should say:

- Where the shop was
- When you went there
- What service you received

And explain why you thought the service was good.

---

## E06｜与朋友度过的愉快夜晚

Describe an enjoyable evening you spent with friends.

You should say:

- When it was
- Who you were with
- What you did

And explain why you enjoyed the evening.

---

## E07｜经常做却浪费时间的活动

Describe an activity you often do that wastes your time.

You should say:

- What the activity is
- How often you do it
- Why you think it wastes time

And explain how you feel about this habit.

---

## E08｜特别场合的聚会

Describe a party you attended for a special occasion.

You should say:

- When and where it was
- What occasion it celebrated
- Who attended and roughly how many people were there

And explain why you remember this party.

---

## E09｜帮助别人的一次经历

Describe a time you helped someone.

You should say:

- Who you helped
- Why they needed help
- What you did

And explain how you felt about the result.

---

## E10｜一次早起经历

Describe a time you got up early.

You should say:

- When it happened
- Why you got up early
- What you did that morning

And explain how you felt about getting up early.

---

## E11｜最近改变的计划

Describe a plan you changed recently.

You should say:

- What your original plan was
- When you changed it
- What caused the change and what your new plan was

And explain how you felt about changing it.

---

## E12｜团队合作经历

Describe a time you worked in a team.

You should say:

- What you worked on
- Who you worked with
- What difficulties you faced

And explain why working together was useful.

---

## E13｜做过的重要决定

Describe an important decision you made.

You should say:

- What the decision was
- How you made it
- What happened as a result

And explain why it was important.

---

## E14｜遇到的技术难题

Describe a technological problem you experienced.

You should say:

- When and where it happened
- What the problem was
- Why it was difficult

And explain how you dealt with it.

---

## E15｜很久没收到回复的消息

Describe a message you sent that took a long time to receive a reply to.

You should say:

- Who you sent it to
- What the message was about
- How long you waited and what you did while waiting

And explain how you felt about the delay.

---

## E16｜改变重要观点

Describe a time you changed an important opinion.

You should say:

- What your original opinion was
- When you changed it
- What caused you to change your mind

And explain how you feel about your new opinion.

---

## E17｜最近做出的改变

Describe a change you made recently.

You should say:

- What the change was
- Why you made it
- How you put it into practice

And explain how you feel about the change.
