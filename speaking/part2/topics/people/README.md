# 人物类｜16 个 Topic 与 Cue Card

[返回首页](../../../../README.md) · [分类总览](../README.md) · [Zoe 人物 24 题完整稿](../../people/README.md)

> 仅保留此前整理的中国大陆基线题。以下为根据原手册题意与扣题要点整理的英文练习卡，非逐字官方题卡；本次未重新核验题库。全部题卡在本页直接展示。

## P01｜做成困难事情的人

Describe a person who achieved something difficult.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- What difficult thing they achieved
- How they succeeded

And explain why you admire this achievement.

---

## P02｜快乐的人

Describe a happy person you know.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- How you know them
- What they do that shows they are happy

And explain why you think they are happy.

---

## P03｜有条理的人

Describe a person you know who is very organized.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- When you have seen them being organized
- How they organize things

And explain why being organized is important to them.

---

## P04｜爱拍照的人

Describe a person you know who enjoys taking photos.

You should say:

- Who this person is and how you know them
- Where they take photos
- What they like to photograph

And explain how you feel about their interest.

---

## P05｜敬佩的年长者

Describe an older person you admire.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- How you got to know them
- What they are like

And explain why you admire them.

---

## P06｜擅长手工的人

Describe a person who is good at making things by hand.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- What they make
- How they make these things

And explain why you think they are good at it.

---

## P07｜喜欢学历史的人

Describe a person who enjoys learning about history.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- How they learn about history
- What interests them about history

And explain how you feel about their interest.

---

## P08｜教你新技能的人

Describe a person who taught you a new skill.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- What skill they taught you
- How they taught you and how you practiced

And explain what you gained from learning this skill.

---

## P09｜受欢迎的人

Describe a popular person you know.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- How often you see them or their work
- What their personality is like

And explain why they are popular.

---

## P10｜钦佩的运动员

Describe a successful sportsperson you admire.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- What sport they play and what they have achieved
- What they are like outside their sport

And explain why you admire them.

---

## P11｜有健康习惯与作息的人

Describe a person who has healthy habits and a healthy daily routine.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- What healthy habits they have
- How they maintain their routine

And explain why you think these habits are beneficial.

---

## P12｜喜欢在家种植物的人

Describe a person who enjoys growing plants at home or in a garden.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- What they grow and where
- How they care for their plants

And explain why they enjoy growing plants.

---

## P13｜童年好友

Describe a friend from your childhood.

You should say:

- Who this friend is
- How you met
- What you used to do together

And explain what your relationship is like now.

---

## P14｜想从事医疗行业的人

Describe a person who would like to work in the medical field.

You should say:

- Who this person is
- What medical career they would like to pursue
- How they are preparing for it

And explain why this career interests them.

---

## P15｜有成功生意的人

Describe a person who has a successful business.

You should say:

- Who this person is and how you know them
- What their business does
- Why they started it

And explain why you consider the business successful.

---

## P16｜擅长学语言的人

Describe a person who is good at learning languages.

You should say:

- Who this person is and how you know them
- What languages they know
- How they learn languages

And explain how you feel about their ability.
