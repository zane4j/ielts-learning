# 地点类｜9 个 Topic 与 Cue Card

[返回首页](../../../../README.md) · [分类总览](../README.md)

> 仅保留此前整理的中国大陆基线题。以下为根据原手册题意与扣题要点整理的英文练习卡，非逐字官方题卡；本次未重新核验题库。全部题卡在本页直接展示。

## L01｜嘈杂的地方

Describe a noisy place you have been to.

You should say:

- Where it is
- When you went there
- What you did there

And explain what made it noisy.

---

## L02｜喜欢的城市自然之地

Describe a natural place in a city that you enjoy visiting.

You should say:

- Where it is
- What it is like
- How often you go there and who you go with

And explain why you enjoy visiting it.

---

## L03｜拥挤的地方

Describe a crowded place you have been to.

You should say:

- Where it was and when you went
- What you did there
- How crowded it was

And explain how you felt about being there.

---

## L04｜常走却不喜欢的短途

Describe a short journey you often make but dislike.

You should say:

- Where you travel to
- How often you make this journey
- Why you make it

And explain why you dislike it.

---

## L05｜喜欢去的公共建筑

Describe a public building you enjoy visiting.

You should say:

- Where it is
- How you learned about it
- What it looks like

And explain why you enjoy visiting it.

---

## L06｜喜欢或不喜欢的高楼

Describe a tall building you like or dislike.

You should say:

- Where it is
- What it is used for
- What it looks like

And explain why you like or dislike it.

---

## L07｜去过的无聊地方

Describe a place you visited that you found boring.

You should say:

- Where it was and when you visited
- Why you went there
- What you did there

And explain why you found it boring.

---

## L08｜推荐的旅行地

Describe a place you have visited that you would recommend to travelers.

You should say:

- Where it is
- When you visited it
- What you saw and did there

And explain why you would recommend it.

---

## L09｜喜欢拜访但不想住的家

Describe a home you enjoy visiting but would not want to live in.

You should say:

- Whose home it is and where it is
- What it is like
- Why you enjoy visiting

And explain why you would not want to live there.
