# 物品与抽象事物类｜12 个 Topic 与 Cue Card

[返回首页](../../../../README.md) · [分类总览](../README.md)

> 仅保留此前整理的中国大陆基线题。以下为根据原手册题意与扣题要点整理的英文练习卡，非逐字官方题卡；本次未重新核验题库。全部题卡在本页直接展示。

## O01｜不喜欢的电影

Describe a film you disliked.

You should say:

- What the film was
- When and where you watched it
- What it was about

And explain why you disliked it.

---

## O02｜读起来开心／精彩的书

Describe a book you enjoyed reading or found exciting.

You should say:

- What the book was
- When you read it
- What it was about

And explain why it made you happy or why you found it exciting.

---

## O03｜有趣的视频

Describe an interesting video you watched.

You should say:

- When and where you watched it
- What it was about
- Why you chose to watch it

And explain why you found it interesting.

---

## O04｜想推行的新法律

Describe a new law you would like to introduce.

You should say:

- What the law would be
- Who it would affect
- Why it is needed

And explain whether you think people would accept it.

---

## O05｜喜欢的现场体育赛事

Describe a live sporting event you enjoyed attending.

You should say:

- What the event was
- When and where it took place
- Who you went with

And explain why you enjoyed it.

---

## O06｜特殊场合的食物

Describe a food people eat on special occasions.

You should say:

- What the food is
- When people eat it
- How it is made

And explain why it is important on these occasions.

---

## O07｜有名人出演的广告

Describe an advertisement featuring a famous person.

You should say:

- Who appeared in it
- Where you saw it
- What it advertised

And explain how you felt about it.

---

## O08｜有动物的故事或书

Describe a story or book involving animals.

You should say:

- What the story or book is
- What animals appear in it
- What happens and why you read it

And explain what you thought of it.

---

## O09｜现有环保法律

Describe an existing law that helps protect the environment.

You should say:

- What the law is
- How you learned about it
- Who or what benefits from it

And explain how you feel about it.

---

## O10｜长期目标／抱负

Describe a long-term goal or ambition you have.

You should say:

- What it is
- How long you have had it
- How you plan to achieve it

And explain why it matters to you.

---

## O11｜希望推行的环保法律

Describe an environmental law you would like to introduce.

You should say:

- What the law would require
- Why people should follow it
- Whether it would be popular

And explain how you feel about introducing it.

---

## O12｜人们关心的本地新闻

Describe a piece of local news that people are interested in.

You should say:

- What the news is
- How you learned about it
- Who it concerns

And explain why people are interested in it.
